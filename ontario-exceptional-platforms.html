The Exceptional Platforms — Ontario 2026

The best of the best, from the Ontario Platform Baseline (corpus of August 10–11, 2026, METHODOLOGY v2.1, full-field completion of 2026-08-11 — every candidate with captured content individually read; Toronto re-scored fresh under the same instrument this edition). Praise only, by standing rule: no rankings, no scores, no negative naming, anywhere, ever. Alphabetical within region.

What this list is: we read every published municipal platform in Ontario — 2.36 million words of assessable candidate text, all 4,743 researched candidates individually dispositioned — and scored each under a public, viewpoint-neutral instrument. The 75 candidates below cleared three gates: the instrument's top band; an independent second reading confirming a current, forward-looking platform; and this page's own test — a verbatim policy commitment, quoted below from each candidate's own platform: a specific future action with a number, mechanism, or deadline attached. Being here means one thing only — this candidate wrote down concrete commitments a voter can read and later check. It is not an endorsement, not a viability judgment, and not agreement with any position: a fully specified plan to build a thing and a fully specified plan to remove it qualify identically. Absence from this list is never criticism — many strong candidates campaign without websites, several publish records of delivery rather than forward pledges, and nominations don't close until August 21. Publish concrete commitments and tell us — we re-measure gladly, and this page will grow.

How this list was made

The full method, aggregate results and reliability statistics live on the baseline page. In brief: 80 platforms across the province — Toronto included, re-scored fresh, and every word-count-gated candidate individually re-read in the 2026-08-11 full-field completion pass — reached the instrument's top band under the v2.1 commitment-gated rules. Every one was independently re-read before any naming; then this page applied its strictest gate — each candidate's platform had to yield a quotable, verbatim forward commitment (a pledged action with a number, mechanism or deadline; hedged "could/might" language and default-of-the-job pledges don't qualify). The 75 above passed all three gates. The rest of the top band stays in the aggregate count, un-named — absence here is not criticism, and a re-read after any update is a message away. Platform links go to each candidate's own site, exactly as published; quotes are verbatim. External links are the candidates' own responsibility.

Found a mistake?

If a link is wrong, a quote reads out of context, or your platform is missing or has grown — the corrections page takes thirty seconds. Every correction is published, dated, and left visible; every re-measure request is honored.

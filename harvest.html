The Harvest

The Harvest — one record for one Toronto. One form, three lines: a vision (“Toronto in 2030 should ___”), a complaint (what’s broken — cross-streets optional, never an address), and a nomination (a neighbourhood superhero). From dinners, services, block circles, classrooms, clinics, assemblies — and from you.

The online form isn’t open yet. The paper is the record — nothing is lost. Every kit’s harvest sheet works today: run your gathering, keep the sheets, and when this form opens they come in first through the door. Nothing is collected here until the record’s independent data custodian is in place — that’s a promise about how this is built, not a delay excuse.

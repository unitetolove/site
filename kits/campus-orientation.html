The Orientation Table — a campus kit

📄 Print this kit (PDF, letter, black-and-white-safe)

One table, one week, one project your club actually runs. This is the table sheet, the thirty-second door script, the share text, and the menu of projects a club can adopt. Nothing else.

Kids welcome. Food shared. Nobody pays. (This kit’s whole family of gatherings runs on that rule — no cost to run any of it, food or not, whatever your campus allows.)

A note to the club exec

This is your club, your table, your name on everything. Nothing behind this kit shows up to run your event, joins your club, collects your members’ names, or asks your student union to approve anything. Take what’s below, change whatever doesn’t fit your campus, and run it during orientation week if you can — it’s built to be ready the week you print it.

Your campus has its own rules for tabling, postering, and outside materials. Follow them; this page asks nothing of your institution beyond its own process. Where your union requires a club to declare an external affiliation — you have none. This is a free open kit. You are not affiliating with anyone by printing it.

The one thing worth knowing before you decide: every conversation your club runs this fall joins a citywide count — a record of what Toronto’s students said they want the city to become, counted in aggregate, alongside every other club, classroom, block circle and dinner table running the same harvest. Your club’s room becomes primary-source civic material. That is the whole offer.

The thirty-second door script — orientation week

This is the raw, spoken register — written to be said, not handed over. Say it in your own words; these are the beats. Softer registers are legitimate too — the beats are what carries, not the profanity.

Say it to one person, not to a crowd:

There are no more jobs — not the way you were promised. And you’ve been told bullshit your whole life about how this works.

But there is more real work to be done than there has ever been, and it is your future being built right now, by people who aren’t asking you.

So: stop scrolling. Stop pretending the degree alone is teaching you what you need. Start organizing. Direct your own learning — you have the best ideas in the world in your pocket if you go get them.

Then let’s demand the adults and the rich sit down with us and talk about the future we all want, and how we get there.

The plan is public. The mistakes are findable. Break something of ours and show us.

September, or wait a year.

Then the ask — one rung, never five: “Take a sheet. Run one thing with your club this term. It’s your name on it, not ours.”

Never say: “leaders of tomorrow.” They are operators of today.

What you hand them: the table sheet (below, one page), and nothing else. No clipboard, no sign-up list, no QR that asks for their name. One link, printed once: uniteTOlove.ca

The table sheet — one page, print as many as you like

Print-ready PDF: the print link at the top of this page.

TORONTO IS WRITING ITS OWN PLAN. IN THE OPEN. WITH RECEIPTS.

And the plan has a hole shaped exactly like what your generation knows.

Here is the honest version: the world is broken, we are all professionals at describing it, and nobody is coming. Global unity sounds impossible — but building and strengthening our own neighbourhoods is easy. The answer has never been left or right. The answer is down, then up.

Toronto already holds all the money, permission, ideas, tools and talent it needs to decide the future it wants. What’s missing is the conversation, and the conversation is missing you.

What a club can do this term: pick one project from the menu overleaf. Run it in your club’s name, on your campus, on your schedule. When it’s done, the room’s ideas go into the citywide record — as a count and a set of lines, never a roster, never a name.

What we will never ask you for: your members’ names, your mailing list, your union’s endorsement, your money, or your permission to speak for you.

Check us: the plan is public, the mistakes are findable, and the fastest way into this is to break something of ours and show us. Bring it. → uniteTOlove.ca

The club-adoption menu — one project per club

One club, one project, one term. Pick the row that already looks like what your club does — the point is not to become a civics club, it’s to add one civic beat to the thing you already run.

Your club is… Your one project The kit that runs it About
Debate / model UN / mooting Run one public debate on a real city question, open to non-members Debate in a Box 2 hrs, one room
Student journalism / campus paper Co-publish the city’s own open data under your masthead — the ward numbers, the turnout, the candidate record published artifacts only — see Open data your editorial call, always
Urban studies / planning / policy / geography Run a Citizens’ Assembly in a Box on one neighbourhood question Citizens’ Assembly in a Box a half-day, done properly
Cultural / diaspora / language association Host a Vision Dinner in your community’s own language — the meal you already hold, plus twenty minutes Vision Dinner one dinner
Faith / chaplaincy / spiritual life Three questions after the service, in your own tradition’s language of dignity The After-Service Three Questions ten minutes
Volunteering / service / community outreach Run a Complaint Clinic — help people log what’s broken and route it to the right door Complaint Clinic one afternoon, repeatable
Intergenerational / gerontology / health / nursing A story-collection evening: students interview elders, in pairs A Story-Collection Evening 90 min
Residence council / floor reps / commuter association Start a block circle where you actually live — ten doors, one hour Found a Block Circle one evening, then monthly
Education / teacher candidates / tutoring Run the Futures Hour in a placement classroom — with the teacher, under the school’s own rules The Futures Hour one class period
Sport / intramural / run crew / outdoors Add one civic beat to your season — end one practice with ten minutes of the three questions Found a Block Circle’s harvest, standalone ten minutes
Engineering / CS / hackerspace / design Build one thing the city needs and give it away — the plan is public, the gaps are listed see the library one term
Environment / sustainability / gardening Adopt a quest scoreboard: Cleanest, or Most Beautiful quest scoreboards, live from ignition week your own count
Anything else Run the conversation your club would actually enjoy, and harvest it any kit in the kits your call

The six citywide quests, if your club wants a scoreboard instead of a room: Happiest · Healthiest · Safest · Cleanest · Friendliest · Most Beautiful. Six scoreboards, all public, all counted in aggregate. Adopt one; score it in your club’s name.

The harvest — what leaves the room

Every kit ends the same way. Each person, one line each:

  • VISION — “Toronto in 2030 should ___”
  • COMPLAINT — “What’s broken?” (cross-streets optional)
  • NOMINATION — a neighbourhood superhero: someone doing more than anyone notices

One link, printed once on the sheet: uniteTOlove.ca/harvest

If the online form isn’t live yet, the paper is the record — nothing is lost.

Only the aggregate ever travels: how many people were in the room, how many lines collected. Not names, not who said what, not a roster, not an attendance list. Your club’s membership list is yours and is never asked for.

One club room of ten people = one conversation in the citywide count.

The campus-channel share text

Three lengths. Post them under your club’s own name, in your club’s own channels. Nothing here is sent by anyone but you.

📄 Grab the share image — square, ready to post alongside any of the three below.

Short — group chat / Discord / Slack, one line:

Toronto is writing its own plan in the open this fall and our club is running one conversation for it. One room, one hour, our name on it. Free kit: uniteTOlove.ca

Medium — Instagram caption / club newsletter blurb:

The city is asking what we want Toronto to be in 2030 — and for once the asking is public, the plan is public, and the mistakes are findable.

We’re running one conversation this term. One room, one hour, our club’s name on it, and what comes out of it goes into a citywide record — as a count and a set of ideas, never a list of names.

No party, no donations, no sign-up. Free open kits: uniteTOlove.ca

Long — campus paper / union listserv / society announcement:

There is more real work to be done than there has ever been, and it is our future being built right now — mostly by people who never asked us.

This fall Toronto is running something unusual: ten thousand small conversations, in rooms people already meet in, all asking the same three things — what should this city be by 2030, what’s broken where you live, and who quietly holds your block together. Every answer joins one public record. Everything is counted in aggregate; no names travel, ever.

Our club is running one of those conversations this term, in our own name, on our own schedule, with a free open kit anyone can copy and change. If your club wants to run one too, the kits are free and there is no one to ask permission from.

The plan is public. The mistakes are findable. Come break something of ours and show us. → uniteTOlove.ca

Translate this kit

A one-page translation is itself a civic act. Priority languages, per the multilingual program (pending census confirmation): Cantonese, Mandarin, Punjabi, Urdu, Tagalog, Spanish, Tamil, Italian, Portuguese, Korean, Vietnamese, Arabic, Farsi, Bengali. A club that runs its conversation entirely in its members’ home language is first-class, not a fallback — a bilingual room is worth more, not less. Send translations through the same link: uniteTOlove.ca/harvest.

Accessibility

Print at any size your campus printer supports; the sheet is type-first, black on white, and survives a bad photocopier. Read it aloud where reading it is a barrier — the door script exists because the spoken version is the real one. Any room you run should name its own access facts honestly (stairs, washrooms, quiet space) in your own invitation; this kit does not know your building.

A free, open kit. Copy it, change it, translate it, make it yours. — uniteTOlove.ca

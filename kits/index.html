The Kits

Ten free, open kits for running your own conversation — a dinner, a block circle, a classroom hour, a folding-table clinic, an all-candidates assembly, and more. Nothing behind any kit convenes it, attends it, or shows up to run it: pick one up, change whatever doesn't fit your room, and run it in your own name. Every kit ends in the same harvest — a vision, a complaint, a nomination — which you can read about at the Harvest.

    A 40-minute dinner-table script for four to eight people — three questions, one shared harvest.

  • The After-Service Three Questions

    Students (or any younger group) interview elders in pairs — the interview guide, the consent, and the harvest.

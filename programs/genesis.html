GENESIS — The Village

A neighbourhood-sized layer of support built around every birth, sized to units of roughly 120 to 300 households.

It offers matched postpartum circles by due date and neighbourhood, a trained volunteer-plus-professional support layer, automatic meal trains and practical help, an unbiased birth-choices navigator, and an optional family-formation record the family fully owns and controls. It is designed to complement, not duplicate, existing midwifery and public-health services.

This program has not been ratified. Nothing here is a promise, and no real family has been contacted about any part of it.

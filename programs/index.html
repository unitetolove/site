The Programs

These are longer-range civic design programs — each one asking a single hard question about the city and working through what a real answer would take. None of them are organizations to join. Every one is paper design work, at an early stage, going through the same walk toward ratification and verification as everything else on this site. What follows is a one-page plain-language summary of each, drawn only from that program’s own public summary document, with its real status shown honestly.

A program page here is not a promise anything will be built. It is a description of a proposal, and how far along the proposal is.

GENESIS — The Village

A neighbourhood-sized layer of support built around every birth.

EDUCATION — The Learning City

Learning built on doing, not just being taught.

NEWCOMERS — The Diaspora Bridge Network

Linking Toronto’s diaspora communities into one lightweight network.

ELDERS — Talk to an Elder

Connecting elders and young people across the world for mentoring.

VITALITY — The Health-Data Commons

A shared, resident-owned system for health data.

CONNECTION — The Connection Stack

Rebuilding everyday community connection in a big city.

SPORT — The 158-Neighbourhood Games

A sport system for elite athletes and lifelong practice, together.

MCI — The Enough Line

One trusted measure of what “enough” actually costs in this city.

HOMES — Village Unlock

Safely connecting underused housing with people who need it.

TORONTOOS — TorontoOS

One shared, trustworthy technology platform for the city.

AGORA — The Toronto Deliberation Stack

Changing how the city listens to and deliberates with residents.

LIVELIHOOD — The Parallel Economy

A small, voluntary, neighbourhood-scale economy alongside the existing one.

HARVEST

Toronto’s food system, studied honestly and redesigned.

REGENERATION — The Living City

Making Toronto’s ravines, water, and climate defenses measurably healthier.

DR3 — Disaster Response 3.0

Remote-capable volunteer disaster response, for Toronto and beyond.

ABUNDANCE — The People’s Endowment

Putting money the city already wastes or locks away to work for everyone.

AUTOMATA — The Toronto Robot Fair

Helping the city talk about automation honestly, before it’s decided elsewhere.

MUSE

Chartered 2026-07-24, in early drafting. No public summary yet.

HEALING

Chartered 2026-07-24, in early drafting. No public summary yet.

This is the complete list of programs shown on this site. Every program page above is version 0.1 (ABUNDANCE’s Web3 sub-topic explainer is version 1.0), drafted, pre-ratification — none has an approved charter yet, and none has contacted a real person, household, or organization about anything described. DR3 is additionally blocked pending its own program lead’s review before any further work proceeds. Nothing here is an offer to join; read who is behind this for why that framing matters to us.

CONNECTION — The Connection Stack

A plan to rebuild everyday community connection in a big city through one unified system with four layers.

It includes a bulletin-board-style listing of local happenings; small recurring circles of neighbours formed around major life changes; a network unlocking underused community rooms for gatherings; and a later, more careful layer using structured formats to build understanding across faith and conflict lines. It also includes a flagship “24/7 Global Dance Party” concept meant to demonstrate that citywide joy and connection can scale, starting with a small pilot across roughly two dozen cities.

This program has not been ratified. Nothing here is a promise, and no real neighbour has been contacted about any part of it.

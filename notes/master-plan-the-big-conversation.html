§1. “Candidates we support” — the architecture that survives the firewall

STATUS: RATIFIED (operator, 2026-08-05, verbatim “approved, go” — banked OPERATOR_LOG). The adoption model below is law for G4; D-mint owed at the next Founder sitting.

Your goal, held against the constitution (commons endorses no one, serves all identically; a recorded standing decision; nobody elected us): the compliant, stronger form is adoption, not endorsement:

  1. The crowd’s plan is published with the same receipts discipline as everything else.
  2. Every candidate in every race receives it identically, with one equal question: “Will you commit to implementing the people’s plan — and which parts?” (This is Answer Day, aimed at the plan.)
  3. Adoption is public, candidate-by-candidate, in their own words — never a ranking, never an endorsement from us. The “suite of candidates” is self-assembling: whoever adopts, is the suite. The crowd supports the plan; candidates earn the crowd by adopting it.
  4. Any activity beyond publishing the record (posters naming adopters, “vote for adopters” messaging) is a different legal animal — that’s the ⚖️ MEA third-party question, which your note routes to the counsel package. Consequence of this mission: the counsel package should fire within ~2 weeks, not “eventually.” Until counsel clears it, we publish records; we do not campaign.
  5. Added to the counsel-package question set 2026-08-06 (S2 sitting), for the S4 governance sitting to carry: under the MEA and related law, what are the boundaries on candidates coordinating among themselves (mutual withdrawal, cross-endorsement), and separately on a third party discussing, describing, facilitating, or reporting on such coordination — where does neutral reporting of candidate conduct end and participation begin? Asked to know where the line is, not because anything is planned near it: the standing law is unchanged and absolute — the commons endorses no one, convenes nothing, and publishes records only.

This is also strictly more powerful than endorsement: an endorsement is our opinion; an adoption record is their commitment, on the record, enforceable at the ballot box and reviewable all term.

§2. The honest math (internal, so the targets stay credible)

  • 10,000 conversations ≈ 110/day for 90 days. Connector visits supply ~150 total — irrelevant at this scale. The engine is: kits × existing gatherings × replication. Toronto already HOLDS thousands of gatherings weekly (congregations alone ≈ thousands; add teams, meetups, classes, dinners). We are not creating 10,000 events; we are converting a fraction of existing ones into harvesting conversations + seeding new circles. Conversion needs: kits everywhere (P0), the counter visible, the harvest trivially easy (one QR → one form), and org adoption (G5 drives G1).
  • Tens of thousands co-authoring = signatures + vision submissions + room harvests + Polis participants. Festival-sized crowd (canon) ≈ 20–50k: consistent.
  • 1M+ discussing = reach, not participation: media cycles (the X-plays), embeds, shares, diaspora channels. 2M = stretch. Never published as a claim — published as an invitation (“help us get to…”).
  • 1,000 orgs = registry holds ~1,016 verified rows TODAY. The ask ladder starts at a pin (zero-cost). This is the most achievable number on the board — and the one that makes G1 arithmetic work (1,000 orgs × 10 conversations each = G1 done).

§5. The coalition ladder (G5) — how 1,000 orgs happens

PIN (map presence, zero-cost — the S1 play, starts with the 108 anchors) → HOST (one kit conversation in their room, their name) → QUEST (adopt one of the Six for the 6ix with a scoreboard) → SPRINT COVENANT (the year-one commitment: “we will point at the same goals at the same time” — the narrative’s own What-If, made signable). Each rung is one ask, one receipt; orgs climb at their own pace; the count of each rung publishes aggregate. The covenant text is an L5 writing-desk deliverable (rides the one-year sprint scenario — they’re the same document seen from two sides).

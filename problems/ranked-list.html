The entanglement map (judge portfolios with this, rank with the tiers)

Five load-bearing chains, every link a receipted register entry:

  1. The price chain: X09 trade war → food/goods prices (§1: the 2026 food report names the trade dispute as a driver) → X14 squeeze → X06 deep poverty → H2 food banks (4.1M visits) → C2 shelter pressure. A tariff decision in Washington ends in a Toronto shelter queue.
  2. The housing chain: C1 supply failure (632 completions vs 65k target) → C5 rent churn → X06/E4 → C2 homelessness → M budgets (C3) → less capital for E2/F4 climate work. The 47:1 approvals-to-completions ratio is the chain's choke point.
  3. The fiscal chain: C3 is load-bearing under every M-lever row — E2 adaptation ($4.1B/yr provincial exposure), H10 water ($9.28B renewal), D3 parks backlog, A1 the largest discretionary line. One structural revenue gap rations all of them simultaneously.
  4. The work chain: X01 AI exposure (57.4%) + X16 driving automation (91k+ counted drivers) + X09 manufacturing losses (119,200 jobs at risk) all land on the same population within the same decade → X03 precarity → X06/X14. Three separate literatures, one labour market.
  5. The trust chain: X05 education decay + X02 isolation (13% often-lonely) + H8 participation gap + X08 provincial override risk → weakened civic capacity to respond to chains 1–4. The metacrisis version: the problem-solving system is itself a problem row.

Portfolio implications: X10 resilience and C3 fiscal capacity punch above their tier ranks — each de-risks multiple chains at once; a Tier-3 values ruling that ignores chain 1 treats poverty as separable from the trade war when the receipts say otherwise; and any Solution Suite portfolio should cover at least one link in every chain rather than stacking on one.

Source tags like (homes HM12) mark questions carried from the project's own question banks (QUESTION_HARVEST.csv); untagged questions were raised in the 2026-08-11 rewrite.

Ratification record (2026-08-11): Tiers 1–2 confirmed as the working order · Tier 3 placed by an explicit values call · the fifteen beyond-taxonomy problems added to the issue map · the research roadmap re-ordered to this rank. Full internal record: the project's decision log.

How to read this page. Scores are disclosed judgment calls anchored to the receipts on each problem's register entry — ⚠️ marks a score resting on a receipt that has not been independently verified yet. Method: six 0–5 axes (SCALE · SEVERITY · TREND · EQUITY · LEVER · URGENCY) scored from four perspectives; a problem that stays high across all four rises, and one that moves with the lens goes to human judgment — never silently averaged. Disagreeing with the order is part of the point: tell us where the ranking is wrong.

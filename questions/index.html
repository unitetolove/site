Candidate Questions

A set of questions for this city’s candidates — any office, any candidate, any persuasion. Every question presupposes no position: it asks what a candidate would do, how they would decide, and how residents would check. Where a question carries a figure, that figure is sourced and cited — candidates are welcome to dispute any of them; the source trail is public. These questions never rank, score, or endorse anyone, and every one of them works identically for every candidate for the office named.

The full bank below covers all twelve of the city’s citizen-facing categories in one document, plus a short universal set that works for any office on the ballot. All twelve categories are also available on their own as standalone, single-category banks — useful if you only want to ask about one thing.

The Full Bank

All twelve categories plus the universal set, in one document.

By category

1. The City’s Money, Good Government & Democracy

Revenue tools, strong-mayor powers, freedom of information, integrity oversight, city borrowing.

2. Homes & Homelessness

Development approvals, winter shelter capacity, encampment response, social housing funding, tenant protections, short-term rentals.

3. Getting Around

Congestion, transit service, cycling infrastructure, road safety, fare integration, parking policy.

4. Cost of Living, Food & Family Essentials

Utility rates, food access, family essentials, affordability pressures.

5. Health, Care & a Long Life

Primary care access, long-term care, mental health and crisis response, aging well.

6. Safety & Emergency Readiness

Emergency response, community safety, public-health resilience.

7. Clean, Green & Resilient

Climate adaptation, waste diversion, ravines and biodiversity, water and wastewater infrastructure.

8. Work, Business & the Local Economy

Local business support, labour conditions, economic development.

9. Community, Culture, Sport & Belonging

Arts and culture funding, sport and recreation access, community belonging.

10. Learning & Growing Up

Education access, youth opportunity, growing up in Toronto.

11. Newcomers

Refugee-claimant shelter and settlement, credential recognition, community partnership.

12. Technology, AI & the Automated City

Algorithmic decisions, automated vehicles, data privacy, digital equity.

Every question on this page and the pages it links to went through an independent verification pass before publication — every figure checked against its original source. The internal verification records have been removed from these public pages; the sourced facts and figures themselves are unchanged by that removal, and each page keeps a visible list of what it draws on. All thirteen banks (the full bank plus all twelve categories) are now live. Read the method behind that pass: How we verify.

Indigenous context: Community Safety Crime Policing

No Indigenous person or organization has reviewed, endorsed, or participated in this work, and this page says so.

First Nations policing, in their own words

Toronto's own policing is municipal (Toronto Police Service), not a First Nations-administered police service — but the province's First Nations policing system is the direct comparator for any "self-administered public safety" framing this page might eventually cover, and several Toronto and Ontario-wide organizations speak directly to it:

"I commend the Tyendinaga Police Service for their unwavering commitment to community safety... This collaboration exemplifies the strength and resilience of our First Nations when we work together in the interest of community well-being." — Ontario Regional Chief Abram Benedict, Chiefs of Ontario, October 2025, https://chiefs-of-ontario.org/chiefs-of-ontario-support-joint-police-operations-against-organized-crime-reiterate-call-for-federal-recognition-of-first-nations-policing-as-an-essential-service/ (Indigenous-authored)

"The federal government's refusal to recognize First Nations policing as an essential service continues to create dangerous gaps in public safety and disregards the inherent right of First Nations to self-governance." — Chiefs of Ontario, same source.

Ontario's nine self-administered First Nations police services are represented provincially by the Indigenous Police Chiefs of Ontario (https://ipcounified.ca/about/, Indigenous-authored) and nationally by the First Nations Chiefs of Police Association (https://fncpa.ca/, Indigenous-authored).

Restorative and Gladue-based justice in Toronto

Aboriginal Legal Services runs Toronto's Gladue Report/Letter program and Community Council — Canada's first urban Indigenous criminal-diversion program, bringing Indigenous offenders before community members rather than mainstream court. https://www.aboriginallegal.ca/community-council.html (Indigenous-authored; both program pages are procedural/descriptive, with no quotable position statement found as of this review — see an earlier internal research file for the full fetch record.)

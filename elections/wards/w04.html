Ward 04 — Parkdale-High Park

The math — 2022 baseline

  • Turnout: 29.7% — this is the citywide 2022 turnout figure carried in this dataset (563,124 of 1,898,750 electors voted in the 2022 general election), not a ward-specific number — per-ward turnout was not compiled in this review.
  • Margin: 3.9 percentage points — the 2022 winner's margin over the runner-up, as a share of votes cast in this ward (this figure, unlike turnout, is ward-level).
  • Acclaimed in 2022: no
  • Vote counts: not derivable from the data on file. races.csv carries a ward-level percentage margin but no ward-level elector count or total-ballots-cast figure, and turnout here is citywide, not per-ward — there is no arithmetic path from these two numbers to a vote count, so none is stated.

The hook — a block party decides this

The 2022 margin in this ward was just 3.9 percentage points between the top two finishers — about as tight as a Toronto council race gets. This dataset's turnout figure is citywide (29.7%), not ward-by-ward, so this card can't turn that into a precise vote count — but a gap this size is the kind of thing a single block party's worth of neighbours, voting differently than they did last time, could plausibly have closed.

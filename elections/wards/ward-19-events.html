Ward 19 election events — Beaches—East York

Every row verified August 15, 2026. Each event links the source it was read from, with its own freshness stamp. Candidate fields close soon — provincial nominations close August 20, 2 p.m.; municipal nominations close August 21, 2 p.m. — and this page re-checks after both, and again after Labour Day when fall all-candidates meetings are typically announced. This page lists events and dates; it does not tell you who to vote for, and it does not rate anyone.

Three elections touch this ward in ten weeks: the federal by-election on Monday, August 31 (the riding of Beaches—East York), and the Toronto municipal election on Monday, October 26 (mayor, Ward 19 councillor, school trustee). This page is every event and date we have verified, in one place: debates, official voting dates, and the civic events where the ward gathers. City-wide rows are chipped City-wide so nothing is mixed silently.

The math — 2022 baseline

  • Turnout: 26.48% — this ward’s own 2022 turnout (18,867 of 71,240 eligible electors cast a ballot, all voting methods), against a citywide 2022 figure of 29.66% (563,124 of 1,898,750). Per-ward figures were compiled 2026-08-15 from the City Clerk’s “Elections – Voter Statistics” 2022 dataset. This is 2022 municipal turnout — not a federal figure, and not comparable to one without saying so.
  • Margin: 41.5 percentage points — the 2022 winner’s margin over the runner-up, as a share of votes cast in this ward.
  • Acclaimed in 2022: no
  • Vote counts: the ward cast 18,867 ballots in 2022, so a 41.5-point margin is on the order of 7,800 votes. Treat that as an order of magnitude, not a count: the ballots-cast figure is every ballot cast in the ward across all offices on that ballot, and this dataset carries no per-candidate vote totals, so the councillor race’s own valid-vote base is somewhat smaller.

The hook — a block party decides this

The 2022 margin in this ward was 41.5 percentage points of a ward that cast 18,867 ballots — roughly 7,800 votes between first and second place. A real cushion, not a squeaker: closing a gap that size takes more than one block party’s worth of neighbours — more like several blocks organizing together.

The math — 2022 baseline

  • Turnout: 29.7% — this is the citywide 2022 turnout figure carried in this dataset (563,124 of 1,898,750 electors voted in the 2022 general election), not a ward-specific number — per-ward turnout was not compiled in this pass.
  • Margin: 65.4 percentage points — the 2022 winner's margin over the runner-up, as a share of votes cast in this ward (this figure, unlike turnout, is ward-level).
  • Acclaimed in 2022: no
  • Vote counts: not derivable from the data on file. races.csv carries a ward-level percentage margin but no ward-level elector count or total-ballots-cast figure, and turnout here is citywide, not per-ward — there is no arithmetic path from these two numbers to a vote count, so none is stated.

The hook — a block party decides this

The 2022 margin in this ward was 65.4 percentage points — a wide result. No ward-level vote count is on file here, but a gap this size would take a much bigger, sustained shift across the ward — not just one block's worth of neighbours — to move.

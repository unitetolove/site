Ward Math Cards — Toronto 2026

This is the raw math of every Toronto council race, one card per ward — each ward’s own 2022 turnout, its 2022 baseline margin, who has filed to run in 2026, and when each of them registered, built strictly from this project’s own receipted election dataset. Per-ward turnout was compiled for all 25 wards on 2026-08-15 from the City Clerk’s official 2022 voter-statistics dataset, and each percentage is recomputed from that ward’s own elector and ballot integers — the citywide figure (29.66%) is carried alongside it as a comparison, never as a substitute. Every filed candidate is listed once, alphabetically by surname, with no ranking or characterization — that’s the receipts culture this whole project runs on.

Provisional — data as of 2026-08-16; nominations close August 21, 2026. Filed-candidate counts and competitiveness tiers will change as more candidates register; each ward page carries its own receipt with the exact source and date. These figures are 2022 municipal turnout — a different election and a different denominator from any federal figure elsewhere on this site.

Read next: the whole field, honestly mapped — the standing baseline these cards sit inside — and the Platform Baseline, every candidate’s published platform measured for breadth, depth and quality. Back up to State of the Election for the full biweekly record.

Toronto election events — the city-wide calendar

Every row verified August 15, 2026. Each event links the source it was read from, with its own freshness stamp. Candidate fields close soon — provincial nominations close August 20, 2 p.m.; municipal nominations close August 21, 2 p.m. — and this page re-checks after both, and again after Labour Day when fall all-candidates meetings are typically announced. This page lists events and dates; it does not tell you who to vote for, and it does not rate anyone.

Toronto votes three times before November: a federal by-election in Beaches—East York on August 31, a provincial by-election in Scarborough Southwest on September 3, and the city-wide municipal election on October 26 (mayor, councillor, school trustee). This calendar carries every debate and every official voting date for all three, plus city-wide civic events. Ward rows are chipped and link down to their own full ward calendar.

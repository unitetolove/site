Beaches—East York voted 71.9% in April 2025 — one of the highest-participation ridings in Canada. That average hides a canyon. These five figures are the canyon, one claim at a time; the flagship scrolly (Two worlds, one street) walks the same ground on the map. Hover any dot or bar for its numbers.

Three terms, used everywhere on this page:
E-day turnout — votes cast on election day only, as a share of registered electors. The only number available per polling division or neighbourhood.
Advance votes — ballots cast early: 42% of all votes riding-wide, 45% within the zone universe these figures map. Recorded only per advance zone, never per polling division.
True total turnout — e-day + advance together, per zone. The honest measure; whenever a figure can use it, it does, and says so.
Non-partisan floor: this page maps participation only — no candidate, party, or individual-level data appears anywhere. Offered identically to every neighbour of every politics.
Method: committed CSVs built from Elections Canada open data + the City of Toronto's open data portal, raw receipts kept; per-PD figures are election-day only — advance votes live at zone grain (the honest total-turnout comparator). Merged/void polls carry notes; receiver polls read slightly high. The by-election may renumber PDs — zones + neighbourhoods are the stable grain.

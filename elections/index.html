State of the Election — 2026

One question, asked at two scales: what can a voter actually read before voting day — Monday, October 26, 2026? The State of the Election is the running, dated answer — for Toronto, and now for all of Ontario. The whole field, mapped honestly, in editions that publish roughly every two weeks between now and voting day. Nothing below is a prediction or an endorsement; it’s a record, sourced and dated as it’s built, and it serves every candidate identically.

State of the Election — Toronto 2026, the biweekly record

A running, dated record of Toronto’s 2026 municipal election — the whole field, mapped honestly, in editions that publish roughly every two weeks between now and voting day. Nothing below is a prediction or an endorsement; it’s a record, sourced and dated as it’s built.

Start here: the baseline. Before the editions, read the whole field, honestly mapped — the standing baseline this biweekly record builds on — and its companion, the Platform Baseline: every candidate’s published platform, measured for breadth, depth and quality, re-measured with each edition.

Editions

Every two weeks between now and voting day — Monday, October 26, 2026 — a new dated edition of this record will publish here. Nothing is posted before it exists; this list only shows what’s real right now.

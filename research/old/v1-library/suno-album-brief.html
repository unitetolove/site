Album Brief (Suno AI)

THE BRIEF

This album tells the story of Toronto's homelessness crisis through twelve songs. Each song is grounded in verified data but speaks like a person, not a policy brief. The genre mix matters: some tracks are folk/country for accessibility, some hip-hop for credibility with affected communities, some rock/punk for anger, some gospel/choir for moral weight.

Every song connects a specific verified fact to a human experience. The campaign's firewall rules apply: no false claims, no fabricated quotes from real people, no specific named individuals as villains.

TRACK 6 — "216"

Theme: 132 confirmed shelter resident deaths in Toronto in 2021 (223 broader total per TPH's current dashboard); 59 shelter resident deaths in 2024 (corrected again, July 5, 2026 — this document's July 4 "fix" to 216 was itself wrong. Direct verification against TPH's own March 2023 data release, independently corroborated by CBC, the Globe and Mail, and MAP Centre, confirms 223 is TPH's own current, standing figure for 2021 — an upward revision from an earlier 216 that TPH itself superseded before this library ever cited either number. MAIN_HMK_021 already documented this exact supersession; the July 4 fix should have checked that document first and didn't. See MAIN_TRACK_001 for the full account of this correction-to-a-correction.)

A genuine, unresolved tension, disclosed rather than silently fixed: this track's title, structural refrain, and every lyric line use "216," not 223 — the song is built around that specific number, not just referencing it in passing. Correcting the number now would mean rewriting the song, not fixing a data table; that's a creative decision beyond the scope of a citation-accuracy pass. Until a rewrite happens, if this track is used publicly, disclose plainly that TPH's current figure is 223, and that the song's "216" reflects an earlier, since-superseded count. PRODUCTION NOTES FOR SUNO

Voice and style guidance for the set: - Tracks 1, 3, 5: primarily white/working class register to expand coalition audience - Tracks 4, 7, 11: primarily Black Canadian register; don't whitewash the racial disparity data - Tracks 6, 8, 12: gospel/choir — inclusive, cross-racial, morally universal - Tracks 2, 9: country/folk — aimed at reaching suburban and rural Ontario audiences - Tracks 3, 10: rock/alt-country — anger without spectacle

What NOT to do: - Do not name Doug Ford, Olivia Chow, or any living individual by name in lyrics - Do not include any statistic not in the verified research library - Do not use inflammatory language about specific organizations - The power is in the specificity of the documented facts, not in the anger

What the album is about: Not hatred of people. Not hatred of politicians. The specific, documented, verifiable gap between what we spend, what we get, and what the evidence says we could get. Anger at systems, not souls.

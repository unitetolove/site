FROZEN EDITION — July 2026. This page is part of the v1 Toronto homelessness research library, a complete, sealed project frozen at its July 2026 figures (92% of its 770 claims individually verified against primary sources). It is a product of its July-2026 campaign context, published as a citable historical artifact. Where our newer research disagrees, a dated margin note points at the living page — the frozen text is never silently rewritten. Why there are two editions.

The frozen v1 edition — the data

The edition’s machine-readable spine, published verbatim: the 770-claim ledger (92% individually verified, with per-claim source URLs and verification paths), the source registry, and the underlying data series. Frozen at July 2026 — for our current datasets see open data.

MAIN_DATA_001_Claim_Ledger.csv

Download — 773 rows, 12 columns: claim_id, short_claim, canonical_metric, value, unit, time_frame, fact_or_inference, confidence_tag, primary_source, source_url, verification_path, notes

MAIN_DATA_002_Shelter_Sites_2017_2019_Baseline.csv

Download — 69 rows, 7 columns: organization, shelter_name, address, postal_code, sectors_served, max_capacity_2017_2019, data_currency_note

MAIN_DATA_003_Resource_Registry.csv

Download — 607 rows, 9 columns: resource_id, url, category, description, in_sources_md, sources_md_id, supporting_claims, claim_count, ingestion_batch

MAIN_DATA_012_Deaths_of_Shelter_Residents_Monthly.csv

Download — 232 rows, 7 columns: _id, Year, Month, Total decedents, Male, Female, Transgender/Non-binary/Two-Spirit

FROZEN EDITION — July 2026. This page is part of the v1 Toronto homelessness research library, a complete, sealed project frozen at its July 2026 figures (92% of its 770 claims individually verified against primary sources). It is a product of its July-2026 campaign context, published as a citable historical artifact. Where our newer research disagrees, a dated margin note points at the living page — the frozen text is never silently rewritten. Why there are two editions.

The v1 product line — 16 derivative products

Built from the frozen edition’s verified claim base in June–July 2026: tools for journalists, voters, candidates, and organizers. Each product cites the edition’s own claim ledger; the full ledger and source registry are on the data page.

  • Wave 1 Campaign Assets — The first derivative asset pack built from the verified claim base.
  • Journalist Quick-Start Guide — The report's findings repackaged for a reporter on deadline, claim keys included.
  • 2026 Municipal Election Toolkit — An eleven-question candidate questionnaire, ward map, council voting records, and deputation templates for the October 26, 2026 election.
  • Freedom-of-Information Filing Templates — Ready-to-file FOI requests targeting the report's open accountability questions.
  • Fact Sheet — The load-bearing figures on one page, each with its claim key.
  • Album Brief (Suno AI) — A creative brief turning the research into music — an experiment in reach.
  • Provincial Promises File — Documented provincial commitments on housing and homelessness, tracked against delivery.
  • Ward-Level Accountability Tracker — Shelter capacity and council record, ward by ward.
  • Provincial Accountability Timeline — The dated sequence of provincial policy choices the report documents.
  • Five Facts — The five hardest-to-dispute findings, sourced.
  • Follow the Money — Where the $897.957M actually goes — contracts, surpluses, per-bed costs.
  • Candidate Accountability Guide — How to put the report's questions to 2026 council candidates, on the record.
  • Volunteer Quick Reference Card — The one-card version for door-to-door and event conversations.
  • Compassion Narrative Assets — Person-first framings of the same verified findings.
  • Declining Shelter-Cost Transparency — A standalone finding: the City's per-bed cost reporting has become less transparent over time.
  • Public Opinion and Messaging Research — What surveyed opinion says about which framings move which audiences.

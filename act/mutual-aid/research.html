The research behind this

Before building anything we studied the networks that worked — Los Angeles’s mutual-aid ecosystem and its fire response, Occupy Sandy, the UK’s COVID mutual-aid groups and what made them last, and Toronto’s own 2020 wave, including why most of it went dormant — and we live-verified which local organizations are active today (that’s the directory).

The full research pack publishes here after its independent verification pass — sources, corrections and all, like everything else on this site. Until then, the short version: regular rhythm beats big launches, cells outlive umbrellas, joy is a survival requirement, and nobody should ever have to prove they deserve help.

We never handle money. No donations are taken here or anywhere — if you want to give money, give it directly to the verified organizations in the directory.

Volunteer-run and non-partisan — no party, no candidate, no memberships, nothing for sale. Beaches–East York Mutual Aid Networks (“BEY Mutual Aid”) is part of Toronto’s Mutual Aid Networks: a network of networks — nobody owns mutual aid.

Toronto's Mutual-Aid Landscape, Live-Verified

Every named organization checked alive-or-dormant on 14 Aug 2026, method and date per row — because a directory nobody re-verifies becomes a list of dead phone numbers.

Every row below was checked TODAY by direct fetch or current-record search after the operator's correction ("verify everything in Toronto, most of that is not active any longer" — he was right). Method column says how. Rules: a live website is NOT proof of a live group (two sites below are frozen in 2022); a dead umbrella is not proof the cells died; anything Facebook/Instagram-only is marked UNVERIFIABLE-FROM-HERE, not guessed.

The headline, honestly: Toronto's COVID mutual-aid wave is dead. CareMongering-TO (the movement's global spark) is inactive; the pod networks (Davenport, Progress Toronto's) are frozen COVID-era pages; the MPP "mutual aid" resource pages are stale lists. What survived is what either (a) institutionalized (settlement-house agencies, food banks), or (b) specialized around one sharp function (fridges, bike delivery, encampment outreach). Nobody is operating a general neighbour-to-neighbour mutual aid network in Beaches–East York in 2026. The field is open — and the graveyard is the design brief.

4. What the graveyard teaches (binding on the design)

  1. Enthusiasm is not a structure. CareMongering hit 25k members in weeks and still died —

scale without rhythm, roles, and care jobs is a balloon, not a building (matches the UK 75%-decay literature exactly).

  1. Cells outlive umbrellas; functions outlive movements. ESN's umbrella died, one cell

fought on; the general networks died, the fridge/bike/food specializations survived. Our network = light umbrella + sharp functions (the loop, the walk, the Hub Hour) + block cells.

  1. Institutions are the continuity layer. Everything verified ACTIVE in the riding is 50+

years old or government-backed. A network that WEAVES the agencies together with neighbour capacity inherits their continuity; one that competes with them inherits the graveyard.

  1. Facebook is where Toronto mutual aid went to die. Every dead group lived on Meta; every

design doc we ship keeps the coordination spine on owned/portable platforms (Discord export, Signal, the site) with paper/phone front doors.

  1. The riding-framed pod network was already invented here (Davenport, 2020). It didn't

survive COVID's end because it was built FOR COVID. Ours is built for the permanent base of the pyramid — need that does not expire.

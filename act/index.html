Act

Nothing here requires a committee, a membership, or anyone’s permission. This is the ladder — every rung is optional, every rung returns a receipt, and the first one costs thirty seconds. Climb exactly as far as you want.

Thirty seconds

Sign up for the conversation. A name and a way to reach you. The running public count is how the city watches itself show up — your signature literally moves a number everyone can see. This is the whole first ask.

An hour

The Harvest — bring us what your corner of the city already knows. A resource, a dataset, a story, a fix, a person we should read. What you send becomes part of the commons, credited if you want credit.

The Civic Hour — one hour, on your own schedule, doing one civic thing. The page explains the practice and gives you a menu.

This week

Next actions — the live list of what moves the project right now: things to read, check, share, and build, updated as the work moves. If you only bookmark one page in this section, bookmark that one.

Spread it. Tell one person. Send one page from the Library to the friend who argues about that exact issue. Posters, memes, and print kits are being built now — until they land, the Harvest is also where to send the ones you make yourself. Post only where you have the right to post.

As far as you want to go

Run for office. In October 2026 every council seat in Ontario is on the ballot, and many go uncontested. This site equips any candidate, of any politics, on identical terms, for free. If not you — vote, and help someone else decide to.

Find your people. Toronto’s tribes, assemblies, and the rooms where the conversation already lives — the Connect section is the door.

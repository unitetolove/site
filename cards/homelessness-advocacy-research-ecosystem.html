RC-ARE-01 — Adopt This Leaf's Own Citation-Crediting Practice Project-Wide — Recommendation Card

Card id: RC-ARE-01 · File: research/cards/homelessness-advocacy-research-ecosystem.md#rc-are-01 · Card class: INTERNAL Issue slug: homelessness-advocacy-research-ecosystem · Backgrounder: homelessness-advocacy-research-ecosystem Trust class: INHERITED (this library's prior synthesis document (Advocacy Research Ecosystem)) · Date: 2026-07-14

Problem

This project's own research draws heavily on work originated by Toronto's homelessness advocacy and research sector (TAEH's data dashboard, COH/Gaetz's definitional frameworks, Wellesley Institute's equity data, and more), but individual backgrounders elsewhere in this project do not consistently name the originating organization or researcher in the same sentence as the finding — this leaf's own promoted material names this as a stated recommended practice ("Wherever this library cites a statistic or framework originating from one of these organizations, name the organization... in the same sentence — not just in a footnote") that has not yet been checked for consistent application project-wide [INHERITED].

Action

A future CURATE-lane pass audits a sample of this project's Domain H backgrounders for whether findings originating from the organizations/researchers profiled in this leaf are credited by name in-line, and corrects any that only cite a CL-#### ID without naming the originating organization where the underlying source itself is one of these named bodies.

Jurisdiction split

  • City does: not applicable — internal research-practice action.
  • City demands of Province: none.
  • City demands of Feds: none.

Cost

Order-of-magnitude: low — a documentation-audit pass across already-written backgrounders, not new research.

Funding path

None required; internal CURATE-lane action.

Who benefits, and how

The credited organizations/researchers themselves, via accurate public credit for originating work this project draws on — directly consistent with this project's own stated credibility argument ("everything is sourced, nothing is invented"); readers of this project's research, via clearer provenance.

Who bears the cost, and how

No payer class — internal research-maintenance cost only.

Financial ROI

Not applicable.

Economic ROI

Range: not applicable. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high on the "not applicable" finding.

Social ROI

Directional: proper crediting directly serves this project's own stated ethical commitment (this leaf's own promoted material states the campaign's "credibility depends on never claiming originality for what others built first") and reduces the risk of a hostile reader finding uncredited reliance on named organizations' work. Confidence: medium.

Environmental ROI

Range: not applicable. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high.

Evidence

  • this library's prior synthesis document (Advocacy Research Ecosystem) · INHERITED · stated citation-crediting recommendation (Part 5)

Confidence & uncertainties

High confidence on the problem statement (the recommendation is explicit in the leaf's own promoted material); the audit itself has not yet been performed and would need to be scoped as a bounded task, not attempted in this pass.

Status

DRAFT — blocked on: scoping the audit's sample size and which backgrounders to check first; Lane R pass.

RC-ARE-02 — Keep This Leaf's Leadership/Contact Currency Checks on a Standing Refresh Cycle — Recommendation Card

Card id: RC-ARE-02 · File: research/cards/homelessness-advocacy-research-ecosystem.md#rc-are-02 · Card class: INTERNAL Issue slug: homelessness-advocacy-research-ecosystem · Backgrounder: homelessness-advocacy-research-ecosystem Trust class: NEW-LOAD-BEARING (backgrounder NEW-2026-1) · Date: 2026-07-14

Problem

This pass found that TAEH's leadership had changed since the leaf's own promoted material was written (a March 2026 appointment, resolving a leadership question that material had flagged as uncertain just months earlier) [NEW-2026-1]. This leaf's own promoted material separately flags at least one other organization's (Houselink & Mainstay's) post-merger leadership as unconfirmed. Organizational leadership in this sector changes often enough that a one-time research pass will predictably go stale.

Action

This leaf's own maintenance record notes that leadership/contact details for the organizations profiled here should be re-checked directly against each organization's own website on a periodic basis (e.g., each time this leaf is next substantively revisited), rather than treated as permanently current from this pass's check.

Jurisdiction split

  • City does: not applicable.
  • City demands of Province: none.
  • City demands of Feds: none.

Cost

Order-of-magnitude: negligible — a periodic direct-website-check practice, not a new research program.

Funding path

None required.

Who benefits, and how

Readers of this leaf's research, via current rather than stale leadership/contact information; the profiled organizations, via not being misrepresented by an outdated leadership claim.

Who bears the cost, and how

No payer class — negligible periodic-check cost only.

Financial ROI

Not applicable.

Economic ROI

Range: not applicable. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high on the "not applicable" finding.

Social ROI

Directional: this is a direct, low-cost application of this project's own trust-layer discipline ("anything not yet trustworthy says so on its face") to a category of fact (organizational leadership) that is unusually prone to going stale between research passes. Confidence: medium.

Environmental ROI

Range: not applicable. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high.

Evidence

  • NEW-2026-1 · quote-context (this pass) · TAEH leadership change, March 2026, resolving prior uncertainty

Confidence & uncertainties

High confidence on the problem statement (directly demonstrated by this pass's own finding); this is a process recommendation, not a substantive research claim.

Status

DRAFT — blocked on: this project's own broader maintenance-cadence decision (CURATE lane), not a leaf-specific blocker.

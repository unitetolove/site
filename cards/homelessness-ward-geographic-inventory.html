RC-WGI-01 — Join the Base-Shelter and Broader-Occupancy Datasets by Ward to Close This Leaf's Own Named Gap — Recommendation Card

Card id: RC-WGI-01 · File: research/cards/homelessness-ward-geographic-inventory.md#rc-wgi-01 · Card class: INTERNAL Issue slug: homelessness-ward-geographic-inventory · Backgrounder: homelessness-ward-geographic-inventory Trust class: INHERITED (CL-771) · Date: 2026-07-14

Problem

This leaf's authoritative inventory establishes that 7 of 25 Toronto wards have zero base emergency shelter sites [CL-771], but this figure does not account for temporary hotel or warming-centre capacity tracked separately in shelter-system-capacity-strain's own broader occupancy dataset. The leaf's own promoted material names this exact combined analysis as a specific, bounded, not-yet-done next step. Without it, "7 wards have zero shelter capacity" could overstate or understate the real geographic-equity picture depending on where temporary capacity happens to sit.

Action

This project (CURATE lane) performs a one-time ward-level join of the 59-site base inventory against shelter-system-capacity-strain's broader occupancy dataset, to determine whether any of the seven zero-base-shelter wards have meaningful temporary capacity, and publishes the combined finding as an update to this backgrounder.

Jurisdiction split

  • City does: not applicable — this is an internal research action, not a City of Toronto program action. (Stated explicitly per template guardrail rather than left blank.)
  • City demands of Province: none.
  • City demands of Feds: none.

Cost

Order-of-magnitude: low — a data-join exercise using two datasets this project already has committed (this library's prior data compilation (Current Base Shelter Locations Wards) and whatever occupancy-by-address data shelter-system-capacity-strain already cites), not a new data-collection effort.

Funding path

None required; internal research-maintenance action within existing CURATE-lane discipline.

Who benefits, and how

Readers of this project's ward-equity findings, via an accurate combined picture rather than a base-shelter-only one; any future ward-councillor-facing accountability use of this data, via a more complete and defensible figure.

Who bears the cost, and how

No payer class — negligible internal research cost only.

Financial ROI

Not applicable — internal research action.

Economic ROI

Range: not applicable. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high on the "not applicable" finding.

Social ROI

Directional: a combined dataset directly serves this project's own accuracy discipline (its trust-layer rule) and prevents an incomplete "zero shelter capacity" claim from being used, inaccurately, in local political debate about any of the seven wards. Confidence: medium.

Environmental ROI

Range: not applicable — no physical component. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high.

Evidence

  • CL-771 · INHERITED · 59-site base inventory, 7 zero-shelter wards
  • CL-710 · INHERITED · earlier confirming pass

Confidence & uncertainties

High confidence on the problem statement (the gap is explicitly named in this leaf's own promoted material); the action itself is mechanical and low-risk.

Status

DRAFT — blocked on: confirming shelter-system-capacity-strain's occupancy data is available at address/ward granularity (not yet checked in this pass); Lane R pass.

RC-WGI-02 — Track the 66 Third Street Litigation Outcome as a Precedent for Future HSCIS Site Disputes — Recommendation Card

Card id: RC-WGI-02 · File: research/cards/homelessness-ward-geographic-inventory.md#rc-wgi-02 · Card class: INTERNAL Issue slug: homelessness-ward-geographic-inventory · Backgrounder: homelessness-ward-geographic-inventory Trust class: NEW-LOAD-BEARING (backgrounder NEW-2026-1, NEW-2026-2) · Date: 2026-07-14

Problem

The 66 Third Street litigation is this leaf's own concrete test case for the site-selection-transparency tension, and this pass found it is more actively contested — with both organized opposition and organized support — than the leaf's own promoted material alone showed. The litigation's outcome (still pending as of this pass) will likely set a practical precedent for how future HSCIS site disputes unfold, given 7 more unfunded sites remain to be selected [CL-163].

Action

This leaf tracks and updates its own record of the 66 Third Street litigation's outcome as a named, dated follow-up item, and flags the precedent for shelter-system-capacity-strain's own RC-SSCS-01 card (which proposes funding the remaining 7 HSCIS sites) as directly relevant context — new sites funded under that card would face the same site-selection-transparency tension this leaf documents.

Jurisdiction split

  • City does: not applicable — research-tracking action only.
  • City demands of Province: none directly.
  • City demands of Feds: none directly.

Cost

Order-of-magnitude: negligible — research-tracking action within CURATE-lane discipline.

Funding path

None required.

Who benefits, and how

This project's own HSCIS-related research (this leaf and shelter-system-capacity-strain), via an up-to-date precedent for how site-selection disputes actually resolve; any future ward affected by one of the 7 remaining unfunded HSCIS sites, indirectly, via this project's own research reflecting the real, current state of the one test case available.

Who bears the cost, and how

No payer class — negligible internal research-maintenance cost only.

Financial ROI

Not applicable.

Economic ROI

Range: not applicable. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high on the "not applicable" finding.

Social ROI

Directional: keeping this leaf's own most concrete case study current, rather than frozen at its filing-date framing, serves this project's own accuracy discipline directly. Confidence: medium.

Environmental ROI

Range: not applicable. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high.

Evidence

  • NEW-2026-1 · quote-context (this pass) · ELSSC counter-organizing record, successful July 15 2025 vote
  • NEW-2026-2 · quote-context (this pass) · New Toronto Initiative's own cost-comparator claims
  • CL-574, CL-50008 · INHERITED · litigation filing detail

Confidence & uncertainties

Medium confidence: the litigation's outcome was not located in this pass and remains genuinely pending; the two advocacy groups' own claims (both NTI's and ELSSC's) are attributed to their sources rather than independently verified.

Card RC-FZ-01 — Retire "Functional Zero" Language in Favour of Built for Zero's Current Multi-Dimensional Framework — Recommendation Card

Card id: RC-FZ-01 · File: research/cards/homelessness-functional-zero-case-studies.md#rc-fz-01 Issue slug: homelessness-functional-zero-case-studies · Backgrounder: homelessness-functional-zero-case-studies Trust class: NEW-LOAD-BEARING (backgrounder NEW-2026-2) · Date: 2026-07-14

Problem

"Functional zero" remains in common civic and advocacy usage in Canada as a target/aspiration term (including in this leaf's own promoted material's framing), but Built for Zero — the organization that originated and defined the term — formally retired it as an active designation in 2025-2026, citing practical limits in large urban systems and confusion when visible encampments persisted alongside a technical "functional zero" designation for one population [NEW-2026-2]. Any Toronto-facing document (this project's own included) that cites "functional zero" as a still-current target risks using a standard its own author has moved past.

Action

This project's own writing (backgrounders, briefs, day-one papers, any future Toronto-facing recommendation) uses Built for Zero's current framing — progress toward homelessness being "rare, brief, nonrecurring, safe, and believable," measured via "verified reductions" — rather than "functional zero," when describing a forward-looking target; "functional zero" is used only in its historical sense, describing what Medicine Hat, London, Rockford, and others achieved under the now-retired standard.

Jurisdiction split

  • City does: if Toronto ever adopts or references a Built for Zero-style target, doing so under the organization's current framework rather than the retired one is within the City's own communications/planning discretion — no external approval required.
  • City demands of Province: none — this is a terminology/methodology-currency question, not a funding or authority question.
  • City demands of Feds: none.

Cost

Order-of-magnitude: negligible — this is a documentation/terminology-discipline action, not a program or spending action.

Funding path

None required.

Who benefits, and how

Readers of this project's own research (and any Toronto policy document citing "functional zero"), via accurate, current terminology that doesn't imply a still-active designation that has in fact been retired; this project's own credibility, via avoiding a citation that a hostile fact-checker could correctly flag as outdated.

Who bears the cost, and how

No payer class — negligible internal documentation-discipline cost only.

Financial ROI

Not applicable — no financial mechanism.

Economic ROI

Range: not applicable. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high on the "not applicable" finding.

Social ROI

Directional: accurate terminology avoids a specific, documented failure mode Built for Zero itself named — "visible encampments, creating confusion about whether progress was real" [NEW-2026-2] — which is exactly the kind of credibility gap this project's own standing rules (survive hostile scrutiny) exist to prevent. Confidence: medium — the terminology-accuracy benefit is clear; its practical effect on any reader's actual trust is not separately measured.

Environmental ROI

Range: not applicable — no physical/environmental component. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high on the "not applicable" finding.

Evidence

  • NEW-2026-2 · quote-context (this pass) · Built for Zero's formal retirement of functional zero, replacement framework

Confidence & uncertainties

High confidence on the underlying fact (Built for Zero's own current page states this plainly); this card's own recommendation (use current terminology going forward) is a low-stakes documentation-discipline action, not a policy recommendation requiring independent verification.

Status

DRAFT — blocked on: Lane R pass; a documentation-style-guide update for this project's own future writing (a mechanical follow-up, not a research task).

Card RC-FZ-02 — Flag Medicine Hat's Non-Monotonic 2022-2025 Trend Precisely, Not as a Simple Reversal or Recovery — Recommendation Card

Card id: RC-FZ-02 · File: research/cards/homelessness-functional-zero-case-studies.md#rc-fz-02 Issue slug: homelessness-functional-zero-case-studies · Backgrounder: homelessness-functional-zero-case-studies Trust class: NEW-LOAD-BEARING (backgrounder NEW-2026-1) · Date: 2026-07-14

Problem

This leaf's own promoted material stops at Medicine Hat's 2022 reversal (19 chronically homeless, ~150 in-and-out) [CL-176]. This pass's live discovery found the count has since fluctuated further: 120 (2022), 104 (2024), 136 (2025) [NEW-2026-1] — a pattern that supports neither a clean "reversed and staying bad" narrative nor a "recovering" narrative. Any future citation of Medicine Hat's status (in this project or elsewhere) risks flattening this into whichever single data point supports the citing document's own argument.

Action

This project's own future citations of Medicine Hat state the full three-point trend (or whatever the current full series is at time of citation) rather than a single year's figure, and distinguish the roughly 33 people counted outdoors from the 136 total (which includes sheltered, transitional-housing, and in-custody populations) [NEW-2026-1], per this leaf's own backgrounder discipline.

Jurisdiction split

  • City does: not applicable — this is a citation-discipline action internal to this research project, not a Toronto municipal action. (Included for template completeness per the L6 guardrail requiring an explicit statement when a bucket is empty.)
  • City demands of Province: none.
  • City demands of Feds: none.

Cost

Order-of-magnitude: negligible — internal citation-discipline practice.

Funding path

None required.

Who benefits, and how

Readers of this project's research, via an accurate, non-flattened picture of Medicine Hat's actual trajectory; Medicine Hat's own local stakeholders, indirectly, via not having their city's record used as a simplified prop for an argument the actual data doesn't cleanly support in either direction.

Who bears the cost, and how

No payer class — negligible internal cost only.

Financial ROI

Not applicable.

Economic ROI

Range: not applicable. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high on the "not applicable" finding.

Social ROI

Directional: precise, non-flattened citation of a real city's real record is a direct application of this project's own trust rule ("anything not yet trustworthy says so on its face") — Medicine Hat's own multi-directional trend is exactly the kind of finding that should say so rather than be smoothed into a cleaner story. Confidence: medium.

Environmental ROI

Range: not applicable. Comparator source: none needed. Confidence: high.

Evidence

  • NEW-2026-1 · quote-context (this pass) · Medicine Hat 2025 PiT count, full three-year series and category breakdown
  • CL-175, CL-176 · INHERITED · Medicine Hat's original 2021 achievement and 2022 reversal

Confidence & uncertainties

Medium-high confidence: the three-point count series (120/104/136) is directly quoted from a single news source citing the Medicine Hat Community Housing Society's own report, not independently cross-verified against the MHCHS report itself in this pass (flagged in the backgrounder's own open questions).

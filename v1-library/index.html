The frozen v1 edition — Toronto homelessness research, July 2026

A complete, sealed research project on Toronto homelessness: one public report, sixteen derivative products, and the full verification machinery behind them — a 770-claim ledger (92% individually verified against primary sources), a 608-row source registry, and a public corrections log. Frozen at its July-2026 figures and published as a citable historical edition alongside our living research library.

  • The Toronto Homelessness Report — how we got here, who is accountable, and the fastest, cheapest way out.
  • The 16 products — journalist guide, Election Toolkit 2026, FOI templates, ward accountability tracker, fact sheets.
  • The data — claim ledger, source registry, deaths and overdose series, as plain CSV.
  • Why there are two editions — and which one to cite.

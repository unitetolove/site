Faith & festival calendar

One verified calendar — August 2026 through January 2028 — of Toronto’s faith, festival, cultural and civic community moments: when a community gathering is likely to welcome a visitor, and just as important, when to stay quiet.

What this is

This is a timing resource, not an invitation on anyone’s behalf. It doesn’t contact anyone, convene anything, or claim any community's calendar as ours. Every kit named below is a free tool a community can pick up and run in its own name — never an instruction to show up and run something in a room that isn't ours. See the kits themselves, or choosing tools for bigger rooms.

Where a tradition’s own norms make an ask inappropriate — a fast, a day of atonement, a period of mourning — this calendar says so under AVOID, and that guidance is as much the point as the dates themselves. Community moments the calendar defers entirely to that community’s own leadership — Indigenous-designated days above all — are marked as such rather than pointed at a generic tool.

A note on the far half of this calendar: the August 2026 – January 2027 span was verified against primary and community sources throughout. The February 2027 – January 2028 span is necessarily calculated further out — lunar and movable dates a year or more ahead carry slightly wider uncertainty (typically ±1 day, occasionally ±1 week for dates still governed by moon-sighting rather than astronomical calculation) than the near-term months. Re-confirm anything in the 2027–28 span within a few weeks of using it, the same rule that applies to any movable date.

How to read the tables

Date(s) are Gregorian, 2026 unless marked otherwise; movable/lunar dates carry a source and are worth a fresh check close to the day, since local moon-sighting can shift Islamic/lunar observances by a day. Fits well with names a free kit that suits the kind of gathering involved, plus a plain read of how big or how personal a moment it tends to be — never a headcount claim. Timing guidance is the approach window, and any AVOID. Source links the primary or community reference used.

The two that actually land in 2028 are the Gregorian New Year (Jan 1) and, far more significantly, Lunar New Year 2028 on Jan 26 — the Year of the Monkey. Toronto's Chinese, Vietnamese, and Korean communities are among the largest ethnocultural-diaspora populations in the city, this date sits right at the end of the window, and — unlike most of this calendar's entries — it's a single day where three distinct diaspora communities' biggest home-and-temple gathering of the year coincides exactly.

The AVOID stretches, gathered in one place

For anyone scanning fast: these are every date or window above where the calendar says do not ask anything of the named community.

  • Aug 9, 2026 / Jun 21, 2027 — Indigenous-designated days, deferred entirely to Indigenous-led organizations' own governance
  • Sept 8–15, 2026 / Aug 28–Sept 4, 2027 — Śvetāmbara Jain Paryushana (fasting/penance)
  • Sept 11–21, 2026 / Oct 1–11, 2027 — the Jewish Days of Awe in full; Yom Kippur itself is absolute both years
  • Sept 15–24, 2026 — Digambar Jain Das Lakshan Parva
  • Sept 30, 2026 / Sept 30, 2027 — National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, a day of mourning, not a day to make an ask
  • Nov 11, 2026 / Nov 11, 2027 — Remembrance Day itself (the surrounding week is fine)
  • Nov 15 – Jan 6 (both cycles) — Eastern Orthodox/Coptic/Ethiopian Nativity Fast (breaks Jan 7 each year)
  • Dec 24–25, 2026 / Dec 24–25, 2027 — Christmas Eve/Day
  • Feb 8 – Mar 9, 2027 — Ramadan daytime fasting (evenings-only, invitation-only; breaks at Eid al-Fitr, Mar 9)
  • ~Jun 16, 2027 — Ashura, a solemn mourning day for Shia Muslims
  • Jan 28, 2028 — Ramadan 2028 begins right at this calendar's edge

How this was verified

Every date claim in this calendar was independently checked before publishing: 73 date claims verified, 66 confirmed exactly as researched, 7 corrected on the record (including three-week and one-day errors caught before they could reach print) — a normal, honest rate for a calendar this size, not hidden or rounded away. Fixed civil and statutory dates were cross-checked against federal/provincial holiday lists; long-fixed liturgical dates are noted as general knowledge rather than separately sourced, since they don't move year to year. Every internal link in this calendar was verified to resolve. Where a source disagreed with another, or a far-future event hadn't yet published its exact date, that is flagged inline with ⚠ rather than presented as certain.

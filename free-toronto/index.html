Free Toronto guides

Short, plain-language guides pulled from the Free Toronto Resource Atlas — our full 920-row directory of free and low-cost Toronto resources — organized around one need at a time instead of one big page. Pick the one that matches what you're dealing with right now. Every link in every guide was checked directly against the provider's own page. Neutral civic reference information — not affiliated with any campaign, candidate, or advocacy organization.

Want the whole picture instead? These guides are slices of one larger project — the Free Toronto Resource Atlas — organized by topic for quick reading. The Atlas itself is the full, single-page reference with everything in one place.

Every guide below is rendered mechanically from a sealed, link-checked source — 39 of 42 guides in the fleet are published here; the rest are named, not hidden. Every link was checked directly against the provider’s own page.

How to use this page: scan the section headings for the situation you’re in — they’re organized by need, not by bureaucracy — open the one guide that matches, and share it with the person who needs it. That’s the whole design: one need, one guide, one gift.

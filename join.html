Unsubscribe anytime, one click. Your details are never sold or shared, there are no dues, and no donations are ever asked for — here or anywhere on this site.

Under 18? Welcome — please have a parent or guardian send this for you, from their own email. That’s the whole rule, and you can withdraw anytime.

Your email app should now be open with everything you wrote. Press Send there and it’s on its way to a human.

Prefer to write it yourself? Copy this into your own email instead of using the button above — same door, same welcome.

To: unknownsoldier@unitetolove.ca
Subject: Joining the conversation

Include:
• your name
• how to reach you (email or phone)
• anything else from this page you’d like us to know

Three things, plainly

What happens next: a human reads it — every note — and you get a receipt about what your note became, not a newsletter.

What we never do: sell or share your details, ask for dues or donations, or take foreign money. Ever.

The public count: the number of people who’ve joined goes live on the homepage once the first few dozen are in — until then it’s held, honestly, at zero. No fake numbers, here or anywhere.

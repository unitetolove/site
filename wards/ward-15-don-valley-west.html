Ward 15 — Don Valley West

Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

Don Valley West covers Leaside, Bennington Heights, Thorncliffe Park, Flemingdon Park (part), Lawrence Park (part, east of Yonge), the Bridle Path, and Wanless Park — a wide-ranging ward from dense high-rise apartment towers (Thorncliffe Park) to some of Toronto's most expensive low-rise streets. Its biggest recent civic change is transit: the Eglinton Crosstown (Line 5) opened in February 2026, serving Leaside with three stations (including Laird), after a century in which the area's rail connections were mostly industrial. The seat itself changed hands mid-term: councillor Jaye Robinson died in office and Rachel Chernos Lin won the November 2024 by-election to succeed her — background already carried on this ward's data card.

Geography & boundaries

Legally defined (Elections Ontario boundary text, per Wikipedia — identical description to Ward 14's northern/ western portion, reflecting the two wards sharing much of their boundary text): commences at Highway 401 & Leslie Street, south along Leslie to Eglinton Avenue East, west along Eglinton to the Don River West Branch, southeasterly along the branch to Overlea Boulevard, east to Don Mills Road, south to the Don River East Branch, southwesterly along the branch and the Don River to Pottery Road, along Pottery Road to Bayview Avenue, north/ northwest along Bayview to the CPR line, southwest along the railway to the Beltline Trail in Moore Park Ravine, northwesterly to Mount Pleasant Cemetery's southerly boundary, west along that boundary to Mount Pleasant Road, north to Broadway Avenue, west to Yonge Street, north to Highway 401, then back to the point of commencement. In plain terms (per the same source, describing the ward's pre-2025-federal-redistribution boundaries, which mirrored the municipal ward): west border Mount Pleasant Road/Yonge Street, north border Highway 401, east border Leslie Street/the Don River/part of Don Mills Road, south border the Don River/Beltline Trail. Named areas commonly associated with the ward: Leaside, Bennington Heights, Thorncliffe Park, the Bridle Path, Wanless Park, and part of Lawrence Park. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_15_Don_Valley_West, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leaside, accessed 2026-08-14]

Civic texture — what’s live here

  • Eglinton Crosstown (Line 5) opening. The Eglinton Crosstown LRT opened in February 2026, serving Leaside with three stations, per the Leaside neighbourhood's own Wikipedia history section — a return of major transit investment to an area whose passenger rail service (Leaside Station) had ended in 1970. Laird station, one of the three, sits at Laird Drive & Eglinton Avenue and was built by sequential excavation mining (one of only three Line 5 stations built that way). [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leaside, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laird_station, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Active City capital works, 2026. The City's own Ward 15 profile page lists a substantial set of live/recent construction notices — a long-running Basement Flooding Protection Program on Bennington Heights Drive and surrounding local roads (multiple construction-impact notices and temporary closures through mid-2025); The Bridle Path road reconstruction/sidewalk/sewer/watermain project (construction updates into mid-2025); watermain and road work on Bayview Avenue/Lawrence Avenue East, Leslie Street/Vanderhoof/Research Road, and Yonge Street (storm sewer, with updates into April/May 2026); and continuing work on the North Toronto Treatment Plant outfall/Don River watercourse restoration. A general 2026 local road-resurfacing notice for the ward was issued in March 2026. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-15-don-valley-west/, accessed 2026-08-14, page dated "modified May 15, 2026"]

Transit & development

TTC: the ward is now served by Line 5 Eglinton (Laird and two further stations, per §3) along its Eglinton Avenue corridor, in addition to bus routes; exact station-to-ward assignment for all three Line 5 stops was not individually verified against the boundary polygon — named as general context, not a formal join. Major transit project: the February 2026 Eglinton Crosstown opening (§3) is the largest transit change affecting this ward in decades. What else is on your ballot

Every Toronto voter marks three races on October 26, 2026: this ward’s councillor (above), the citywide mayor, and one school trustee.

  • Mayor (citywide — the same race for every ward) — 42 candidates registered as of 2026-08-14, identical treatment: the mayoral race card.
  • School trustee — one seat, for whichever of Toronto’s four school boards matches your school-support designation. Trustee-district boundaries do not in general line up with the 25 council wards; here is what this project has established for THIS ward, tiered by how confidently each is sourced:
    • Conseil scolaire Viamonde (French public) — Toronto-Est (Secteur 2, Annexe NORD-EST) (race card) — confirmed exact from the board’s own official regulatory determination document (Détermination et répartition des conseillers scolaires, Conseil scolaire Viamonde, March 2026, Annexe C).
    • TCDSB — narrowed to one of two TCDSB wards (TCDSB Ward 3 or TCDSB Ward 5), not resolved further by any source found — not presented as settled; see this ward’s precision note below.
    • CSC MonAvenir (French Catholic) — this ward falls in one of MonAvenir’s two Toronto régions (Toronto-Est or Toronto-Ouest); the board has not published a ward-level boundary document, so which région applies here is not resolved — a region-level statement, not a guess at which one.
    • TDSB — not established for this ward: no reliable source pairs it to a specific TDSB ward (a named gap, not a guess).
    39 TDSB and 21 TCDSB trustee candidates are registered citywide as of 2026-08-14. Whatever the tiers above show, MyVote is the authoritative resolver for your own address — check it before you vote: MyVote.

Your ward, in one minute

Ward 13 — Toronto Centre is home to about 116,930 people (2021 Census), with a median household income of $65,000 a year (2020 reference year) and a 70.0% / 30.0% renter/owner household split. Chris Moise won the 2022 council race here by 30.2 percentage points — a landslide, not a close call. As of 2026-08-05, 8 candidate(s) are registered to run here in 2026.

The 2022 receipt

Result: Chris Moise won by 30.2 percentage points over the runner-up. Election day is Monday, October 26, 2026, 10 a.m.–8 p.m.; advance voting runs October 6–11, 2026, 10 a.m.–7 p.m. daily. You vote in the ward where you live. Check or update your registration, and find your voting place once it is published, at MyVote.

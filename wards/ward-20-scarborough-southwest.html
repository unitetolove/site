Ward 20 — Scarborough Southwest

Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

Scarborough Southwest hugs Lake Ontario and the Scarborough Bluffs in Toronto's east end — Birch Cliff, Cliffside, Clairlea, Oakridge, Scarborough Junction, and the western half of Scarborough Village. It's a largely residential ward stitched together by the Kingston Road and Danforth Road corridors, where a wave of mid-rise and high-rise rezonings is now moving through city planning, with a subway station (Warden), two GO stations, and the western edge of the Eglinton Crosstown LRT's Golden Mile redevelopment area anchoring its transit geography.

This is separate from the Ward 20 council race below: different office (Member of Provincial Parliament, not city councillor), different ballot, different day. The Sept 3, 2026 provincial by-election here

Doly Begum, the NDP MPP who held the seat since 2018, resigned Feb 3, 2026 to run federally. Premier Doug Ford called the by-election on Aug 5, 2026 for Thursday, September 3, 2026. This is a provincial race — the office is Member of Provincial Parliament for Scarborough Southwest, run under Elections Ontario’s rules, not the City of Toronto’s.

How this riding relates to the ward — and a common mix-up to avoid. The *provincial* riding of Scarborough Southwest and the City of Toronto’s Ward 20 have shared the same boundaries since 2018, per Wikipedia’s sourced riding history — unlike the Beaches–East York/Ward 19 pair, where the federal riding and the ward are close but not identical. One thing worth flagging plainly: there is *also* a **federal** riding called “Scarborough Southwest” (currently held by Liberal MP Doly Begum, who won it in an April 2026 federal by-election), and its boundaries changed in the 2023 federal redistribution — so the federal riding, the provincial riding, and the ward all carry the same or similar names but are not guaranteed to be the same map. This by-election is for the **provincial** seat only. Confirm your own boundary before voting at Elections Ontario’s Voter Information Service.

Geography & boundaries

Legally defined (Elections Ontario boundary text, reproduced on Wikipedia): commencing at Eglinton Avenue East and Markham Road; south along Markham Road and its southerly production to the city's southerly limit; southwesterly along that limit to the southerly production of Victoria Park Avenue; north along Victoria Park Avenue to Eglinton Avenue East; east along Eglinton Avenue East back to the point of commencement. In plain terms: Eglinton Avenue East to the north, Markham Road to the east, Lake Ontario to the south, Victoria Park Avenue to the west. Named neighbourhoods, per the same source: Birch Cliff, Birch Cliff Heights, Clairlea, Cliffcrest, Cliffside, Fallingbrook, Hunt Club, Oakridge, Scarborough Junction, and the western half of Scarborough Village. Ward 20 was created in the 2018 ward redistribution and has been coterminous with the province's Scarborough Southwest electoral district since then. Civic texture — what’s live here

  • 468 to 480 Danforth Road and 509 Birchmount Road — zoning approval, June 2026. City Council adopted a Zoning By-law Amendment on June 24-25, 2026 permitting a 14-storey mixed-use building (526 residential units, 1,563 m² of ground-floor retail) at the site of a closed Sun Valley grocery store, plus a single-detached dwelling at 509 Birchmount Road. The approval includes a 754 m² parkland dedication expanding the adjacent Sadler Parkette. Community consultation (April 2023) raised loss of the grocery store, construction impacts, and traffic/congestion concerns. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2026/sc/bgrd/backgroundfile-286852.pdf (Decision Report), accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://civicdashboard.ca/actions/item/2026.SC32.2, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • 3130 Danforth Avenue — zoning approval, May 2026. City Council adopted a separate Zoning By-law Amendment on May 20-21, 2026 (Ward 20 — Scarborough Southwest, per the official item record) permitting a 33-storey mixed-use tower with a five-storey podium, replacing an existing low-rise commercial building. Staff's report describes the site as within a Protected Major Transit Station Area. [Source: https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2026.SC31.5, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Golden Mile Secondary Plan. Covers Eglinton Avenue East between Victoria Park Avenue and Birchmount Road (within Ward 20's boundary), roughly 280 acres. City planning staff describe it as a near-total redevelopment of existing auto-oriented retail/industrial land, anticipating up to 48-storey towers on the largest sites and close to 38,000 new residential units over as much as 30 years, catalyzed by five planned Eglinton Crosstown LRT stops through the area; as of this reporting, 10+ major sites (Choice Properties/Daniels at the former Golden Mile Shopping Centre, Starlight, SmartCentres, Madison Group, Cosmetica Laboratories, RioCan, Dream, and others) had active applications. [Source: https://canada.constructconnect.com/dcn/news/projects/2024/01/whats-to-become-of-scarboroughs-golden-mile, accessed 2026-08-14, article dated January 2024 — treat the specific project list/figures as of that reporting, not independently re-verified against a live 2026 application count]
  • Active City capital works, 2025-2026. The City's own Ward 20 profile page lists an extensive slate of recent and ongoing construction: Basement Flooding Protection Program sanitary/storm sewer upgrades on Birchmount Road, Danforth Avenue and other local roads, and separately on Kennedy Road, Midland Avenue and other local roads (notices running from May 2025 into mid-2026); watermain replacement and road resurfacing on Cathedral Bluffs Drive, on Eglinton Avenue East/Kingston Road/Scarborough Golf Club Road/Belmuir Place/Bellamy Road South/Adanac Drive (deferred as of May 2026), and on Victoria Park Avenue; sidewalk/pedestrian improvements on Fishleigh Drive and Kitson Drive; a sanitary forcemain replacement on Fallingbrook Crescent and Fallingbrook Drive; culvert rehabilitation on St. Clair Avenue East over Massey Creek; and sediment removal/maintenance at the Bell Estate Stormwater Management Pond in Oates Park. A citywide 2026 Capital Works local-road-resurfacing program also lists streets in the ward (pre-construction notice dated March 3, 2026). [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-20-scarborough-southwest/, accessed 2026-08-14, page itself dated "modified July 29, 2026"]

Transit & development

Subway: TTC Line 2 (Bloor-Danforth) runs through the ward's northern section; Warden Station (701 Warden Avenue, at the southeast corner of St. Clair Avenue East and Warden Avenue) sits within the ward's interior, well south of the Eglinton Avenue East boundary. Kennedy Station, the line's current eastern terminus, is located at Eglinton Avenue East and Kennedy Road — directly on the ward's northern boundary line; whether the station falls on the Ward 20 or Ward 21 (Scarborough Centre) side was not independently verified against the boundary polygon (flagged, not asserted with precision — see §8). GO: the Lakeshore East line serves Scarborough GO Station (3615 St. Clair Avenue East, near Danforth Road, in Scarborough Junction) and Eglinton GO Station (2995 Eglinton Avenue East); the latter also sits on the Eglinton Avenue East boundary line and its ward side was not independently verified. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warden_station, accessed 2026-08-14] Buses: multiple TTC routes cross the ward along Kingston Road (12 Kingston Rd), Danforth Road, Birchmount Road (17 Birchmount), Warden Avenue (68/69 Warden), and Eglinton Avenue East, several converging at Warden Station. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warden_station, accessed 2026-08-14] Eglinton Crosstown LRT (Line 5): secondary-plan literature treats the line as the catalyst for Golden Mile redevelopment (§3); this research could not independently confirm current in-service status for the Golden Mile/Eglinton East segment (a direct TTC page fetch returned no usable content) — flagged as a gap, see §8. Development: intensification is concentrated along the Danforth Road/Kingston Road corridor — recent examples include Merge Condos (12 storeys, 180 units, completed 2023, at Kingston Road and Danforth Avenue) and the Manderley Condominiums (12 storeys, 198 units, under construction at Kingston Road and Manderley Drive in Birch Cliff) — and at the ward's northwest corner inside the Golden Mile Secondary Plan/Protected Major Transit Station Area (3130 Danforth Avenue, 33 storeys, approved 2026; §3). A November 2023 UrbanToronto ward-level report counted 66 pre-construction, 12 under-construction and 11 recently completed residential projects ward-wide (23,130 total dwellings), describing much of the pre-construction pipeline as a response to the anticipated Crosstown LRT; treat as historical/dated context pending a fresh pull, not a current count. [Source: https://urbantoronto.ca/news/2023/11/utpro-instant-reports-ward-20-scarborough-southwest-seeing-major-boost-developments.54636, accessed 2026-08-14, dated November 2023]

What else is on your ballot

Every Toronto voter marks three races on October 26, 2026: this ward’s councillor (above), the citywide mayor, and one school trustee.

  • Mayor (citywide — the same race for every ward) — 42 candidates registered as of 2026-08-14, identical treatment: the mayoral race card.
  • School trustee — one seat, for whichever of Toronto’s four school boards matches your school-support designation. Trustee-district boundaries do not in general line up with the 25 council wards; here is what this project has established for THIS ward, tiered by how confidently each is sourced:
    • Conseil scolaire Viamonde (French public) — Toronto-Est (Secteur 2, Annexe NORD-EST) (race card) — confirmed exact from the board’s own official regulatory determination document (Détermination et répartition des conseillers scolaires, Conseil scolaire Viamonde, March 2026, Annexe C).
    • TCDSB — narrowed to one of two TCDSB wards (TCDSB Ward 8 or TCDSB Ward 12), not resolved further by any source found — not presented as settled; see this ward’s precision note below.
    • CSC MonAvenir (French Catholic) — this ward falls in one of MonAvenir’s two Toronto régions (Toronto-Est or Toronto-Ouest); the board has not published a ward-level boundary document, so which région applies here is not resolved — a region-level statement, not a guess at which one.
    • Toronto District School Board (TDSB) — local reporting places this ward in TDSB Ward 10 — not yet verified against an official board document (race card); see this ward’s precision note below.
    39 TDSB and 21 TCDSB trustee candidates are registered citywide as of 2026-08-14. Whatever the tiers above show, MyVote is the authoritative resolver for your own address — check it before you vote: MyVote.

  • The November 2023 UrbanToronto ward development count (§4) is a point-in-time figure, not re-verified against a current pipeline; no 2026 comprehensive private-development-application count was pulled for this ward — a full pipeline would need the City's Application Information Centre, not attempted.
  • Birchmount Community Centre's address/history rely on the operating charity's own site and a 2018 community-news article, not an independently fetched primary toronto.ca facility page (that page rendered no static content via fetch) — flagged in §5.
  • This session's WebSearch tool allotment (200 calls) was exhausted partway through this brief's research (after the transit/development and early civic-texture searches were already complete). All subsequent research relied on targeted web_fetch calls against sources already identified via search or cross-reference, not additional open search — meaning some adjacent topics (e.g. an independent check on the Golden Mile primary document, a broader media-landscape sweep, a park-by-park inventory) that a full search budget might have surfaced were not pursued. No internet outage was encountered; this is a tool-budget constraint, not a connectivity gap.
  • Trustee-ward crosswalk not attempted here — already a named citywide gap (22 of 25 wards, per `profiles/`), out of this brief's scope.
  • A full address-to-trustee-district crosswalk is not complete for every board (school board trustee races do not share the 25-ward boundary map): for this ward, TDSB carries a news-reported mapping, not yet verified against an official board document and TCDSB is narrowed to one of two TCDSB wards, not resolved further; CSC MonAvenir is resolved only to a region (Toronto-Est or Toronto-Ouest), never a ward, for every ward citywide. Conseil scolaire Viamonde is the one board fully resolved, exact, for every ward. See the tiered breakdown and this ward’s precision note in “What else is on your ballot” above.
  • Libraries and community/recreation centres are now geospatially joined to this ward’s boundary (see “What’s live here, by the numbers” under Local commons above — complete citywide datasets for both layers). Parks joining is partial citywide; not shown rather than shown unevenly (400 of 1,511 City-classified park-type rows fetched and joined so far).

Ward 20 — Scarborough Southwest

Your ward, in one minute

Ward 20 — Scarborough Southwest is home to about 110,095 people (2021 Census), with a median household income of $79,000 a year (2020 reference year) and a 45.7% / 54.3% renter/owner household split. Parthi Kandavel won the 2022 council race here by 5.5 percentage points — a real contest, not a landslide. As of 2026-08-05, 7 candidate(s) are registered to run here in 2026.

How to vote here

Election day is Monday, October 26, 2026, 10 a.m.–8 p.m.; advance voting runs October 6–11, 2026, 10 a.m.–7 p.m. daily. You vote in the ward where you live. Check or update your registration, and find your voting place once it is published, at MyVote.

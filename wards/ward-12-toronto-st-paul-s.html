Ward 12 — Toronto-St. Paul’s

Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

Toronto-St. Paul's is a largely residential midtown ward stretching from Forest Hill and Chaplin Estates through the Yonge-Eglinton area to Humewood-Cedarvale, Wychwood Park, Hillcrest, and Fairbank. Its biggest recent civic change is transit: the Eglinton Crosstown (Line 5) opened February 8, 2026, with a Forest Hill station at Bathurst and Eglinton, already drawing new transit-oriented development to that corner.

Geography & boundaries

Legally defined (per Wikipedia, reproducing the Elections Ontario boundary text — partial, the full description continues past what search results captured): commences at Eglinton Avenue West & Dufferin Street, south along Dufferin to Rogers Road, east along Rogers Road to Oakwood Avenue, south along Oakwood to Holland Park Avenue, east along Holland Park to Winona Drive, generally south along Winona to Davenport Road, west along Davenport to Ossington Avenue, south along Ossington to the CPR line, generally east along the railway to Yonge Street, north along Yonge to Jackes Avenue, east along Jackes to the Rosehill Reservoir's westerly boundary, north along that boundary to Rosehill Avenue, then continuing east (full remainder of the legal description not captured — see source for the complete text). Named areas commonly associated with the ward, per search results: Forest Hill (part), Chaplin Estates, Yonge-Eglinton (part), Humewood-Cedarvale, Wychwood Park, Hillcrest- Bracondale, Fairbank (part), Oakwood Village (part). [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_12_Toronto%E2%80%94St._Paul's, accessed 2026-08-14]

Civic texture — what’s live here

  • Eglinton Crosstown (Line 5) opening and Forest Hill station. The Eglinton Crosstown LRT opened February 8, 2026, including an underground Forest Hill station at 842 Eglinton Ave. W. (at Bathurst Street). The new station has already drawn transit-oriented development to that corner, including a 14-storey condo (completed 2024, ahead of the line's opening) built with a lobby directly connected to the station. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_Hill_station_(Toronto), accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://www.reminetwork.com/articles/forest-hill-condo-promises-direct-lrt-access/, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Active City capital works, 2026. The City's own Ward 12 profile page lists a large, sustained set of live or recent construction notices — the multi-phase Basement Flooding Protection Program for the Fairbank- Silverthorn storm system (storm tunnel and local collector sewers, with construction notices spanning Blandford/Glenholme, Keywest/Northcliffe/Allenvale, Oakwood Avenue, and Westmount Avenue through 2025); watermain replacement and road resurfacing on Balmoral Avenue, Dunloe Road, Duplex/Berwick Avenue, Oriole Parkway, Penrose Road, and Yonge Street; road reconstruction on Clovelly Avenue, Gibson Street, and Rathnelly Avenue; and a Boulton Drive transmission-watermain abandonment through Roycroft Park. A general 2026 local road-resurfacing notice for the ward was issued in March 2026. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-12-toronto-st-pauls/, accessed 2026-08-14, page dated "modified June 9, 2026"]

Transit & development

TTC: the ward is served by Line 1 (Yonge-University) at Eglinton and (per boundary) potentially St. Clair-area stations along its eastern Yonge Street edge, and now Line 5 (Eglinton Crosstown) at Forest Hill and Eglinton West/Cedarvale stations along Eglinton Avenue West; exact station-to-ward assignment not independently verified against the boundary polygon — named as general context, not a formal join. Major transit project: the Eglinton Crosstown's February 2026 opening (§3) is the single largest transit change affecting this ward. Development: the clearest transit-oriented development signal found this research is the Forest Hill/Bathurst- Eglinton condo activity tied to the new LRT station (§3); broader ward-wide development-application data was not pulled. [Source: https://www.metrolinx.com/en/projects-and-programs/eglinton-crosstown-lrt, accessed 2026-08-14]

What else is on your ballot

Every Toronto voter marks three races on October 26, 2026: this ward’s councillor (above), the citywide mayor, and one school trustee.

  • Mayor (citywide — the same race for every ward) — 42 candidates registered as of 2026-08-14, identical treatment: the mayoral race card.
  • School trustee — one seat, for whichever of Toronto’s four school boards matches your school-support designation. Trustee-district boundaries do not in general line up with the 25 council wards; here is what this project has established for THIS ward, tiered by how confidently each is sourced:
    • Conseil scolaire Viamonde (French public) — Toronto-Centre (Secteur 2, Annexe CENTRAL) (race card) — confirmed exact from the board’s own official regulatory determination document (Détermination et répartition des conseillers scolaires, Conseil scolaire Viamonde, March 2026, Annexe C).
    • TCDSB — not established for this ward: no reliable source pairs it to a specific TCDSB ward (a named gap, not a guess).
    • CSC MonAvenir (French Catholic) — this ward falls in one of MonAvenir’s two Toronto régions (Toronto-Est or Toronto-Ouest); the board has not published a ward-level boundary document, so which région applies here is not resolved — a region-level statement, not a guess at which one.
    • Toronto District School Board (TDSB) — local reporting places this ward in TDSB Ward 7 — not yet verified against an official board document (race card); see this ward’s precision note below.
    39 TDSB and 21 TCDSB trustee candidates are registered citywide as of 2026-08-14. Whatever the tiers above show, MyVote is the authoritative resolver for your own address — check it before you vote: MyVote.

This ward’s trustee-crosswalk precision note: TDSB confirmed (CP24/CTV News, same citation as ward 11). TCDSB: NOT ESTABLISHED. Viamonde exact. MonAvenir not disambiguated.

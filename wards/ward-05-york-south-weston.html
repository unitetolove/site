Ward 05 — York South-Weston

Your ward, in one minute

Ward 5 — York South-Weston is home to about 115,675 people (2021 Census), with a median household income of $72,000 a year (2020 reference year) and a 51.9% / 48.1% renter/owner household split. Frances Nunziata won the 2022 council race here by 0.4 percentage points — razor-tight — about as close as a Toronto council race gets. As of 2026-08-05, 6 candidate(s) are registered to run here in 2026.

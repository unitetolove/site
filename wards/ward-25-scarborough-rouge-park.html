Ward 25 — Scarborough-Rouge Park

Profile vintage 2026-08-06. [NO WARD-LEVEL DATA] appears wherever that is the honest state, never filled in by guessing. Every figure below cites its own dataset and vintage.

Your ward, in one minute

Ward 25 — Scarborough-Rouge Park is home to about 101,485 people (2021 Census), with a median household income of $105,000 a year (2020 reference year) and a 20.6% / 79.4% renter/owner household split. Neethan Shan won the 2022 council race here by 54.7 percentage points — a landslide, not a close call. As of 2026-08-05, 6 candidate(s) are registered to run here in 2026.

How to vote here

Election day is Monday, October 26, 2026, 10 a.m.–8 p.m.; advance voting runs October 6–11, 2026, 10 a.m.–7 p.m. daily. You vote in the ward where you live. Check or update your registration, and find your voting place once it is published, at MyVote.

Scarborough-Guildwood runs along Lake Ontario in southeast Scarborough, bounded by Highway 401 to the north, Morningside Avenue to the east, a stepped line along Markham Road/Bellamy Road/McCowan Road to the west, and the lake to the south — it takes in Guildwood, West Hill, Morningside, Woburn, and Scarborough Village. Its anchor point is the Guildwood GO station on Kingston Road, also a Via Rail stop, now the subject of a provincially led plan to build as many as six towers (up to 60 storeys) on the station's north parking lot — one of several mid- and high-rise proposals currently moving through Scarborough Community Council for this ward. The ward is also home to Guild Park and Gardens, a 50-acre heritage park on the Scarborough Bluffs built around a collection of architectural fragments salvaged from demolished downtown Toronto buildings.

Geography & boundaries

Legally defined (Elections Ontario boundary text, reproduced on Wikipedia): commencing at the intersection of Highway No. 401 with Morningside Avenue; thence southerly along said avenue to the city's southerly limit; thence southwesterly along that limit to the southerly production of Markham Road; thence northerly along Markham Road to Eglinton Avenue East; thence westerly along Eglinton to the southerly production of Bellamy Road North; thence northerly along Bellamy Road North to Lawrence Avenue East; thence westerly along Lawrence to McCowan Road; thence northerly along McCowan Road to Highway No. 401; thence easterly along the highway back to the point of commencement. In plain terms: Lake Ontario to the south, Highway 401 to the north, Morningside Avenue on the east, and a stepped western edge that runs Markham Road (lake to Eglinton), then jogs west to Bellamy Road North (Eglinton to Lawrence), then jogs west again to McCowan Road (Lawrence to Highway 401). Community council: Scarborough Community Council. Named neighbourhoods commonly associated with the ward, per the sources below: Guildwood, West Hill, Morningside, Woburn, and Scarborough Village. Civic texture — what’s live here

  • Guildwood GO Transit-Oriented Community (TOC) proposal. Infrastructure Ontario and Metrolinx have identified the Guildwood GO Station area as a site for a provincially led Transit-Oriented Community. Per the Guildwood Village Community Association's (GVCA) summary of the proposal, the plans call for roughly 2,500 housing units across six towers on the station's north parking lot (two at 60 storeys, one at 40, one at 35, and two at 30), with the GVCA's own posted response describing the scale as "extreme density" that would be "significantly out of character for this community" — that characterization is the community association's own, not independently verified against the underlying application by this brief (the official Metrolinx/Infrastructure Ontario project page could not be located/fetched, see §8). [Source: https://www.guildwood.ca/guildwood-toc, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Guildwood Village Neighbourhood Character Guidelines, adopted 2026. City Planning prepared, and City Council officially adopted, a set of neighbourhood character guidelines for Guildwood Village (mid-20th-century low-rise character: horizontal forms, broad low-pitched roofs, generous setbacks, mature tree canopy) — per the GVCA's own news post, adoption followed "months of community engagement and advocacy." [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/968d-city-planning-urban-design-guildwood-village-neighbourhood-character-guidelines.pdf, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://www.guildwood.ca/post/guildwood-neighbourhood-character-guidelines-officially-adopted, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • 3718 and 3730 Kingston Road — Zoning By-law Amendment. An application to permit a 24-storey mixed-use building with a 7-storey podium (419 residential units, 30,905 m² total gross floor area) was withdrawn from the Scarborough Community Council agenda on February 19, 2026, because public notice of the statutory public meeting was incomplete; no further action had been taken on it as of August 14, 2026. [Source: https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2026.SC29.1, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • 1215 to 1255 McCowan Road — Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment, refusal recommended. A May 11, 2026 staff report recommends refusal of an application for three residential towers (25, 45, and 55 storeys; 1,412 dwelling units; 117,913 m² total gross floor area; FSI 6.42) on grounds the proposal is inconsistent with the Provincial Planning Statement 2024, does not conform with the Official Plan, and fails to meet Scarborough Centre Secondary Plan policies, including a lack of on-site parkland dedication and public streets; staff recommend the City Solicitor be authorized to defend the refusal at the Ontario Land Tribunal. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2026/sc/bgrd/backgroundfile-286872.pdf, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • 1710 and 1712 Ellesmere Road — Official Plan Amendment, Zoning By-law Amendment, Draft Plan of Subdivision — approved. City Council adopted (May 20–21, 2026) an application for five towers (29 to 55 storeys; 2,328 dwelling units; 169,273 m² total gross floor area; FSI ~8.98), including an on-site parkland dedication of at least 1,613 m² split into two blocks to form one contiguous public park, and a new east-west public street extending through adjacent TTC McCowan Yard lands toward a future connection to Progress Avenue. [Source: https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2026.SC31.3, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • 60 Guildwood Parkway — Official Plan and Zoning By-law Amendment — approval recommended. A staff report recommends approval of a 9-storey long-term care facility redevelopment (512 resident rooms, 29,848 m² total gross floor area, FSI 0.98), redesignating the site from *Neighbourhoods* to *Institutional Areas*; at Scarborough Community Council on July 9, 2026 the vote to adopt the item was a 3–3 tie (lost). [Source: https://civicdashboard.ca/actions/item/2026.SC33.12, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Capital infrastructure works, 2024–2026 (representative, not exhaustive). The City's own Ward 24 profile page lists active or recently completed capital works including: Blakemanor Boulevard culvert replacement over West Highland Creek (notices May–July 2024); Centre Street/Luella Street watermain cleaning and relining (Feb–June 2025); Eglinton Avenue East/Kingston Road/Scarborough Golf Club Road/Bellamy Road South/Adanac Drive watermain replacement and road resurfacing (pre-construction notice December 2025, deferred May 2026); Highland Creek storm sewer outfall repair (updates 2020–2024); Scarborough Golf Club Road watermain replacement and road reconstruction (November 2024–October 2025, multiple update notices); West Highland Creek/Morningside Park storm sewer outfall rehabilitation (October 2024–November 2025); and a 2026 Capital Works Program local road resurfacing package (pre-construction notice dated March 3, 2026). [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-24-scarborough-guildwood/, accessed 2026-08-14, page itself dated "modified May 25, 2026"]

Transit & development

GO/Via: Guildwood GO Station, on Kingston Road in the Guildwood neighbourhood (CN Kingston Subdivision), is a stop on GO Transit's Lakeshore East line and also serves intercity Via Rail Corridor trains to Ottawa and Montreal. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guildwood_GO_Station, accessed 2026-08-14] TTC bus: Per the same source, the station is served by the 86 Scarborough bus and its branches (Kennedy Station to Toronto Zoo), the 12D Kingston Road rush-hour-only local route (Victoria Park Station to University of Toronto Scarborough), the 905 Eglinton East Express (to UTSC), the 986 Scarborough Express (to Meadowvale Loop), and, Tuesday–Thursday middays, the 406 Scarborough-Guildwood community bus (northbound to Scarborough Centre Station). No subway line runs through the ward's interior; the nearest Line 2 Bloor-Danforth stations (Kennedy, Scarborough Centre) sit outside the ward boundary to the west/north. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guildwood_GO_Station, accessed 2026-08-14] Scarborough East Rapid Transit (SERT, future Line 7, formerly branded Eglinton East LRT): A City/TTC-planned 18.6 km higher-order transit line proposed to run from Kennedy Station to Malvern Town Centre via the University of Toronto Scarborough campus, with 27 stops and five interchanges; the City's own page describes it as bringing transit and public- realm improvements to "historically underserved areas of Scarborough, including eight Neighbourhood Improvement Areas." [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/streets-parking-transportation/transit-in-toronto/transit-expansion/scarborough-east-rapid-transit/, accessed 2026-08-14] As of April 15, 2026, Ontario's Transportation Minister said the project is "not currently" on the province's list of priorities, even as Toronto's mayor's executive committee passed a motion the same week calling on the federal and provincial governments to fund a third of its cost each; a 2023 city staff report had pegged the project's cost at $4.65 billion. [Source: https://www.cp24.com/local/toronto/2026/04/15/province-says-eglinton-east-lrt-not-currently-on-priority-list/, accessed 2026-08-14] This brief did not confirm a precise SERT alignment through Ward 24's interior — the project's Kennedy-to- Malvern corridor runs near/through the Eglinton Avenue East and Kingston Road corridors that border or cross the ward, but a stop-by-stop, in-ward confirmation was not attempted (see §8). Development: Beyond the Guildwood GO TOC proposal (§3), the ward currently has at least four active or recently decided mid/high-rise zoning files working through Scarborough Community Council — 3718/3730 Kingston Road, 1215–1255 McCowan Road, 1710/1712 Ellesmere Road, and 60 Guildwood Parkway (§3) — indicating a live pipeline of intensification proposals along the Kingston Road/Eglinton Avenue East corridor and near the Guildwood GO/McCowan Yard lands, alongside a newly adopted neighbourhood character-guidelines document specific to Guildwood Village (§3).

Local commons

  • Guild Park and Gardens (201 Guildwood Parkway) — a 50-acre public park on the Scarborough Bluffs, owned by the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority and operated by Toronto Parks, Forestry & Recreation. It is the former site of the Guild Inn artists' colony and holds a collection of architectural fragments (columns, carvings, panels) salvaged from more than 60 demolished Toronto-area buildings, an outdoor "Greek Theatre" built from Bank of Toronto building remnants (home to Guild Festival Theatre since 2011), and the 19th-century Osterhout Log Cabin.

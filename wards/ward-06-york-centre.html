Ward 06 — York Centre

Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

York Centre sits in Toronto's north end, in the former City of North York, roughly bounded by Steeles Avenue West, Bathurst Street, Highway 401, and Jane/Keele Streets — a largely residential ward built out through the postwar Bathurst Manor and Clanton Park subdivisions, with two Line 1 subway stations (Wilson and Sheppard West) at its core. Its biggest current civic story is the former Downsview Airport: closed to aviation in 2024 after decades as a Bombardier aircraft plant, the 370-acre site (now branded "YZD" by owner Northcrest Developments) is planned as the largest urban redevelopment in North America, with more than 35,000 homes envisioned over roughly 30 years. Part of those same lands opened in 2025 as Rogers Stadium, a large outdoor concert venue whose traffic and noise impacts are an active file for the ward's councillor's office. The ward is also home to Earl Bales Park's ski hill and Holocaust memorial, and to a significant concentration of Jewish community institutions along the Bathurst Street corridor.

Geography & boundaries

  • Location & formation. York Centre sits in Toronto's north-central area, in the former City of North York. The ward was first contested in the 2018 municipal election as an amalgamation of the pre-2018 Ward 9 and Ward 10; despite its name, it is administered under the North York Community Council, not the former City of York. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_6_York_Centre, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Legal boundary description (per the ward's Elections Ontario legal description, reproduced on Wikipedia): commencing at the intersection of the city's northerly limit with Keele Street; south along Keele to Grandravine Drive; west along Grandravine to Black Creek; southeast along Black Creek to Sheppard Avenue West; west along Sheppard to Jane Street; south along Jane to Highway 401; east/northeast along the 401 to the Don River West Branch; northwest along the Don River West Branch to Bathurst Street; north along Bathurst to the city's northerly limit (Steeles Avenue West); west along that limit back to the starting point. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_6_York_Centre, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Plain-language summary, consistent with the above: the ward is roughly bounded by Steeles Avenue West (north/city limit), Bathurst Street (east), Highway 401 (south), and Jane Street/Keele Street (west), with a Black Creek/Grandravine Drive notch on the northwest side. [Source: https://thelocal.to/ward-6-york-centre/, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/8543-CityPlanning-Map-Ward-6.pdf, accessed 2026-08-14 (City Planning ward map)]
  • Neighbourhoods named by cited sources. Wikipedia's neighbourhood-level articles place Clanton Park (also called Dublin Heights or Wilson Heights) and Bathurst Manor within the ward — Clanton Park is bounded by Sheppard Avenue West (north), Bathurst Street (east), Highway 401 (south), and the former Downsview Airport lands/Allen Road (west); Bathurst Manor sits on the plateau immediately north, bounded by Finch Avenue West (north), Dufferin Street (west), the Don River West Branch (east), and Sheppard Avenue West (south). [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clanton_Park,_Toronto, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bathurst_Manor, accessed 2026-08-14] The ward councillor's constituency office groups its community-facing content under four named areas — Bathurst Manor, Clanton Park, Downsview, and Stanley Greene — consistent with the above. [Source: https://jamespasternak.ca/free-workshops-for-children-and-adults-downsview-library/, accessed 2026-08-14 (site navigation)] A "Yorkdale-Glen Park" association surfaced in preliminary search results but was not confirmed against a citable ward-boundary source in this pass and is not asserted here — per the plan's standing rule, no formal neighbourhood-to-ward polygon mapping is claimed beyond what a source itself states.

Civic texture — what’s live here

  • The former Downsview Airport lands (now "YZD") — the ward's largest live planning file. Downsview Airport (IATA: YZD), long used by Bombardier Aerospace to build the Dash 8/Q400 and Global Express aircraft, ceased industrial/airport operations in 2023-24; NAV Canada issued the formal closure notice (NOTAM) on March 25, 2024, and the airport closed in April 2024. Owner Northcrest Developments (a subsidiary of the Public Sector Pension Investment Board) unveiled a new site identity, "YZD," on August 17, 2024. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downsview_Airport, accessed 2026-08-14] Northcrest's own site (accessed 2026-08-14) describes YZD as "the largest urban redevelopment in North America" on the 370-acre former airport site: more than 35,000 new homes and more than 20,000 jobs envisioned over roughly 30 years, organized into seven planned neighbourhoods anchored by a 2 km pedestrianized "Runway" through the site's former airstrip. [Source: https://www.northcrestdev.ca/, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • City planning status. The City's "Update Downsview" study produced a Downsview Secondary Plan, Community Development Plan, Urban Design Guidelines, a zoning by-law for "meanwhile" (interim) uses, and a Master Environmental Servicing Plan, adopted by Council in 2024. The Secondary Plan has been in force since August 9, 2024, except for lands south of Wilson Avenue, which remain subject to an Ontario Land Tribunal appeal (OLT-24-000829). As of this pass, live work includes district plans and development applications, a Major Streets Environmental Assessment, and new-park/ community-recreation-centre design; a Wilson District community meeting (Ancaster Community Centre) is referenced on the page's most recent update. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/planning-studies-initiatives/update-downsview/, accessed 2026-08-14, page dated "modified" 2026-07-10]
  • Rogers Stadium — new outdoor concert venue, live neighbourhood-impact issue. A large outdoor concert venue, Rogers Stadium, opened in 2025 at 105 Carl Hall Road on part of the former Downsview Airport lands, which carry Commercial-Residential zoning permitting a temporary up-to-three-storey music venue outside the normal council-approval process. Per the ward councillor's own office, the venue is operated by Live Nation under an agreement with Northcrest Developments running up to five years, and the office states it was not consulted on the building application or review process. The office's public FAQ describes ongoing work with Live Nation, Northcrest, and City divisions on noise, traffic, and street-closure management, including a City-led working group with Toronto Police Service and Transportation Services, a published traffic-management plan, and a stated 11 p.m. concert-end limit consistent with existing noise bylaws. [Source: https://jamespasternak.ca/rogers-stadium-faq/, accessed 2026-08-14] This is reported here as a live civic-infrastructure matter per a primary constituency-office source; it was not cross-checked against independent news coverage in this pass (see §8), and no individual is characterized by its inclusion.
  • Bathurst Manor Neighbourhood Mobility Plan. The City has run a Bathurst Manor-specific traffic/road-safety planning process (addressing speeding, cut-through traffic, and vulnerable-road-user safety), including a November 2021 public meeting; the ward councillor's office continues to list this among its constituency planning files. [Source: https://jamespasternak.ca/event/bathurst-manor-atp-virtual-public-meeting/, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Ongoing City capital works, 2024-2026. The City's own York Centre ward-profile "Construction Notices" listing (accessed 2026-08-14; page itself last modified 2026-05-13) carries a long, actively updated set of items dated into 2026, including: a Grandravine Drive culvert replacement over Black Creek (construction notice dated June 6, 2025); a multi-year Basement Flooding Protection Program covering Wilson Avenue and surrounding local roads, with updates running from a January 2023 pre-construction notice through an April 2026 sidewalk-restoration notice; a Bathurst Street (Steeles Avenue to Kingsbridge Court) watermain replacement, road resurfacing, and road-safety-upgrade project (pre-construction notice dated April 15, 2026); and a Keele Street-area "Downsview Transmission Main," Keele Pumping Station, and storm-sewer upgrade project running from a November 2022 construction notice through a May 2025 update. A separate notice records a temporary walkway closure at Elie Wiesel Park (dated September 26, 2024), confirming the park's name and location within the ward. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-6-york-centre/, accessed 2026-08-14]

Transit & development

  • TTC subway. Two Line 1 Yonge-University stations sit within the ward: Wilson Station (570 Wilson Avenue, at Wilson Avenue and Allen Road — a major bus terminal complex and, per Wikipedia's Downsview Airport article, the site of Wilson Yard, the TTC's largest subway yard) and Sheppard West Station (1035 Sheppard Avenue West, at Sheppard and Allen Road, connecting to York Region Transit). Both stations are named within the Clanton Park neighbourhood article. [Source: https://www.ttc.ca/subway-stations/wilson-station, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://www.ttc.ca/subway-stations/sheppard-west-station, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clanton_Park,_Toronto, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downsview_Airport, accessed 2026-08-14 (Wilson Yard)]
  • Bus network. Both stations serve as hubs for a wide local bus network, including routes 29/929 Dufferin, 96/396/996 Wilson, 101 Downsview Park, 104 Faywood, 105 Dufferin North, 106 Sentinel, 107 Alness-Chesswood, 108 Driftwood, 118 Thistle Down, 119 Torbarrie, 120 Calvington, 160 Bathurst North, 165 Weston Road North, 184 Ancaster Park, and 84/384/984 Sheppard West. [Source: https://www.ttc.ca/subway-stations/wilson-station, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://www.ttc.ca/subway-stations/sheppard-west-station, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • GO Transit / further subway. Downsview Park Station, at the north end of Downsview Park, opened December 17, 2017 as part of the Line 1 extension into Vaughan and functions as an intermodal interchange with GO Transit's Barrie line. Its precise position relative to the Ward 6/Ward 5 boundary was not independently verified against a ward polygon in this pass (see §8); it sits within or immediately adjacent to the ward, at the core of the Downsview lands the ward's own construction-notice page and councillor's office both treat as part of the ward's file. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downsview_Park, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Eglinton Crosstown West Extension — checked, does not appear to serve this ward. The planned Line 5 extension (Mount Dennis to Renforth Drive, expected 2031) runs through Mount Dennis, Jane/Eglinton, Scarlett, Royal York, Islington, Kipling, Martin Grove, and Renforth — all south and west of Ward 6, in York South-Weston/Etobicoke Centre territory. No source found in this pass places any ECWE station or alignment within York Centre; it is not covered further here. [Source: https://www.metrolinx.com/en/projects-and-programs/eglinton-crosstown-west-extension/what-were-building, accessed 2026-08-14 (via WebSearch result summary; not independently re-fetched this pass)]
  • Development context. Beyond the Downsview/YZD file (§3), the ward's construction-notices page shows routine capital works (watermain, road resurfacing, sidewalks) rather than large private development applications outside the Downsview lands; no additional non-Downsview development application was identified as ward-specific in this pass (see §8).

Local commons

  • Toronto Public Library — Downsview Branch, 2793 Keele Street (at Keele Street and Wilson Avenue, behind the plaza). The branch dates to a 1956 North York Public Library Board decision and opened in 1962; it was closed for renovation and expansion in 1995 and reopened in 1996. [Source: https://tpl.ca/locations/DO, accessed 2026-08-14 (redirected from https://www.torontopubliclibrary.ca/downsview/)] A search for a "Bathurst Manor" TPL branch page did not resolve to confirmed content in this pass — not stated as fact without confirmation (see §8).
  • Parks. Earl Bales Park (4169 Bathurst Street, ~51 ha/127 acres) contains a ski/snowboard hill (the North York Ski Centre — the only alpine ski centre remaining within Toronto's city limits since Centennial Park's closed in 2022), the Earl Bales Community Centre, a dog park, an amphitheatre, and the Canadian Society for Yad Vashem Holocaust Memorial Site. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_Bales_Park, accessed 2026-08-14] Downsview Park (~231.5 ha/572 acres, at the site of the former CFB Downsview) is a large urban park containing the Hangar Sports Centre, the Toronto Wildlife Centre, and the BMO Training Ground (Toronto FC's practice facility); it borders Clanton Park and is managed by the Canada Lands Company. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downsview_Park, accessed 2026-08-14] Bathurst Manor's own Wikipedia article names several smaller municipal parks in that neighbourhood — Garthdale Park, G. Ross Lord Park, Irving W. Chapley Park, Maxwell Park, and the West Don Parklands. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bathurst_Manor, accessed 2026-08-14] A City construction notice separately confirms Elie Wiesel Park as a named park within the ward (walkway-closure notice, September 2024), and the ward councillor's office reported the August 2025 grand opening of a renamed Milton Berger Park (formerly Tippett Park). [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-6-york-centre/, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://jamespasternak.ca/free-workshops-for-children-and-adults-downsview-library/, accessed 2026-08-14 (recent-news list, "Grand Opening of Milton Berger Park")]
  • Community/recreation centres. Earl Bales Community Centre (within Earl Bales Park) and Irving W. Chapley Community Centre (Bathurst Manor; outdoor pool, tot's play area, meeting rooms) are named City of Toronto Parks, Forestry and Recreation facilities. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_Bales_Park, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bathurst_Manor, accessed 2026-08-14] The Prosserman Jewish Community Centre (formerly the Bathurst Jewish Community Centre), a large privately/community-operated multi-purpose facility with indoor and outdoor pools, an indoor track, and the Leah Posluns Theatre, also sits in Bathurst Manor — flagged here as a private/community institution, not a City-run CRC. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bathurst_Manor, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • This is a light research pass (named facilities found by source, not the formal point-in-polygon dataset join the plan §6 reserves as future work) — a fuller, geospatially-verified branch/CRC/park list needs that join, not this brief.

What else is on your ballot

Every Toronto voter marks three races on October 26, 2026: this ward’s councillor (above), the citywide mayor, and one school trustee.

  • Mayor (citywide — the same race for every ward) — 43 candidates registered as of 2026-08-14, identical treatment: the mayoral race card.
  • School trustee — one seat, for whichever of Toronto’s four school boards (Toronto District School Board, Toronto Catholic District School Board, Conseil scolaire catholique MonAvenir, Conseil scolaire Viamonde) matches your school-support designation. Trustee-district boundaries do not in general line up with the 25 council wards, and a verified address-to-trustee-district crosswalk is not on file for this ward — a named gap, not a guess (see below). 39 TDSB and 21 TCDSB trustee candidates are registered citywide as of 2026-08-14; find your own designation and voting place at MyVote.

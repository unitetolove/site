Ward 21 — Scarborough Centre

Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

Ward 21 sits entirely north of Highway 401 in central Scarborough, running from the highway up to Steeles Avenue, between Victoria Park Avenue to the west and Midland Avenue to the east. Its core is Scarborough City Centre — the former City of Scarborough's downtown, built around Scarborough Town Centre and the Scarborough Civic Centre — now one of Toronto's biggest active construction sites: three new subway stations are under construction as the Scarborough Subway Extension tunnels its way north, and the mall's owner has an approved master plan for more than 10,000 new homes on the surrounding land, with the first 1,285-unit phase already rising.

Geography & boundaries

  • Legal boundary (Elections Ontario legal description, reproduced on Wikipedia): commencing at the intersection of the city's northerly limit with Midland Avenue; south along Midland Avenue to Highway 401; west along the 401 to Victoria Park Avenue; north along Victoria Park Avenue to the city's northerly limit; east along that limit back to the start. In plain terms: Steeles Avenue to the north, Midland Avenue to the east, Highway 401 to the south, Victoria Park Avenue to the west — the ward sits entirely north of the 401. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_21_Scarborough_Centre, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Official City ward map (PDF), linked from the City's own Ward 21 profile page: [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/945c-CityPlanning-Map-Ward-21.pdf, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • The ward's identifiable core is the Scarborough City Centre district — centred on Scarborough Town Centre, the Scarborough Civic Centre, Albert Campbell Square, and the Consilium Place office towers. This district was formerly represented as Ward 38 Scarborough Centre under the pre-2018 44-ward model; in 2018, Wards 37 and 38 merged to form the current Ward 21 Scarborough Centre. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarborough_City_Centre, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Not asserted as a formal neighbourhood-to-ward crosswalk (none is on file project-wide, per the plan §3) — several neighbourhood names are commonly associated with "Scarborough Centre" in secondary sources but, on direct check, sit south of Highway 401 and so outside this ward's current legal boundary; see §8 for the specific finding and why it isn't asserted here.

Civic texture — what’s live here

  • Scarborough Town Centre master-plan redevelopment. Oxford Properties (the mall's owner, the real-estate arm of OMERS) has an approved master plan for the land around Scarborough Town Centre providing for more than 10,000 residential units across 89 developable acres, intended to build a mixed-use urban hub around the new transit station. [Source: https://www.oxfordproperties.com/news/oxford-breaks-ground-on-750m-development-delivering-scarboroughs-first-major-purpose-built-housing-project-in-a-generation, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Alta — first phase of that plan, under construction. Oxford broke ground in July 2025 on a $750-million, 1,285-unit purpose-built rental project ("Alta") at 25 Borough Drive, on a 3.4-acre site on the west side of the mall — 268 units designated affordable, backed by a $650-million CMHC construction loan (the largest single CMHC loan issued in Toronto through that program). Three towers sit atop two 7-storey podiums, including a new 22,000-square-foot public park at the south end; completion is targeted for summer 2029. [Source: same URL as above, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Scarborough Subway Extension (SSE) — active, multi-site construction, this ward is one of four named. A City staff report to Scarborough Community Council (dated May 11, 2026) states the SSE affects Wards 20, 21, 23, 24, and details construction status as of Q2 2026:
  • Tunnelling: as of May 7, 2026, the tunnel-boring machine had bored over 4.3 km of the 6.9 km tunnel (Sheppard/McCowan to Kennedy Station), travelling south under McCowan Road.
  • Scarborough Centre Station (in this ward, at Scarborough Town Centre): piling work began September 2025 and was halfway complete as of the report, continuing to spring 2027; McCowan Road north of Bushby Drive reduced to three lanes in places; utility (storm/sanitary/watermain) replacement underway; road reconfigurations in effect March 2026 through April 2027.
  • Lawrence Avenue/McCowan Road Station (this ward): excavation prep underway for the main station entrance; McCowan Road at Lawrence Avenue West rerouted; site work hours extended to 24 hours on weekdays.
  • Community benefits reported project-wide (not ward-specific): 635 jobs created including 58 local hires, 30% of new contractor hires identifying as women and 50% as Black, Indigenous, or a Person of Colour, $29 million in local business investment.
  • Metrolinx held five days of community open houses (May 5–14, 2026) including a session at Scarborough Town Centre, gathering input on station-naming recommendations. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2026/sc/bgrd/backgroundfile-286899.pdf ("Metrolinx's Scarborough Subway Extension – Q2 2026 Semi-Annual Update," report to Scarborough Community Council, dated May 11, 2026), accessed 2026-08-14 — fetched and read in full]
  • City capital works, 2026 (from the City's own Ward 21 profile page). Multiple concurrent City construction projects are listed for this ward, including: Bertrand Avenue culvert replacement over Massey Creek (notices 2024–2025); Lupin Drive pedestrian bridge replacement over Taylor Massey Creek (notices into 2026); Warden Avenue watermain replacement, intersection improvements and road resurfacing (notices through November 2025); Warden Avenue culvert replacement over West Highland Creek (notices through September 2025); Brimley Road sanitary sewer replacement (notice dated May 2026); and a 2026 Local Road Resurfacing program under the Council-approved 2026 Capital Works Program. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-21-scarborough-centre/, accessed 2026-08-14, page itself dated "modified July 7, 2026"]

Transit & development

Rail: TTC Line 3 Scarborough (SRT) currently serves this ward's core with stops at Midland, Scarborough Centre, and its terminus at McCowan, but Line 3 is being demolished and replaced by the Line 2 Bloor-Danforth extension (the SSE, §3), which relocates Scarborough Centre station and adds new stations at Lawrence Avenue/McCowan Road and Sheppard Avenue/ McCowan Road. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarborough_City_Centre, accessed 2026-08-14; SSE construction detail per §3] GO Transit / regional bus: the Scarborough Centre Bus Terminal, adjacent to Scarborough Centre station, is used by GO Transit bus services and several inter-city coach carriers. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarborough_City_Centre, accessed 2026-08-14] Development: the ward's dominant current development story is the Scarborough Town Centre-area master plan and its first phase, Alta — see §3. Checked, not confirmed: an AI-generated search summary claimed the Golden Mile Secondary Plan Study (an Eglinton Avenue East corridor redevelopment) spans "Wards 20 and 21." The study area's own stated boundary — Victoria Park Avenue to Ashtonbee Road/Hydro Corridor — sits along and south of Highway 401, outside Ward 21's legal boundary (§2), which begins at the 401 and runs north from there. A direct read of the City's Golden Mile Secondary Plan Final Report background file did not turn up any "Ward 21" or "Ward 20" reference. This claim is not included as Ward 21 civic texture — flagged as unresolved, not asserted either way. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/planning-studies-initiatives/golden-mile-secondary-plan-study/golden-mile-secondary-plan-study-overview/, accessed 2026-08-14; background file checked in full: https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2020/sc/bgrd/backgroundfile-154881.pdf, accessed 2026-08-14]

Networks & local commons

As of August 17, 2026, this project's public organization registry maps 93 organizations directly to Ward 21 (Scarborough Centre) — down slightly from the 94 this brief stated the previous day. A dedicated dork-library sweep completed the same day found this ward is a genuine "hollow core" for organization discovery: its actual legal boundary (Victoria Park to Midland, Highway 401 to Steeles) is a narrow north-south corridor, and nearly every "Scarborough"-named organization a generic search surfaces sits just outside it — south of the 401, east of Midland, or across the line into a neighbouring ward. Confirmed real gaps with no in-ward hits: no BIA (checked against the City's own BIA register), no condo-tower tenant group, no seniors' club, no community garden, no ward-specific Discord/Nextdoor/Meetup group, no union local, and no historical society. Six organizations were found and independently confirmed inside the boundary — Heathwood Ratepayers Association, BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir's Scarborough temple (1840 Birchmount Road, distinct from BAPS's separate Etobicoke temple), Sakinah Community Center (1430 Birchmount Road), and three Scarborough-wide groups reachable from this ward (Scarborough Mutual Aid, a Buy Nothing group, and Scarborough Minor Football Association, whose primary field is outside the ward but whose secondary venue is inside it) — the last three are recorded at the broader "Scarborough" level rather than credited to this ward specifically, since their own base isn't ward-bound. The sweep also caught a real pre-existing data error: four Toronto Public Library branches and one school council had been explicitly tagged to this ward despite having street addresses south of Highway 401, outside its actual boundary; those tags have been removed (the correct ward was not determined) — only the Scarborough Civic Centre Branch library, the ward's true TPL presence, remains. 9 of the 93 organizations carry `verified-live` status with an independently direct-fetched confirmation; the rest are `seed-web-verified` or, for one thinly-sourced find, `seed-unverified` pending a liveness re-check — real organizations, not yet independently confirmed live or carrying a public website in this registry, so this brief does not list them individually as public doors the way a directly-verified organization would be.

This brief's Local Commons section above already names Ward 21's civic-infrastructure anchors; those, plus the organizations above, are the backbone the city-wide network map builds outward from.

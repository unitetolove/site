Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

Built for three readers at once: a resident wants sixty seconds — is my ward’s seat contested, who’s running, what does my ward look like. A journalist wants five minutes — the receipted numbers, fast, with a citation trail to verify without re-reporting. A candidate or community organization wants the full depth — every other candidate in the race treated identically, named gaps stated honestly. Every reader gets the same page; only how far you read changes.

Methodology & firewall

  • Registry facts only. Candidate rosters, registration dates, incumbency, and 2022 results come from this project’s Toronto elections registry, re-pulled from the City Clerk’s official candidate feeds.
  • Bios, strictly sourced. Every candidate bio is synthesized only from that candidate’s own published campaign material and registry facts — never from news coverage, opposition material, or this project’s own characterization. A candidate with no located material gets that stated plainly, in the same format as every other candidate, never a blank and never an apology.
  • No scores, no rankings, no characterization — anywhere. This project informs; it never ranks, endorses, or attacks a candidate. Every candidate in a race gets identical fields, identical length, identical treatment, alphabetical by surname only.
  • Named gaps, never papered over. Where a fact isn’t on file — a trustee-ward crosswalk, a library/park geospatial join — each ward page says so plainly rather than guessing.
  • Provisional until nominations close. Every registrant figure is dated 2026-08-14 and re-pulls after nominations close 2026-08-21; structural facts (geography, transit, census, development) are dated separately and don’t move at that pivot.

Found a mistake?

That’s the system working. Every figure on this page traces to a public source or a candidate’s own published material, cited in the Receipts section above; if one is wrong, out of date, or reads differently than the source supports, we want to know before anyone else repeats it. Send it to the corrections page — it takes thirty seconds, and every correction is published, dated, and left visible.

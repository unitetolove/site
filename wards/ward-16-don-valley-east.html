Ward 16 — Don Valley East

Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

Don Valley East covers Flemingdon Park, Victoria Village, and the O'Connor-Parkview/Thorncliffe-adjacent area north of the Don Valley — a mix of 1960s-70s high-rise apartment communities and post-war bungalow neighbourhoods. It's currently at the centre of two major, overlapping transit projects: the Eglinton Crosstown (Line 5) opened in February 2026 with three stops in Flemingdon Park, and the Ontario Line (under construction) will add two more elevated stations here, including an interchange with Line 5 — making this one of relatively few Toronto wards gaining two new rapid-transit lines within a few years of each other.

Geography & boundaries

Legally defined (Elections Ontario boundary text, per Wikipedia): commences at Highway 401 & Victoria Park Avenue, south along Victoria Park to Sunrise Avenue, west along Sunrise (and its production) to the Don River East Branch, generally southwest along that branch to Don Mills Road, north along Don Mills Road to Overlea Boulevard, southwest along Overlea to the Don River West Branch, generally northwest along that branch to Eglinton Avenue East, east along Eglinton to Leslie Street, generally north along Leslie to Highway 401, then east along the highway back to the point of commencement. Named areas commonly associated with the ward, per neighbourhood-specific sources: Flemingdon Park (bounded by the West Don River, Eglinton Avenue East, the East Don River, and a rail-adjacent property line to the south) and Victoria Village (bounded by the Don Valley, Lawrence Avenue East, Victoria Park Avenue, and Eglinton Avenue East). [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_16_Don_Valley_East, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemingdon_Park, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Village, accessed 2026-08-14]

Civic texture — what’s live here

  • Eglinton Crosstown (Line 5) opening, three stops in Flemingdon Park. Line 5 opened February 8, 2026, running through both Flemingdon Park and Victoria Village. Flemingdon Park specifically gained three stops: Don Valley Station, Aga Khan Park & Museum, and Wynford, connecting the neighbourhood to Line 1 (Yonge-University) and to Scarborough Town Centre. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemingdon_Park, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Village, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Ontario Line construction — two elevated stations planned for Flemingdon Park. The Ontario Line (under construction as of this pass) is planned to add two elevated stations in Flemingdon Park — Flemingdon Park Station and Don Valley Station — with the latter creating a direct interchange with Line 5. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemingdon_Park, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Former Celestica site redevelopment. Plans are underway for a large development at the 24-hectare former Celestica manufacturing site (844 Don Mills Road, demolished late 2018) at Don Mills Road & Eglinton Avenue, described as bringing thousands of residents across eight condo towers plus new offices, shops, and (per an earlier-stage plan reported by a real-estate industry source) a 100,000-sq.-ft recreation centre and townhomes. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemingdon_Park, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • 25 St. Dennis Drive intensification, resident concern (dated context). A 37-storey residential addition to the existing 25 St. Dennis Drive apartment complex was approved by the City after being proposed in 2017; Toronto Star reporting (November 2020) recorded resident concern about consultation and displacement risk for "vulnerable communities." This is background from a 2020 news report, not independently re-verified against the project's current 2026 status this pass. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemingdon_Park, accessed 2026-08-14, citing thestar.com, November 25, 2020]
  • Active City capital works, 2026. The City's own Ward 16 profile page lists a further set of live/recent construction notices — Don Valley Parkway bridge rehabilitation under Metrolinx rail and slope stabilization at Dallimore Circle; sewer/watermain work on Duncan Mill Road, Cranborne Avenue, Ferrand Drive, and Prestbury Street; O'Connor Drive storm sewer replacement (shared with Wards 19/20); and bridge rehabilitation at Spanbridge Road/Lawrence Avenue/Wynford Drive over the DVP. A general 2026 local road-resurfacing notice for the ward was issued in March 2026. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-16-don-valley-east/, accessed 2026-08-14, page dated "modified July 15, 2026"]

Transit & development

TTC: the ward is now served by Line 5 Eglinton (three Flemingdon Park stops, per §3) and, per Victoria Village's own profile, multiple bus routes (24 Victoria Park, 34 Eglinton, 54 Lawrence East, 70 O'Connor, 91 Woodbine, plus express variants); exact station-to-ward assignment not independently verified against the boundary polygon this pass — named as general context, not a formal join. Major transit projects: the February 2026 Line 5 opening and the under-construction Ontario Line (§3) together make this one of the more transit-active wards in this lane's set. Development: the former Celestica site (§3) is the single largest identified development; the 25 St. Dennis Drive intensification (§3) is a second, smaller but locally contested item. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemingdon_Park, accessed 2026-08-14]

What else is on your ballot

Every Toronto voter marks three races on October 26, 2026: this ward’s councillor (above), the citywide mayor, and one school trustee.

  • Mayor (citywide — the same race for every ward) — 43 candidates registered as of 2026-08-14, identical treatment: the mayoral race card.
  • School trustee — one seat, for whichever of Toronto’s four school boards (Toronto District School Board, Toronto Catholic District School Board, Conseil scolaire catholique MonAvenir, Conseil scolaire Viamonde) matches your school-support designation. Trustee-district boundaries do not in general line up with the 25 council wards, and a verified address-to-trustee-district crosswalk is not on file for this ward — a named gap, not a guess (see below). 39 TDSB and 21 TCDSB trustee candidates are registered citywide as of 2026-08-14; find your own designation and voting place at MyVote.

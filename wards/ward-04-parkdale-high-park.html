Your ward, in one minute

Ward 4 — Parkdale-High Park is home to about 104,715 people (2021 Census), with a median household income of $85,000 a year (2020 reference year) and a 56.3% / 43.7% renter/owner household split. Gord Perks won the 2022 council race here by 3.9 percentage points — razor-tight — about as close as a Toronto council race gets. As of 2026-08-05, 9 candidate(s) are registered to run here in 2026.

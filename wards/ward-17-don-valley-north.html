Ward 17 — Don Valley North

Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

Don Valley North sits between Bayview Avenue and Victoria Park Avenue, north of Highway 401 to the city's northern limit — covering Bayview Village, Henry Farm/Parkway Forest, and adjoining North York neighbourhoods. It's a mostly low-rise, park-rich suburban ward built out largely in the 1950s-60s, with growing condo intensification along the Sheppard Avenue corridor and served by Line 4 (Sheppard) subway stations rather than any of the newer LRT lines opening elsewhere in the city this year.

Geography & boundaries

Legally defined (Elections Ontario boundary text, per Wikipedia): commences at the city's northerly limit & Bayview Avenue, south along Bayview to Highway 401, east along the highway to Victoria Park Avenue, north along Victoria Park to the city's northerly limit, then west along that limit back to the point of commencement. In plain terms: bounded west by Bayview Avenue, south by Highway 401, east by Victoria Park Avenue, and north by Steeles Avenue (the city's northern limit at this longitude). Named areas confirmed by their own sources as within this ward: Bayview Village (bounded by Finch Ave. E., Bayview Ave., Leslie St., and Highway 401, per the City of Toronto's neighbourhood definition) and, per its own boundary description matching this ward's box, Henry Farm/Parkway Forest (bounded by Sheppard Ave., Highway 404, Highway 401, and the Don River East Branch near Leslie St.). Note: the separately-named "Don Mills" neighbourhood (centred well south, near Lawrence/Eglinton) is stated by its own Wikipedia article to sit in Ward 16 (Don Valley East), not this ward — flagged here to avoid the two Don-Mills-Road-adjacent wards being conflated; the Don Mills subway station itself, at Don Mills Road & Sheppard Avenue, sits further north and falls within this ward's boundary box. Civic texture — what’s live here

  • Sheppard Avenue corridor intensification, Bayview Village. Per Bayview Village's own Wikipedia entry, there is "continuing" high-rise condominium development along Sheppard Avenue and in the land south of Sheppard and north of Highway 401 — described as "of a completely different nature" from the single-family low-rise housing in the neighbourhood's northern area, i.e. an active, ongoing contrast between older low-density streets and newer condo development along the ward's main east-west arterial. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayview_Village, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Active City capital works, 2026. The City's own Ward 17 profile page lists a set of live/recent construction notices — watermain cleaning/relining on Adamede Crescent, Cummer Avenue, Hollyberry Trail, and the Sheppard Avenue East corridor (two separate segments, one deferred in late 2024 and later proceeding in March 2026); a Forest Grove Drive culvert rehabilitation over Newtonbrook Creek (notice dated April 2026); Cairnside Crescent road resurfacing (notice dated May 2026); and continuing East Don River sewer connections/creek improvements work (update dated October 2024). A general 2026 local road-resurfacing notice for the ward was issued in March 2026. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-17-don-valley-north/, accessed 2026-08-14, page dated "modified July 2, 2026"]

Transit & development

TTC: the ward is served by Line 4 Sheppard at Bayview, Bessarion, and Leslie stations (all in/near Bayview Village) and Don Mills station (Don Mills Road & Sheppard Avenue, also a York Region Transit connection point, near Henry Farm); no Line 5 Eglinton or Ontario Line stations were identified within this ward's boundary this pass — unlike several neighbouring wards (W12, W15, W16), this ward's rapid transit remains the older Line 4 corridor. Exact station-to-ward assignment not independently verified against the boundary polygon this pass — named as general context, not a formal join. Development: the clearest ongoing signal found this pass is Sheppard-corridor condo intensification in Bayview Village (§3); no other single large development was identified for this ward this pass. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayview_Village, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Farm, accessed 2026-08-14]

Local commons

  • North York YMCA — Bayview Avenue & Sheppard Avenue (southeast corner); described as the largest recreational facility serving Bayview Village, with a gymnasium, dance studio, conditioning room, and two pools. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayview_Village, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Bayview Village Park, Blue Ridge Park, Clarinda Park, Alamosa Park, Leslie Park — named parks in Bayview Village; the East Don Parklands (part of the Toronto ravine system) also runs through the neighbourhood. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayview_Village, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Parkway Forest Community Centre and Havenbrook Park (baseball diamond, toboggan hill, Henry Farm Tennis Club) and Parkway Forest Park — named facilities/parks in Henry Farm. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Farm, accessed 2026-08-14]

Local media landscape

  • Bayview Village Association — a volunteer resident association (established 1956; over 50% of area residents are members) that publishes a regular newsletter to all homes and hosts all-candidates meetings and community events — a genuine, standing local-civic-communication channel for this part of the ward, though not an independent news outlet. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayview_Village, accessed 2026-08-14]

What else is on your ballot

Every Toronto voter marks three races on October 26, 2026: this ward’s councillor (above), the citywide mayor, and one school trustee.

  • Mayor (citywide — the same race for every ward) — 43 candidates registered as of 2026-08-14, identical treatment: the mayoral race card.
  • School trustee — one seat, for whichever of Toronto’s four school boards (Toronto District School Board, Toronto Catholic District School Board, Conseil scolaire catholique MonAvenir, Conseil scolaire Viamonde) matches your school-support designation. Trustee-district boundaries do not in general line up with the 25 council wards, and a verified address-to-trustee-district crosswalk is not on file for this ward — a named gap, not a guess (see below). 39 TDSB and 21 TCDSB trustee candidates are registered citywide as of 2026-08-14; find your own designation and voting place at MyVote.

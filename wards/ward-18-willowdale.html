Ward 18 — Willowdale

Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

Willowdale sits in the former City of North York, running along the Yonge Street subway corridor from Highway 401 north to Steeles Avenue at the city's northern limit. Its core is North York Centre — the office and civic hub around Yonge and Sheppard that is, by the City's own account, the largest office-employment cluster in Toronto outside Downtown — served directly by three subway stations on TTC Line 1. The ward is currently the subject of a multi-year City Planning review (North York at the Centre) rewriting the growth rules for that core, and a City-built supportive-housing project on Cummer Avenue that took years of community process and an Ontario Land Tribunal appeal to reach construction is set to open in 2026.

Geography & boundaries

Legally defined (Elections Ontario boundary text, reproduced on Wikipedia): commencing at the intersection of the city's northerly limit with Bayview Avenue; south along Bayview to Highway 401; generally southwesterly along the highway to the Don River West Branch; generally northwesterly along that branch to Bathurst Street; north along Bathurst to the city's northerly limit; east along that limit back to the start. In plain terms: Steeles Avenue (the city limit) to the north, Bayview Avenue to the east, Highway 401 to the south (until it crosses the Don River), the Don River West Branch to the southwest, and Bathurst Street to the west. Community council: North York. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_18_Willowdale, accessed 2026-08-14]

Ward 18 was created for the 2018 election, made up of the former Ward 23 Willowdale, part of the former Ward 24 Willowdale, and a small portion of the former Ward 10 York Centre. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_18_Willowdale, accessed 2026-08-14]

Named neighbourhoods/areas, as used in the City's own current planning-consultation material for this ward: "Willowdale East," "Willowdale West," "Newtonbrook East," and "Newtonbrook West" are named as the community areas within the North York at the Centre secondary-plan study (itself centred on the ward's Yonge Street corridor). [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/planning-studies-initiatives/north-york-at-the-centre/, accessed 2026-08-14] North York Centre / North York City Centre is the name used for the ward's high-density office/civic core at Yonge and Sheppard. Civic texture — what’s live here

  • North York at the Centre — Secondary Plan review, Phase 3 in progress. The City is rewriting the 1997 North York Centre Secondary Plan, which governs growth across the ward's Yonge Street core (home to more than 50,000 residents and nearly 35,000 employees by the City's own count). Phase 1 (background review) and Phase 2 (options and directions) are complete; City Council endorsed a Recommended Option on March 26, 2026, and directed staff to consider further changes in consultation with the community. A public meeting was held May 14, 2026 to share staff's evaluation of those potential changes; the study area is also being examined for boundary expansion within roughly 800 metres of the ward's three subway stations. A new Secondary Plan is expected to come forward in Phase 3, with implementation zoning to follow in a future Phase 4. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/planning-development/planning-studies-initiatives/north-york-at-the-centre/, accessed 2026-08-14, page dated "modified May 22, 2026"]
  • 185 Cummer Ave. (also referenced as 175 Cummer Ave. in earlier City and news materials) — modular supportive housing. The City is building a three-storey, 59-unit modular supportive-housing building next to Willowdale Manor (a Toronto Community Housing seniors' building), for people exiting homelessness, operated by WoodGreen Community Services. Announced in 2021 as part of the City's modular housing initiative, the project went through a City-initiated rezoning (approved by Council May 2022), a subsequent Ontario Land Tribunal appeal (resolved in the City's favour), and further Council contract amendments in 2024; the City's own project page (last modified August 4, 2026) states the building remains "set for completion in 2026." [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/community-people/housing-shelter/building-affordable-homes/housing-initiatives/supportive-housing-developments/185-cummer/, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Local infrastructure works (per the City's own Ward 18 construction-notice page). Active or recently active items include: Bathurst Street (Steeles Avenue to Kingsbridge Court) watermain replacement, road resurfacing and road-safety upgrades (pre-construction notice dated April 15, 2026); John McKenzie Gate storm sewer replacement (pre-construction notice dated May 30, 2026); Steeles Avenue bridge rehabilitation over the Don River (construction notice dated June 16, 2025, with update notices dated January 6, 2026 and June 29, 2026); Lorraine Drive watermain replacement (construction notice dated January 29, 2024); Terrace Avenue watermain cleaning and relining (construction notice dated July 11, 2024); and a 2026 Capital Works Program local-road-resurfacing package for Willowdale streets (pre-construction notice dated January 21, 2026). [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-18-willowdale/, accessed 2026-08-14, page dated "modified July 23, 2026"]

Transit & development

Subway: TTC Line 1 (Yonge-University) runs the length of the ward along the Yonge Street corridor, with three stations serving it directly — Sheppard-Yonge, North York Centre (the subway system's only infill station, opened 1987 specifically to serve North York City Centre), and Finch (the line's northern terminus). [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_York_Centre_station, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finch_station, accessed 2026-08-14] Line 4 (Sheppard) also originates at Sheppard-Yonge station on the ward's Yonge corridor, though the rest of that line runs east of Bayview Avenue, outside the ward. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheppard%E2%80%93Yonge_station, accessed 2026-08-14]

Transform Yonge (Sheppard Avenue to Finch Avenue). A long-planned full reconstruction of Yonge Street through the ward's core — approved by Council in 2020 with four vehicle lanes, wider sidewalks, raised cycle tracks and a landscaped median — is currently paused for a Council-directed feasibility study (per a December 2025 Council motion) exploring a revised design that would keep existing vehicle lanes while still improving streetscape and bikeways. Findings are expected to go to committee in early 2027; construction timing is not yet set. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/community-people/get-involved/public-consultations/infrastructure-projects/street-improvement-projects-in-north-york-centre/transform-yonge-from-sheppard-avenue-to-finch-avenue/, accessed 2026-08-14, page dated "modified July 23, 2026"]

Development/planning: the same North York at the Centre Secondary Plan review named in §3 is the ward's primary live development-policy thread — it will set new height, density and land-use rules for the Yonge corridor once adopted. [Source: same as §3 above]

Ward 01 — Etobicoke North

Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

Etobicoke North sits in Toronto's northwest corner, wedged between the Humber River and Highway 427. It's a large, mostly suburban ward — older subdivisions, townhome clusters, and apartment towers — and one of the city's most diverse: an estimated 58% of residents are immigrants and 76% identify as visible minorities. The ward includes Rexdale, Thistletown, Kingsview Village, Humberwood, The Elms, and Willowridge, and just gained a new transit line: the Finch West LRT's western terminus opened here in December 2025.

Geography & boundaries

Legally defined (per Elections Ontario's boundary description, reproduced on Wikipedia): the ward runs from the Humber River (east) south to Dixon Road, west along Dixon Road to Martin Grove Road, south to Eglinton Avenue West, west along Eglinton and Highway 427 to Highway 401, then follows the city's western and northern limits back to the Humber. Community council: Etobicoke and York. Named neighbourhoods: Rexdale, The Elms, Humberwood, Kingsview Village, Thistletown, Willowridge. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_1_Etobicoke_North, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/8ca9-CityPlanning-Map-Ward-1.pdf (City of Toronto ward map), accessed 2026-08-14]

Civic texture — what’s live here

  • Finch West LRT (Line 6) opened. The C$2.6B, 10.3 km, 18-stop light rail line opened December 7, 2025, running along Finch Avenue West and terminating at Humber College, in this ward's Humberwood-Rexdale area. It ran under "soft opening" hours (6am-10pm) until service was extended to 1am as of March 2026. Projected ridership: up to 40,000 rides/day (14.6M/year) by 2031. [Source: https://www.railwayage.com/passenger/light-rail/coming-dec-7-finch-line-west/, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://stevemunro.ca/2026/03/13/ttc-service-changes-effective-march-15-2026/, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://www.metrolinx.com/en/projects-and-programs/finch-west-lrt, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Development activity, ward-wide (dated context, not current-year). A February 2023 UrbanToronto ward-level development report counted 33 identified projects in Ward 1 — 4 recently completed, 9 under construction, 20 pre-construction. Notable: Humberwood Heights (6 blocks of 3-storey townhomes, occupancy began summer 2022); Regal Plaza Corporate Centre, a hotel expansion under construction near Dixon/Martin Grove; 1 Adriatic, a proposed 3-tower project (19/34/38 storeys, 1,000+ units) that would be the ward's tallest building if approved. Six of the ward's 33 identified projects were hotels, reflecting proximity to Pearson airport. This report is dated (Feb 2023) and should be treated as historical texture, not a current development count. [Source: https://urbantoronto.ca/news/2023/02/utpro-instant-reports-transit-catalyze-development-ward-1-etobicoke-north.51120, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Active City capital works, 2026. The City of Toronto's own Ward 1 profile page lists a live set of 2026 construction notices: local road resurfacing under the Council-approved 2026 Capital Works Program (notice dated January 21, 2026), plus multiple named-street projects (watermain replacement, culvert replacement, stormwater pumping station work) with notices dated as recently as May 2026. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-1-etobicoke-north/, accessed 2026-08-14, page itself dated "modified May 22, 2026"]
  • Ward's recent representation history. The ward was represented by a member of the Ford family for 22 years until Michael Ford's June 2022 resignation; a brief, contested council appointment process followed (Rosemarie Bryan's five-hour-40-minute tenure, then Rose Milczyn as interim). This is public electoral-history fact, not characterization of any 2026 candidate. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_1_Etobicoke_North, accessed 2026-08-14]

Transit & development

TTC service: multiple bus routes serve the ward; the new Line 6 Finch West LRT (opened Dec 2025) runs along Finch Avenue West and terminates at Humber College Station within the ward, connecting to roughly 30 bus routes, GO Transit, MiWay, York Region Transit, and Brampton Züm at its eastern end (Finch West Station, Line 1). [Source: https://www.ttc.ca/riding-the-ttc/Updates/Line-6-Finch-West-LRT-and-Line-1-Station-Renamings, accessed 2026-08-14] Development pattern (per §3): mostly Neighbourhood/Employment Area designations under the City's Official Plan — intensification here is occurring along the new transit corridor and near the airport (hotels), not downtown-scale density. [Source: UrbanToronto report cited in §3]

What else is on your ballot

Every Toronto voter marks three races on October 26, 2026: this ward’s councillor (above), the citywide mayor, and one school trustee.

  • Mayor (citywide — the same race for every ward) — 42 candidates registered as of 2026-08-14, identical treatment: the mayoral race card.
  • School trustee — one seat, for whichever of Toronto’s four school boards matches your school-support designation. Trustee-district boundaries do not in general line up with the 25 council wards; here is what this project has established for THIS ward, tiered by how confidently each is sourced:
    • Conseil scolaire Viamonde (French public) — Toronto-Ouest (Secteur 2, Annexe OUEST) (race card) — confirmed exact from the board’s own official regulatory determination document (Détermination et répartition des conseillers scolaires, Conseil scolaire Viamonde, March 2026, Annexe C).
    • Toronto Catholic District School Board (TCDSB) — Ward 1 (race card) — confirmed from the board’s own published ward page.
    • CSC MonAvenir (French Catholic) — this ward falls in one of MonAvenir’s two Toronto régions (Toronto-Est or Toronto-Ouest); the board has not published a ward-level boundary document, so which région applies here is not resolved — a region-level statement, not a guess at which one.
    • Toronto District School Board (TDSB) — local reporting places this ward in TDSB Ward 1 — not yet verified against an official board document (race card); see this ward’s precision note below.
    39 TDSB and 21 TCDSB trustee candidates are registered citywide as of 2026-08-14. Whatever the tiers above show, MyVote is the authoritative resolver for your own address — check it before you vote: MyVote.

Viamonde exact (official Annexe C determination doc, ward 1 listed under OUEST). MonAvenir: region-level only (Toronto Est/Ouest), no ward-level source found.

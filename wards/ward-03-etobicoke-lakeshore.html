Ward 03 — Etobicoke-Lakeshore

Reading this in real time: every registrant figure below — who’s filed, incumbency, candidate counts — is a snapshot as of 2026-08-14, from the City Clerk’s candidate feeds. Nominations close Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. — this page re-pulls and re-stamps every registrant figure after that date. Geography, transit, development and census figures are structural facts that don’t move at nomination close and are dated separately, section by section.

Ward 3 – Etobicoke-Lakeshore runs along Toronto's Lake Ontario shoreline in southwest Etobicoke, taking in the old lakeshore villages of Mimico, New Toronto, Long Branch and Alderwood between the Humber River and Etobicoke Creek. It's a waterfront-and-transit ward: two GO stations (Mimico, Long Branch) on the Lakeshore West line and a streetcar line that terminates at Long Branch Loop anchor a corridor that has seen sustained condominium development, especially around the Mimico GO lands. Away from the rail corridor the ward keeps a lower-rise, lake-access character — Marie Curtis Park and Colonel Samuel Smith Park bookend its waterfront, and its library branches (New Toronto, Long Branch, Alderwood, Mimico Centennial) mostly predate amalgamation, several tracing to the 1910s-20s.

Geography & boundaries

  • General location: Southwestern Toronto, within the former City of Etobicoke. Community council: Etobicoke and York. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_3_Etobicoke%E2%80%94Lakeshore, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Boundaries, as legally defined (Elections Ontario boundary description, reproduced on Wikipedia): the ward runs from the Humber River, southwesterly along Dundas Street West to the Canadian Pacific Railway, south along the railway to Mimico Creek, generally northwesterly along the creek to Kipling Avenue, south along Kipling to Bloor Street West, west along Bloor to Highway 427, south along the highway to Dundas Street West, west along Dundas to the city's westerly limit, then south and east along the city's westerly and southerly limits back to the Humber River. In plainer terms: bordered roughly by the Etobicoke Creek on the west, the Humber River on the east, Bloor Street/Kipling Avenue/Mimico Creek/Dundas Street West on the north, and Lake Ontario on the south. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_3_Etobicoke%E2%80%94Lakeshore, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Ward map (City of Toronto): [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/8c67-CityPlanning-Map-Ward-3.pdf, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Named neighbourhoods commonly associated with the ward (as reported by sources, not a formal ward-neighbourhood crosswalk — that spine is not on file project-wide, per the plan §3): Mimico, New Toronto, Long Branch and Alderwood. [Source: https://globalnews.ca/news/4542361/toronto-election-2018-ward-3-etobicoke-lakeshore/, accessed 2026-08-14] A local community newspaper describes its own coverage footprint for this stretch of the city as "Humber Bay Shores, Mimico, Lakeshore Village, Long Branch and along Lake Shore Blvd. W. to Brown's Line, Alderwood" — this is the paper's own self-described readership area, not a City-published neighbourhood list, and is reported here as such. [Source: https://torontonewswire.com/about-us/, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Ward creation: Ward 3 has existed under this boundary and name since the 2018 municipal election (province-wide 25-ward redraw under the Better Local Government Act, 2018); it is an amalgamation of the former Ward 5 (northern section) and former Ward 6 (southern section) under the pre-2018 44-ward system. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_3_Etobicoke%E2%80%94Lakeshore, accessed 2026-08-14]

Civic texture — what’s live here

  • Billy Bishop Airport expansion — the ward's most active current civic issue. The Ontario government is advancing a proposal to expand Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport, including a runway extension studied at up to 1.83 km. A CP24 report (June 2026) states the expansion could affect thousands of homes along the waterfront, including south Etobicoke, by increasing the number of residents under flight paths by roughly 25%. South Etobicoke community groups, including the Mimico Residents Association, co-organized a public town hall on this at the Mimico Cruising Club on July 7, 2026; all 120 in-person seats booked quickly, about 100 more watched a livestream, and over 500 have viewed the recording since. Transport Canada opened a federal public consultation on Billy Bishop's future that closed July 24, 2026. This is a live, ongoing issue as of this brief's research date, not resolved. [Source: https://www.cp24.com/politics/toronto-city-hall/2026/06/04/report-says-billy-bishop-expansion-could-impact-thousands-of-homes-along-waterfront/, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://mimicoresidents.ca/billy-bishop-airport-townhall-for-south-etobicoke-tuesday-july-7-7pm/, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://mimicoresidents.ca/they-said-no-to-jets-in-2015-why-are-we-back-here/, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Transit-oriented development at the Mimico GO lands (315 & 327 Royal York Road). A zoning by-law amendment application, filed in 2022 and still on file as of August 14, 2026, proposes a transit-oriented development integrated with Mimico GO Station on both Metrolinx-owned land (315 Royal York Rd.) and adjacent developer-owned land (327 Royal York Rd., Vandyk Group of Companies). As posted by the Mimico Residents Association, the application text describes two residential towers on a shared 4-to-8-storey podium, a transit facility, and office/retail uses, with a total proposed gross floor area of 60,814 m² and 686 residential units; renderings posted with the same application material show towers as tall as 44 storeys. Given the age and multiple revisions of this file, exact current height/unit figures should be checked against the City's Application Information Centre before quoting precisely — this brief reports what the Residents Association's own page states, not a re-verified City record. [Source: https://mimicoresidents.ca/major-development-project-in-mimico-at-315-327-royal-york-road/, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Active City of Toronto 2026 capital works, ward-wide. The City's own Ward 3 profile page lists a substantial number of live construction notices dated through mid-2026: local road resurfacing under the Council-approved 2026 Capital Works Program (Alcan Avenue, Boncer Drive, East Side Drive, Fourth Street), watermain replacement/relining (Foch Avenue, Forty First Street, Jopling Avenue South, Staffordshire Place), a sanitary forcemain replacement tied to the Mimico Sewage Pumping Station, an Islington Stormwater Pumping Station construction project, a North Queen Street culvert removal, and preliminary design investigations for infrastructure improvements along Belvedere Boulevard, Islington Avenue and Kipling Avenue. Notice dates run as recently as July 2026. [Source: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/data-research-maps/neighbourhoods-communities/ward-profiles/ward-3-etobicoke-lakeshore/, accessed 2026-08-14, page itself dated "modified August 4, 2026"]
  • Ward's 2022 representation history. Amber Morley defeated the long-serving incumbent Mark Grimes in the 2022 election — the only defeat of a sitting councillor seeking re-election that year, citywide. This is public electoral-history fact, not characterization of any candidate. [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_3_Etobicoke%E2%80%94Lakeshore, accessed 2026-08-14]

Transit & development

  • GO Transit (Lakeshore West line). Both Mimico GO Station and Long Branch GO Station sit within the ward. Both are in active Metrolinx GO Expansion upgrade programs aimed at accessibility and higher service frequency (trains every 15 minutes or better on this line). Long Branch GO's planned upgrade includes a new west entrance building and tunnel, a new east pedestrian tunnel and entrance building, revised parking/pick-up areas, secure bike storage, and heated, snow-melting platforms — track work is also intended to enable future electrification. [Source: https://www.metrolinx.com/en/projects-and-programs/lakeshore-west-line-go-expansion/what-were-building/long-branch, accessed 2026-08-14] Mimico GO's planned upgrade includes a new east elevator/stair connection to the platform, a new south tunnel entrance at Manchester Street and Blue Goose Street, and platform accessibility upgrades (tactile edge indicators); as of August 14, 2026, Metrolinx describes the project as "in procurement and design," with a construction manager overseeing design details. [Source: https://www.metrolinx.com/en/projects-and-programs/lakeshore-west-line-go-expansion/what-were-building/mimico, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • TTC streetcar. Long Branch Loop, at the ward's western edge, is the terminus for two streetcar routes: 507 Long Branch (Humber Loop to Long Branch Loop, created November 19, 2023 when the TTC split the old 501 Queen route at Humber Loop) and 508 Lake Shore (Long Branch Loop to Parliament Street via King, weekday rush hours only, since October 30, 2023). [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/507_Long_Branch, accessed 2026-08-14] [Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/508_Lake_Shore, accessed 2026-08-14] TTC bus routes also serve the ward's interior; the New Toronto library branch notes it is reachable by TTC bus #110 from Islington subway station and by the streetcar along Lake Shore Boulevard West. [Source: https://tpl.ca/locations/nt/, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Development pattern. Beyond the single Mimico GO application detailed in §3, general real-estate reporting for the broader "W6" market area (Alderwood, Humber Bay, Mimico, Long Branch) describes ongoing pre-construction condo activity along the Lake Shore Boulevard West/Mimico GO corridor, consistent with the transit-oriented intensification pattern named above; a June 2026 market update for that (real-estate-industry, not municipal-ward) boundary reported 78 home sales that month at an average price of $796,624. This real-estate district does not exactly match the Ward 3 municipal boundary and is cited here only as general market-context texture, not a ward-specific figure. [Source: https://etobicokecondos.ca/blog.html/june-2026-w6-etobicoke-real-estate-market-update-alderwood-mimico-long-9081139, accessed 2026-08-14]
  • Note on the Eglinton Crosstown West Extension: this Line 5 extension runs through Etobicoke along the Eglinton Avenue corridor with stations at Mount Dennis, Jane, Scarlett, Royal York, Islington, Kipling and Martin Grove — all north of Ward 3's boundary as defined in §2 (the ward's northern edge runs along Bloor Street/Kipling Avenue/Mimico Creek/Dundas Street West, well south of Eglinton Avenue). It is not included here as a ward-3 transit project because its stations fall outside this ward. [Source: https://www.metrolinx.com/en/projects-and-programs/eglinton-crosstown-west-extension, accessed 2026-08-14]

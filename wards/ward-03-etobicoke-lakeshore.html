Your ward, in one minute

Ward 3 — Etobicoke-Lakeshore is home to about 139,920 people (2021 Census), with a median household income of $90,000 a year (2020 reference year) and a 43.1% / 56.9% renter/owner household split. Amber Morley won the 2022 council race here by 6.1 percentage points — a real contest, not a landslide. As of 2026-08-05, 4 candidate(s) are registered to run here in 2026.

How to vote here

Election day is Monday, October 26, 2026, 10 a.m.–8 p.m.; advance voting runs October 6–11, 2026, 10 a.m.–7 p.m. daily. You vote in the ward where you live. Check or update your registration, and find your voting place once it is published, at MyVote.

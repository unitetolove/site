The author's exit — a draft for your hand only

Publish word given 2026-08-06 — not yet linked into site navigation. This essay is part of the PC3 (permaculture3) set, cleared for publication on 2026-08-06. This page is reachable at this direct URL but not yet linked from any nav menu or footer, and not yet announced.

Part of a four-part companion set staged together today — this page: The Author’s Exit. Also in the set: A Letter to a Possible Steward · Global Resonance · Reflection: The Author’s Principles.

Blind-spot 9 asked: what would make you stop, or hand it over, or walk away? Principle 30 is now canon, and the charter schedules the work's independence from you. This is the personal half only you can finish — drafted as a skeleton, private, not for the public tree until and unless you choose. Fill the brackets in your own words; the framework says to write this while leaving is still easy, and tonight it is.

Source note: this document originally carried the instruction “private, not for the public tree until and unless you choose” (2026-07-19). The operator ruled the PC3 essay set GO for staged publication on 2026-08-06 — see the banner above. The bracketed placeholders below are preserved exactly as authored, unfilled: this is a skeleton/template, not a completed personal statement, and nothing has been written into the brackets on the author's behalf.

To whoever stewards this after me —

I built this because [ your reason, one honest sentence — not the mission statement, the real one ].

I will know it is time to step back when [ the charter's triggers are the mechanics; this is the felt version — the sign you would recognize in yourself ].

I will know it is time to stop entirely — to let it be composted — if [ your personal kill criterion, beyond the charter's three: the evidence that would tell you this was the wrong tree to plant ].

What I most fear this becoming is [ name it — the fear is the guard ].

What I most hope it makes possible is [ vivid in values, loose in means ].

If I am gone before this is grown: the verbatim bank holds my words exactly as I gave them; trust the written record over anyone's memory of me, including my own late revisions. The work was always meant to belong to the people who use it. Take it. Improve it. Compost what fails. Keep the four ethics; argue with everything else.

And to the one reading this in that far season: thank you for carrying it further than I could. That was the design.

— [ signed, when ready ]

One seed remains yours alone: roughly six of your two dozen principles are still unwritten anywhere but in you. Whatever else the queue holds, capture those — they are the part of this work no fleet, no model, and no framework can recover if lost. Everything else we built today is backed up. Those aren't.

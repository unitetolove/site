Mandate & statutory basis

Milton Public Library is a statutory public library board operating under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 — fetched via text-render fallback and verified 2026-07-30, title confirmed as "Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44"; registry row notes: "board subpage unreachable this session (TLS cert mismatch on mpl.on.ca); homepage confirmed reachable"). The Act requires council-established boards to have at least five council-appointed members (s. 9(1)) who cannot form a majority (s. 10(2)), and to provide "comprehensive and efficient public library service" (s. 20).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Library, branded publicly as "Milton Public Library — Be Inspired," is an agency of the Town of Milton (registry row: milt-milton-public-library, parent_org field), operating a Main Library and Sherwood and Beaty branches plus hold-locker pickup points, under the stated mission "Empowering the community to Read. Learn. Innovate. Connect." (beinspired.ca, fetched). The 2025 Annual Report records 655,536 branch visits, 64,068 members and 3,512 programs delivered that year (doc_id: milt-milton-public-library-ar-2025).

Governance & reporting line

Governance combines a Library Board and management team; the 2025 Annual Report names Sana Malik as Board Chair and Sarah Douglas-Murray as Chief Executive Officer (doc_id: milt-milton-public-library-ar-2025).

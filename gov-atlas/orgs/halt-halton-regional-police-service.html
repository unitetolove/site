Halton Regional Police Service - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-STRAT-EVOL-d1 Wave 1 / 2026-07-30 / registry row: halt-halton-regional-police-service (research/atlas/government_orgs.csv) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Halton Regional Police Service (HRPS) is a municipal police service maintained under the Community Safety and Policing Act, 2019, S.O. 2019, c. 1, Sched. 1 (CSPA), Ontario's consolidated policing statute, which replaced the Police Services Act (fetch-verified at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/19c01). Under CSPA Part IV ("Municipal Policing and Police Service Boards"), a police service board maintains a police service and appoints its members, including the chief of police (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/19c01; https://haltonpoliceboard.ca/roles-and-responsibilities/).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

HRPS delivers front-line policing — patrol, criminal investigations, traffic enforcement, victim services, and specialized units (e.g., Emergency Response, Search Incident Response Team) — across the four Halton Region municipalities of Burlington, Halton Hills, Milton and Oakville (registry row, org_id: halt-halton-regional-police-service; https://www.haltonpolice.ca/about-us/). Current strength is more than 800 uniformed officers, 300+ civilian staff and roughly 200 volunteers (https://www.haltonpolice.ca/about-us/).

Governance & reporting line

HRPS reports to the Halton Police Board (registry parent_org: halt-halton-police-board), the civilian board that under CSPA Part IV appoints and directs the Chief of Police, sets service policies, and adopts the strategic plan and diversity plan, while being statutorily barred from directing specific operational or day-to-day policing decisions (https://haltonpoliceboard.ca/roles-and-responsibilities/). The Board in turn operates under the current 2024-2027 Strategic Plan (registry row strategy_current_url: https://haltonpoliceboard.ca/strategicplan/).

Budget scale

~$187.5M operating budget for 2024, a 9.5% increase over 2023 (2024 Annual Report, https://haltonpoliceboard.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/AnnualReport_2024_HRPS.pdf).

Institutional history

HRPS began operations on January 1, 1974 with 205 sworn officers and 45 civilian staff, coinciding with the creation of the Regional Municipality of Halton (2024 Annual Report, https://haltonpoliceboard.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/AnnualReport_2024_HRPS.pdf).

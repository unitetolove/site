Mandate & statutory basis

Scugog Memorial Public Library is a statutory public library board operating under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 — fetched via text-render fallback and verified 2026-07-30, title confirmed as "Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44"). The Act requires council-established boards to have at least five council-appointed members (s. 9(1)) who cannot form a majority (s. 10(2)), and to provide "comprehensive and efficient public library service" (s. 20).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Library is an agency of the Township of Scugog (registry row: scug-scugog-public-library, parent_org field). The Board maintains "Library Policies" that "meet provincial and local regulations related to public policy" (scugog.ca/council-and-administration/committees/library-board/, fetched). Its 2024 Annual Report is the registry-listed primary document (doc_id: scug-scugog-public-library-ar-2024, flagged in the registry as ⚠️ VERIFY:image-pdf-canva-export).

Governance & reporting line

The 2022-2026 Board has seven council-appointed members — Catie Sahadath, Donna Ankrett, Alexson Philipiah, Jesse Gillis, Jordan Donald, Navjeet Sidhu and Robert Rock — supported by Manager of Public Services Kate Davis and CEO Sarah White (scugog.ca/council-and-administration/committees/library-board/, fetched; registry row notes: "council-appointed 7-member library board 2022-2026 term"). The Board meets monthly, usually the third Thursday, with meetings open to the public (scugog.ca, fetched).

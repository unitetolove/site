Mandate & statutory basis

Scugog Memorial Public Library is a statutory public library board operating under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 — fetched via text-render fallback and verified 2026-07-30, title confirmed as "Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44"). The Act requires council-established boards to have at least five council-appointed members (s. 9(1)) who cannot form a majority (s. 10(2)), and to provide "comprehensive and efficient public library service" (s. 20).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Library is an agency of the Township of Scugog (registry row: scug-scugog-public-library, parent_org field). The Board maintains "Library Policies" that "meet provincial and local regulations related to public policy" (scugog.ca/council-and-administration/committees/library-board/, fetched).

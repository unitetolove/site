Brock Township Public Library - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: broc-brock-public-library (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Governed under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44, consolidated text at ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 (link fetch-verified this review). The library's board page notes meetings are "open to the public as per the Public Libraries Act" (brocklibraries.ca/library-board/, fetched this review); Act s. 20 requires the board to provide "a comprehensive and efficient public library service" (ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Delivers public library service to the Township of Brock across its branches, including Beaverton, Sunderland and the Timothy Findley Memorial Branch in Cannington (brocklibraries.ca/about/, fetched this review).

Governance & reporting line

A council-referenced Public Library Board of 8 members for the 2023-2026 term - 2 Council Representatives (one as Chairperson) and 4 Municipal Appointees - working with a Chief Executive Officer who serves as Secretary-Treasurer (brocklibraries.ca/library-board/, fetched this review; registry row registry id: broc-brock-public-library, parent_org: broc-township-of-brock). Regular meetings held monthly except July/August (same source).

Budget scale

Budget scale: ⚠️ not yet confirmed against an original source. No current operating-budget figure was located on the library's public web pages this review; historical funding on record is a $7,000 Carnegie Foundation construction grant awarded August 28, 1910 for the Beaverton branch (brocklibraries.ca/about/, fetched this review), which is not a current budget figure.

Institutional history

Traces to an 1853 reading room in the Beaverton courthouse, which became a Mechanics' Institute in 1890; the Beaverton Carnegie Library opened in 1913 with Carnegie Foundation funding, and the system later added the Sunderland branch (dedicated June 11, 1972) and the Cannington (Timothy Findley Memorial) branch, reopened in its current location in April 2011 (brocklibraries.ca/about/, fetched this review).

Ministry of Intergovernmental Affairs - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: on-ministry-intergovernmental-affairs (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The Ministry continues in law under the Ministry of Intergovernmental Affairs Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. M.28 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90m28, fetched and verified), whose s.2 provides that "the ministry of the public service known in English as the Ministry of Intergovernmental Affairs... is continued" (statute, fetched). Its mandate is to "provide strategic advice and analysis on matters of intergovernmental relations, international affairs and protocol" (https://www.ontario.ca/page/ministry-intergovernmental-affairs, fetched).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Under s.5 of the Act, the Minister is responsible for advising Executive Council on provincial programs and activities related to federal-provincial, inter-provincial and international affairs, centralizing Ontario's intergovernmental relationships under one portfolio (statute, fetched). The Ministry's Office of International Relations and Protocol is Ontario's primary contact point for over 100 foreign governments represented in the province, managing protocol matters and official state visits (https://www.ontario.ca/page/ministry-intergovernmental-affairs, fetched).

Governance & reporting line

The Minister presides over and manages the Ministry and may delegate powers and duties to the Deputy Minister or other ministry officers in writing (statute, s.6, fetched); s.7 provides Crown liability protection (under the Crown Liability and Proceedings Act, 2019) for staff acting in good faith (statute, fetched).

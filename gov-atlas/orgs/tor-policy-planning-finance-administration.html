Policy, Planning, Finance and Administration - backgrounder

Mandate & statutory basis

Policy, Planning, Finance and Administration (PPFA) is an operating division of the City of Toronto with no dedicated founding statute; its authority rests on the City's broad organizational and interpretive powers under the City of Toronto Act, 2006, S.O. 2006, c. 11, Sched. A, ss. 6(1) and 7 (statute text fetched and confirmed at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/06c11). PPFA is not a public-facing service division but an internal shared-services division: it "provides centralized financial and administrative shared services to Infrastructure Services (IS) and Development and Growth Services (DGS) divisions and Deputy City Managers" (tor-policy-planning-finance-administration-bud-2026, 1d9d992872e2), consistent with the City's own division page describing it as providing "centralized financial and administrative support to the Deputy City Manager and Infrastructure and Development Services programs" (toronto.ca PPFA page, fetched this review).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Per the 2026 Program Summary, PPFA delivers Corporate Leadership and Organizational Effectiveness (strategic advice, planning, coordination and reporting to IS/DGS divisions), Financial Management (financial planning, purchasing, contract management, payables coordination), and Program Support (public consultation, complement management, payroll and administrative services) to eight IS/DGS divisions and their Deputy City Managers' offices (tor-policy-planning-finance-administration-bud-2026, 1d9d992872e2).

Governance & reporting line

Executive Director: Annalisa Mignardi (toronto.ca PPFA staff-directory page, fetched this review). Registry parent organization: tor-deputy-city-manager-infrastructure-services (registry row tor-policy-planning-finance-administration), consistent with the division's own stated service scope covering both the Infrastructure Services (IS) and Development and Growth Services (DGS) clusters under a shared Deputy City Manager reporting structure.

Budget scale

2026 Operating Budget: $20.876M gross, $13.667M revenue, $7.209M net, 216.1 approved positions (37.0 capital, 179.1 operating); no 10-Year Capital Budget and Plan (tor-policy-planning-finance-administration-bud-2026, 1d9d992872e2).

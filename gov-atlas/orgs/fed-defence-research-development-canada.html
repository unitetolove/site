Defence Research and Development Canada - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: fed-defence-research-development-canada (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

DRDC has no standalone enabling statute of its own; it operates within the Department of National Defence, whose department and Canadian Armed Forces are constituted under the National Defence Act, R.S.C., 1985, c. N-5 (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/N-5/index.html - link verified, title/citation confirmed; note that a direct text search of this Act's contents did not surface a section naming DRDC specifically, consistent with DRDC being an internal departmental agency rather than a separately-constituted statutory body). DRDC's own stated mandate is to be "Canada's science, technology and innovation leader, trusted advisor, collaborative partner, and knowledge integrator for defence and security," developing technical solutions and advice for DND, the Canadian Armed Forces, other federal departments, and the safety/security community (canada.ca/en/defence-research-development.html, fetched via https://www.canada.ca/en/defence-research-development.html). Registry row (registry id fed-defence-research-development-canada) lists no strategy_current_url, and its notes state "no separate current plan located; DRDC reports within DND's departmental plan" - consistent with non-standalone statutory status.

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Delivers defence and security science, technology, and innovation - research, technical advice, and knowledge integration - to DND/the Canadian Armed Forces and partner federal departments, working with academia, industry, and allied nations, from seven research centres across Canada (canada.ca/en/defence-research-development.html, as above).

Governance & reporting line

DRDC sits within DND and reports through the Department of National Defence to the Minister of National Defence (canada.ca/en/defence-research-development.html, as above; registry row lists parent_org as fed-national-defence). ⚠️ still being checked - the specific instrument establishing DRDC's internal governance (e.g., its status as a Special Operating Agency and any Ministerial or Treasury Board framework document) was not located at a fetchable primary URL this review.

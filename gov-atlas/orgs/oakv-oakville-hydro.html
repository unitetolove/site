Oakville Hydro Electricity Distribution Inc. - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: oakv-oakville-hydro (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Oakville Hydro Electricity Distribution Inc. was incorporated in 2000 in preparation for the deregulation of Ontario's electricity market (which took effect May 2002), under the market-restructuring framework of the Electricity Act, 1998, S.O. 1998, c. 15, Sched. A (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/98e15, verified; Oakville Hydro history page, https://www.oakvillehydro.com/about-us/about-oakville-hydro/history-oakville-hydro). It is licensed by the Ontario Energy Board to distribute electricity in the Town of Oakville under the Ontario Energy Board Act, 1998, and as a condition of licence must comply with the OEB's Affiliate Relationships Code, Distribution System Code, Retail Settlement Code and Standard Supply Service Code (https://www.oakvillehydro.com/about-us/about-oakville-hydro/regulatory-affairs).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Oakville Hydro is the Town-of-Oakville-owned "wires company" and default electricity supplier for approximately 78,000 accounts, delivering electricity distribution service within Town of Oakville boundaries under OEB rate regulation (WebSearch summary of Oakville Hydro corporate materials; https://www.oakvillehydro.com/about-us/about-oakville-hydro/regulatory-affairs; registry row oakv-oakville-hydro).

Governance & reporting line

Oakville Hydro operates as a wholly-owned corporation of Oakville Enterprises Corporation (OEC), the Town of Oakville's holding company, governed by an eight-member Board of Directors chaired by David Brennan, drawing on expertise in finance, public policy, electricity services, risk management and labour relations (https://www.oakvillehydro.com/about-us/about-oakville-hydro/board-directors; registry row oakv-oakville-hydro, parent_org oakv-town-of-oakville). It is rate-regulated by the Ontario Energy Board (https://www.oakvillehydro.com/about-us/about-oakville-hydro/regulatory-affairs). ⚠️ Still being checked: the direct governance chain between OEC and the Town of Oakville council (e.g., shareholder-declaration mechanics) was not independently confirmed this review.

Budget scale

⚠️ still being checked - not fully grounded this review. No consolidated revenue/budget figure was found on Oakville Hydro's own site. The OEB's 2023 Electricity Distributor Scorecard reports a "total cost per customer" of $871 in 2023, up from $775 in 2022 (OEB 2023 Scorecard – Oakville Hydro Electricity Distribution Inc., https://www.oeb.ca/documents/scorecard/2023/Scorecard%20-%20Oakville%20Hydro%20Electricity%20Distribution%20Inc..pdf). Applied to the ~78,000-account base cited above, this implies an order-of-magnitude total capital-plus-operating cost base in the tens of millions of dollars annually; this is a derived estimate, not a reported consolidated budget figure.

Institutional history

Traces to 1889 (first electric lights near Oakville's Harbour Grove) and the 1908 Oakville Water and Light Commission; predecessor bodies were renamed Trafalgar Public Utilities (1947) and Oakville Public Utilities Commission (1962) before incorporation as Oakville Hydro in 2000 ahead of provincial electricity-market deregulation in 2002 (https://www.oakvillehydro.com/about-us/about-oakville-hydro/history-oakville-hydro). It has not merged into the regional Alectra Utilities consolidation, remaining independently municipally owned (registry row oakv-oakville-hydro, notes field).

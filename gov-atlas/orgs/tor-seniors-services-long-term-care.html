Seniors Services and Long-Term Care - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: tor-seniors-services-long-term-care (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Seniors Services and Long-Term Care (SSLTC) is a City of Toronto public-service division, not a body created by its own enabling statute; it exists under the City's general organizational authority as a municipal corporation under the City of Toronto Act, 2006, S.O. 2006, c. 11, Sched. A (consolidated text verified at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/06c11, fetched 2026-07-30), and its division listing is attested by the City's staff directory of divisions (registry row: tor-seniors-services-long-term-care; source authority: https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/accountability-operations-customer-service/city-administration/staff-directory-divisions-and-customer-service/). The ten long-term care homes it directly operates are licensed and regulated under the Fixing Long-Term Care Act, 2021, S.O. 2021, c. 39, Sched. 1 (consolidated text verified at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/21f39, fetched 2026-07-30).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

SSLTC is "responsible for service planning and strategic coordination of City services for seniors," directly operating ten Long-Term Care Homes (LTCHs) providing 24-hour resident-focused care, and delivering community support programs — Homemakers and Nurses Services, Supportive Housing, and Adult Day Programs — to advance the Toronto Seniors Strategy and help seniors "age in place" (2026 Program Summary: Seniors Services and Long-Term Care, https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/8edc-2026-Public-Book-SSLTC-V1.pdf, fetched 2026-07-30). The division's own page adds that it delivers homecare, tenancy supports, and adult day programs, guided by CARE values (Compassion, Accountability, Respect, Excellence) (https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/accountability-operations-customer-service/city-administration/staff-directory-divisions-and-customer-service/seniors-services-long-term-care/, fetched 2026-07-30).

Governance & reporting line

SSLTC is led by a General Manager (Nicole Welch, per the division's website) sitting within the office of the Deputy City Manager, Community Development and Social Services (registry row: parent_org tor-deputy-city-manager-community-development-social), reporting up through the City Manager to City Council under the general municipal governance framework of the City of Toronto Act, 2006 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/06c11). The directly-operated long-term care homes are licensed to the City as operator under the Fixing Long-Term Care Act, 2021 and are subject to inspection by Ontario's Ministry of Long-Term Care (statute, https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/21f39).

Budget scale

2026 gross expenditures of ~$451.1 million and a Net Operating Budget of $115.709 million, up $19.620 million (20.4%) year-over-year (2026 Program Summary: Seniors Services and Long-Term Care, https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/8edc-2026-Public-Book-SSLTC-V1.pdf, fetched 2026-07-30).

Institutional history

The division operated as "Long-Term Care Homes & Services" (LTCHS) at least through its 2018 annual report and had been renamed "Seniors Services and Long-Term Care" by its 2021-2022 annual reports (registry doc_ids: tor-seniors-services-long-term-care-ar-2018, tor-seniors-services-long-term-care-ar-2021, tor-seniors-services-long-term-care-ar-2022); ⚠️ still being checked - the exact year and administrative instrument of the rename were not independently confirmed this review.

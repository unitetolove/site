Whitby Public Library - backgrounder

Mandate & statutory basis

Whitby Public Library operates under the Ontario Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (verified at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44, HTTP 200, fetched 2026-07-30), which requires municipal library boards to "provide...a comprehensive and efficient public library service reflecting the community's unique needs" (s. 20). The registry confirms Whitby's board is a "statutory library board" (registry row whit-whitby-public-library, notes field).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Board sets policy and oversees service delivery; a CEO carries out day-to-day operations (https://whitbylibrary.ca/library-board, fetched 2026-07-30). The library maintains a public strategic plan for 2024-2028, alongside published board agendas, minutes, and bylaws (https://whitbylibrary.ca/library-board, fetched 2026-07-30).

Governance & reporting line

The Board has ten members: a Mayor's designate plus eight citizen volunteers appointed by Whitby Town Council for a term aligned to the 2023-2026 municipal election cycle; current leadership is Chair Liam Nichols and Vice-Chair Akhil Kapoor (https://whitbylibrary.ca/library-board, fetched 2026-07-30). This matches the registry's description of "statutory library board 10 members including mayor's designate" (registry row, notes field). Parent municipality is the Town of Whitby (registry row, parent_org: whit-town-of-whitby).

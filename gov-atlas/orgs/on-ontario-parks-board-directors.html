Ontario Parks Board of Directors - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-08-04 / registry row: on-ontario-parks-board-directors (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The Board is an advisory body operating under the Ministry of the Environment, Conservation and Parks, providing "independent advice and recommendations to the Minister" on Ontario's provincial parks and conservation reserves system (https://www.ontarioparks.ca/board, fetched). ⚠️ Still being checked: no specific enabling statute (as distinct from the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006, S.O. 2006, c. 12, which governs the parks system itself rather than naming this Board) was located/confirmed this review.

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Board advises on balancing recreation opportunities with ecological integrity, expanding public access and visitation, public engagement/awareness initiatives, and policy direction/overall strategy for the system of 340+ provincial parks and 295+ conservation reserves (https://www.ontarioparks.ca/board, fetched). Per the registry: "Advisory board only, no separate site/plan" (registry row on-ontario-parks-board-directors, notes field); its own page as read this review lists past advisory projects such as cottage-lease policy and the Algonquin logging footprint, but no annual report or strategic-plan series.

Governance & reporting line

Up to 15 volunteer members, including a Chair and Vice-Chair, are appointed by the Lieutenant Governor in Council through the Public Appointments Secretariat (https://www.ontarioparks.ca/board, fetched). The Board reports to the Minister of the Environment, Conservation and Parks (same source). ⚠️ Still being checked: current Chair/Vice-Chair and full membership roster not confirmed this review.

Budget scale

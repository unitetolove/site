Uxbridge Public Library - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: uxbr-uxbridge-public-library (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Uxbridge Public Library operates under the Ontario Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (verified at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44, HTTP 200, fetched 2026-07-30), which lets a municipal council establish a public library by by-law (s. 3) and requires its board to provide "a comprehensive and efficient public library service reflecting the community's unique needs" (s. 20). The Library Board itself explicitly states it "operates under the Ontario Public Libraries Act" (https://uxlib.com/administration/library-board/, fetched 2026-07-30).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Board "is responsible for making policies for the operation of the Library; for obtaining adequate funding for Library materials, services and programs," while a CEO/Chief Librarian runs day-to-day operations (https://uxlib.com/administration/library-board/, fetched 2026-07-30). The Board works through three standing committees: Finance (budgeting/fiscal oversight), Governance and HR (CEO appraisal, Board policy), and Community (identifying community needs) (same source).

Governance & reporting line

Twelve members serve four-year terms aligned to the municipal election cycle, appointed by Township of Uxbridge Council; the current roster includes six citizen appointees, a Chair, and two ward councillors (Gordon Shreeve, Zed Pickering) (https://uxlib.com/administration/library-board/, fetched 2026-07-30). Parent municipality is the Township of Uxbridge (registry row uxbr-uxbridge-public-library, parent_org: uxbr-township-of-uxbridge); the registry's source-authority page also confirms a "township-appointed library board" (registry row, source authority field, https://uxlib.com/administration/library-board/).

Institutional history

The library traces to the Uxbridge Mechanics Institute and Library Association, founded January 7, 1859; it was renamed "Uxbridge Public Library" in 1895 under new provincial legislation and became a free public library under a Board of Management on March 1, 1898 (https://uxlib.com/about-us/history/, fetched 2026-07-30).

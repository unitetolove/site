King Township Public Library - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: king-township-public-library (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

King Township Public Library is a statutory public library board created under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 — fetched via text-render fallback and verified 2026-07-30, title confirmed as "Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44"). Under the Act, a municipal council may by by-law establish a public library board (s. 3), and every board must have at least five members appointed by council (s. 9(1)) who cannot form a majority if they are themselves council members (s. 10(2)); boards must provide "a comprehensive and efficient public library service" (s. 20).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Library operates as an agency of the Township of King (registry row: king-township-public-library, parent_org field), delivering public library service to King Township residents. Its Board is responsible for policy governance and advocacy, working to "promote and represent the Library to both existing and potential stakeholders" and support its strategic vision and intellectual-freedom principles (kinglibrary.ca/library-board-administration, fetched). Day-to-day operations are led by a Chief Executive Officer with managers over patron experience, branch operations, collection development and digital services (kinglibrary.ca/library-board-administration, fetched).

Governance & reporting line

The Board for the 2023-2026 term has 10 members: Chair Caroline Anderson, Vice-Chair Aleisha Richards, Finance Chair Michael Tedesco, Mayor Steve Pellegrini, two councillors (David Boyd, Jordan Cescolini), and community appointees including Irene deGeus, Rose Grimaldi, Danielle Donadio and Patti Skrypek (kinglibrary.ca/library-board-administration, fetched; registry row notes: "statutory library board of 10 incl mayor plus 2 councillors"). Under the Public Libraries Act, Township Council appoints board members following each municipal election and fills vacancies as they arise (kinglibrary.ca/library-board-administration, fetched).

Budget scale

~$2.4M annual operating budget: the Board's KPMG-audited financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2025 report total revenue of $2,435,297 and total expenses of $2,493,683, against municipal grants of $2,342,639 from the Township of King (document id: king-township-public-library-fs-2025).

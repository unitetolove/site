Blue Water Bridge Canada - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: fed-blue-water-bridge-canada (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Blue Water Bridge Canada is the Canadian half of the Blue Water Bridge crossing at Point Edward, Ontario, owned and operated by its parent, The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited (FBCL), a parent Crown corporation listed in Part I of Schedule III to the Financial Administration Act, R.S.C., 1985, c. F-11 (consolidated text verified at https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/F-11/FullText.html, Schedule III entry "The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited" confirmed by direct fetch). FBCL itself is constituted as a corporation under the Canada Business Corporations Act (https://federalbridge.ca/corporate-structure/).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

FBCL's mandate is "to engage in a range of activities to ensure that the international bridges and related infrastructure in its portfolio are safe, secure, efficient, environmentally sound and well-maintained" (registry row fed-blue-water-bridge-canada; search-confirmed via FBCL's own materials). Blue Water Bridge is FBCL's second-busiest commercial crossing, carrying 35-45% commercial vehicle traffic and providing a geographically separate redundant trade route from the Windsor-area crossings (FBCL 2025-2030 Corporate Plan Summary, p.69, https://www.federalbridge.ca/wp-content/uploads/Corporate-Plan-Summary.pdf).

Governance & reporting line

FBCL operates under a Board of Directors with a President and CEO managing day-to-day operations, and reports to the Minister of Transport, who must approve FBCL's corporate plans and budgets before they are tabled in Parliament (https://federalbridge.ca/corporate-structure/).

Budget scale

Budget scale: ⚠️ not yet confirmed against an original source. FBCL's 2025-2030 Corporate Plan Summary contains consolidated financial statements with a "TOTAL REVENUE" line, but Blue Water Bridge's individual segment revenue is not broken out in extractable form from the corporate plan document, and border-related disruption is noted to have caused "over $40 million in revenue losses" across FBCL's crossings without a clean current-year total figure (https://www.federalbridge.ca/wp-content/uploads/Corporate-Plan-Summary.pdf).

Institutional history

The current Federal Bridge Corporation Limited structure resulted from two 2015 amalgamations under the Canada Business Corporations Act: FBCL merged with the St. Mary's River Bridge Company on January 27, 2015, and the resulting entity merged with the Blue Water Bridge Authority on February 1, 2015, bringing Blue Water Bridge Canada into FBCL's portfolio (https://federalbridge.ca/corporate-structure/; registry row fed-blue-water-bridge-canada).

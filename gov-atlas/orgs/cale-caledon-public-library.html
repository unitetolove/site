Mandate & statutory basis

Governed under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44, consolidated text at ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 (link fetch-verified this pass). The library's board page states it "operates under the authority of the Ontario Public Libraries Act" (caledon.library.on.ca/about-your-library/library-board-leadership-team/, fetched this pass); Act s. 20 requires the board to provide "a comprehensive and efficient public library service" (ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Delivers public library service to the Town of Caledon; the Board is responsible for "policies guiding operations; for obtaining funding for Library materials, services and programs; and for raising awareness in the community" (caledon.library.on.ca/about-your-library/library-board-leadership-team/, fetched this pass).

Governance & reporting line

A statutory library board of nine members appointed by Town Council for four-year terms aligned with the municipal election cycle, including two sitting Town Councillors (Cosimo Napoli and Dave Sheen at time of research) (caledon.library.on.ca/about-your-library/library-board-leadership-team/, fetched this pass; registry row org_id: cale-caledon-public-library, parent_org: cale-town-of-caledon). The CEO/Chief Librarian also serves as Secretary-Treasurer to the Board (same source).

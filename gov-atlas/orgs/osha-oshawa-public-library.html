Oshawa Public Library - backgrounder

Mandate & statutory basis

Oshawa Public Library is a statutory public library board operating under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 — fetched via text-render fallback and verified 2026-07-30, title confirmed as "Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44"; registry row notes: "statutory library board per Public Libraries Act"). The Act requires council-established boards to have at least five council-appointed members (s. 9(1)) who cannot form a majority (s. 10(2)), and to provide "comprehensive and efficient public library service" (s. 20).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Library is an agency of the City of Oshawa (registry row: osha-oshawa-public-library, parent_org field). Its Board functions as "a governance board that oversees policies governing the operation and programs of the Library as well as advocating for the support of library programs and services" (oshawalibrary.ca/library-board/, fetched). Operations are led by a McLaughlin branch and other locations across the city; the 2025 Annual Report and audited financial statements are the library's principal registry-listed documents (doc_ids: osha-oshawa-public-library-ar-2025, osha-oshawa-public-library-fs-2025).

Governance & reporting line

The Board has 11 trustees plus one City Councillor representative; current Chair is Eric Guernsey and Vice-Chair Marco Osso, with Councillor Tito-Dante Marimpietri as the Council appointee (oshawalibrary.ca/library-board/, fetched). The Board meets monthly (with some months skipped) with open-session minutes posted publicly (oshawalibrary.ca/library-board/, fetched).

Budget scale

~$10.4M annual operating budget: the Board's Deloitte-audited financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2025 report total revenue of $10,356,005 (including a $9,549,800 municipal contribution from the City of Oshawa) against total expenses of $10,233,538, producing an annual surplus of $122,467 (doc_id: osha-oshawa-public-library-fs-2025).

Institutional history

The Library operates today as a statutory board of the City of Oshawa under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (registry row: osha-oshawa-public-library); its specific founding year and any prior reorganizations are ⚠️ VERIFY - not grounded this pass.

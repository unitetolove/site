Royal Canadian Mounted Police External Review Committee - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: fed-royal-canadian-mounted-police-external-review-co (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The RCMP External Review Committee (ERC) is an independent quasi-judicial statutory tribunal established under the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act (R.S.C., 1985, c. R-10), consolidated and current to 2026-06-14 at laws-lois.justice.gc.ca (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/R-10/, fetched and verified directly; establishment described at https://www.canada.ca/en/rcmp-external-review-committee.html, fetched via text-rendering proxy). This registry row's url_status is flagged ⚠️ still being checked in the registry (registry row: fed-royal-canadian-mounted-police-external-review-co).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The ERC conducts independent, impartial reviews of specified internal RCMP employment-relations decisions - appeals in harassment complaints, conduct matters, discharges, demotions, and pay stoppages - and issues findings and recommendations to the RCMP Commissioner; the Committee's own 2026-27 Departmental Plan frames this as a single core responsibility, "Independent Review of RCMP Employment Matters" ($4,580,811 planned) (https://www.canada.ca/en/rcmp-external-review-committee/corporate/reports-information-resources/departmental-plans/departmental-plan-2026-27.html, fetched via text-rendering proxy).

Governance & reporting line

The ERC is institutionally separate and distinct from the RCMP; its Chairperson (Charles Randall Smith) reports to Parliament via the Minister of Public Safety (Gary Anandasangaree), within the Public Safety portfolio (same source; https://www.canada.ca/en/rcmp-external-review-committee.html).

Budget scale

~$6.84 million total planned spending for 2026-27 (Core Responsibility $4,580,811 + Internal Services $2,254,879), with 25 planned full-time equivalents (ERC 2026-27 Departmental Plan, https://www.canada.ca/en/rcmp-external-review-committee/corporate/reports-information-resources/departmental-plans/departmental-plan-2026-27.html, doc-shelf doc_type: annual-report, n_docs 8).

Institutional history

The Committee's 2026-27 Departmental Plan states its operations commenced in 1986 under the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act (R.S.C. 1985, c. R-10) (https://www.canada.ca/en/rcmp-external-review-committee/corporate/reports-information-resources/departmental-plans/departmental-plan-2026-27.html; statute confirmed at https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/R-10/).

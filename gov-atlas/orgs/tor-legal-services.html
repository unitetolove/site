Legal Services - backgrounder

Backgrounder / this library's government-strategy research Batch 12 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: tor-legal-services (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Legal Services is an operating division of the City of Toronto with no dedicated founding statute; its authority rests on the City's broad interpretive powers and natural-person legal capacity under the City of Toronto Act, 2006, S.O. 2006, c. 11, Sched. A, ss. 6(1) and 7 (statute text fetched and confirmed at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/06c11). The City's own division page describes Legal Services as "the City's in-house law firm," providing legal advice and representation to Council, City divisions and agencies — explicitly noting lawyers in the division "do not provide advice to members of the public" (toronto.ca Legal Services page, fetched this review).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Per the division's 2026 Program Summary, Legal Services delivers three services: Civil Litigation ("protect the City's interests through legal proceedings involving various levels of Courts and Tribunals"), Prosecution (disputing charges and tickets under provincial and municipal law, serving "City Council, City Divisions, Agencies and Boards and Individuals charged with offences"), and Solicitor (strategic legal advice to Council, staff and agencies) (tor-legal-services-bud-2026, 300ef9f7a5d8). The division also functions as the City's risk-management and mitigation-strategy advisor (same source).

Governance & reporting line

City Solicitor: Wendy Walberg (toronto.ca Legal Services staff-directory page, fetched this review).

Polar Knowledge Canada - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-Q10-WAVE1-d1 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: fed-polar-knowledge-canada (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Polar Knowledge Canada (POLAR) was created by the Canadian High Arctic Research Station Act, S.C. 2014, c. 39, s. 145 (assented to 2014-12-16), which came into force June 1, 2015 (statute text and citation confirmed at https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-17.8/FullText.html, fetched this review; in-force date corroborated by https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2015/06/minister-valcourt-announces-establishment-new-federal-organization-polar-knowledge-canada.html). The Act's purpose provisions direct POLAR to "advance knowledge of the Canadian Arctic in order to improve economic opportunities, environmental stewardship and the quality of life" of Northerners and Canadians generally, to promote circumpolar knowledge development, to strengthen Canada's Arctic leadership, and to establish a research hub in the Arctic (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-17.8/FullText.html).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

POLAR's science and technology research program serves Canada and the world, advancing Arctic knowledge to improve economic opportunities, environmental stewardship, and Northerners' quality of life (https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2015/06/minister-valcourt-announces-establishment-new-federal-organization-polar-knowledge-canada.html). It operates from the Canadian High Arctic Research Station campus in Cambridge Bay, Nunavut, with additional regional offices in Whitehorse and Ottawa (Polar Knowledge Canada 2024-25 Departmental Results Report, https://www.canada.ca/en/polar-knowledge/reports/departmental-results-report/2024-2025/departmental-results-report-2024-2025-full-plan.html, fetched this review). Its single core responsibility, "Polar Science and Knowledge," encompasses Arctic and Antarctic research, knowledge exchange, and researcher development (same source).

Governance & reporting line

POLAR is led by a nine-member Board of Directors, including a Chairperson and Vice-chairperson, appointed by the Governor in Council to part-time terms of up to five years and eligible for a second term; the Board is responsible for operational planning and budget oversight, and approves the agency's annual budgets and science plans (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-17.8/FullText.html; Departmental Results Report 2024-25, https://www.canada.ca/en/polar-knowledge/reports/departmental-results-report/2024-2025/departmental-results-report-2024-2025-full-plan.html). A President, accountable to the Board, is responsible for day-to-day operations and reports to the Minister responsible for Northern Affairs within the Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs Canada portfolio (same sources).

Budget scale

For fiscal year 2024-25, POLAR reported total actual spending of $36,467,311, comprising $26,633,137 for its Polar Science and Knowledge core responsibility and $9,834,174 for internal services, supported by 94 full-time equivalents (down from 103 FTE in 2023-24, attributed to Northern-labour-market recruitment challenges) (Polar Knowledge Canada 2024-25 Departmental Results Report, https://www.canada.ca/en/polar-knowledge/reports/departmental-results-report/2024-2025/departmental-results-report-2024-2025-full-plan.html, fetched this review; registry doc id fed-polar-knowledge-canada-drr-2025). Spending increased from $34.1 million in 2022-23, driven in part by one-time program-integrity funding from Budget 2024 (same source).

Institutional history

POLAR was established as a new federal research organization when the Canadian High Arctic Research Station Act came into force on June 1, 2015, merging the mandate and resources of the former Canadian Polar Commission with the pan-northern Science and Technology program under the Canadian High Arctic Research Station initiative (https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2015/06/minister-valcourt-announces-establishment-new-federal-organization-polar-knowledge-canada.html; registry row registry id: fed-polar-knowledge-canada).

Business Development Bank of Canada - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: fed-business-development-bank-canada (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

BDC is established by the Business Development Bank of Canada Act, S.C. 1995, c. 28, confirmed current to 2026-06-14 on Justice Laws (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/B-9.9/index.html). It describes itself as "the bank for Canadian entrepreneurs," supporting economic growth by providing financing, capital and advisory services tailored to small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs) while "complementing the role of the private sector" (BDC Corporate Plan Summary 2025-26 to 2029-30, Executive Summary, https://www.bdc.ca/globalassets/digizuite/58625-summary-bdc-corporate-plan-26-30.pdf).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

BDC provides term financing, securitization, loan guarantees, growth & transition capital, venture capital, and advisory services to Canadian SMEs, and administers government-directed credit-availability initiatives (e.g., COVID-era relief facilities and the Canada Digital Adoption Program) (BDC Corporate Plan Summary 2025-26 to 2029-30, https://www.bdc.ca/globalassets/digizuite/58625-summary-bdc-corporate-plan-26-30.pdf). Of Canada's 1.22 million employer businesses, 99.7% are SMEs, accounting for over 48% of GDP and almost 64% of private-sector employment, per BDC's own environmental scan (BDC Corporate Plan Summary 2025-26 to 2029-30, citing ISED Key Small Business Statistics 2024).

Governance & reporting line

BDC is governed by a Board of Directors and reports to Parliament through the Minister of Small Business; its 2025-26 to 2029-30 Corporate Plan was "approved by the Governor in Council on March 6, 2025, on the recommendation of the Minister of Small Business" (BDC Corporate Plan Summary 2025-26 to 2029-30, https://www.bdc.ca/globalassets/digizuite/58625-summary-bdc-corporate-plan-26-30.pdf).

Budget scale

Consolidated portfolio (financing, growth & transition capital, venture capital, capital incentive programs, credit availability) of approximately $47.2 billion at fiscal year-end 2024, projected to grow to approximately $67.0 billion by fiscal year-end 2030 (BDC Corporate Plan Summary 2025-26 to 2029-30, Table 2, https://www.bdc.ca/globalassets/digizuite/58625-summary-bdc-corporate-plan-26-30.pdf).

Institutional history

BDC's 2025 Corporate Plan states it has been "supporting economic growth" for 80 years, consistent with founding roots in the 1940s (as the Industrial Development Bank), before reconstitution as the Business Development Bank of Canada under the Business Development Bank of Canada Act, S.C. 1995, c. 28 (BDC Corporate Plan Summary 2025-26 to 2029-30, Executive Summary; https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/B-9.9/index.html). ⚠️ still being checked — exact founding year and interim renaming (e.g. to Federal Business Development Bank) not independently source-checked this review.

Greater London International Airport Authority - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-Q10-WAVE1-d1 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: fed-greater-london-international-airport-authority (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The Greater London International Airport Authority (GLIAA) "was continued under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act as a corporation without share capital" (GLIAA Annual Report 2025, Note 1 - Description of Business, https://flyyxu.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/GLIAA-AR-2025.pdf, fetched and parsed this review). Its stated objectives are to manage, operate and develop the Airport - premises leased from Transport Canada - safely, securely, efficiently and financially viably with reasonable user charges and equitable carrier access; to develop airport lands for compatible uses; and to expand transportation facilities and generate economic activity compatible with air transportation (same source). GLIAA operates under the federal government's 1994 National Airports Policy, which enabled the federal government's withdrawal as direct operator of local airports (https://flyyxu.ca/about-us/, fetched this review).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

GLIAA holds full operational and financial control of London International Airport (YXU) (https://flyyxu.ca/about-us/, fetched this review), which its own site describes as Canada's 12th-busiest passenger airport, hosting more than 50 businesses and employing over 1,200 people (same source). 2025 passenger volume was 298,000, a 5% decrease from 2024 (Annual Report 2025, p.19, same source).

Governance & reporting line

Governed by a nine-member Board of Directors that meets seven times annually; directors are nominated by six constituencies - the federal government, the municipal government (City of London), the provincial government, the London & District Labour Council, the London Chamber of Commerce, and the GLIAA Board itself (Annual Report 2025, p.18, same source; https://flyyxu.ca/management-governance/, fetched this review - director names omitted per house style, roles only). Board committees cover Governance & Nominating, Finance & Audit, and Human Resources & Community Consultative functions (same source).

Budget scale

2025 gross revenue was $11.4 million ($11,392 thousand actual vs. $10,680 thousand planned), producing a 2025 operating surplus of $3.57 million (a net surplus of $2.92 million after amortization and other adjustments) (Annual Report 2025, pp.19-20, same source). More than $85.79 million has been invested in capital improvements to the Airport since GLIAA assumed operations in 1998, including $1.65 million reinvested in 2025 (same source). GLIAA paid $131 thousand in ground-lease rent to the federal government in 2025, calculated on gross revenues of $11.4 million (same source).

Institutional history

GLIAA was established in 1995 (https://flyyxu.ca/about-us/, fetched this review). On August 1, 1998, it signed a 60-year ground lease with Transport Canada and assumed responsibility for managing, operating and developing the Airport (Annual Report 2025, Note 1, same source), ending direct federal operation that had begun in 1945 (https://flyyxu.ca/about-us/, fetched this review). On March 5, 2015, Transport Canada approved a 20-year extension to the ground lease, moving its expiry date to July 31, 2078 (Annual Report 2025, Note 1, same source).

Halton Hills Public Library - backgrounder

Mandate & statutory basis

Halton Hills Public Library is a statutory public library board established under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (consolidated text verified at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 via text-rendering fallback). The Board itself is constituted by Town of Halton Hills By-law 2025-0071, "enacted pursuant to the Public Libraries Act," which "explains how the Library Board is set up, how members are chosen, and what the Library Board is responsible for" (https://www.hhpl.ca/library-board-policies).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Board "govern[s] the Library and support services that are inclusive and meet the needs of our community," operating two branches (Georgetown and Acton) and guiding the Library's mission "to empower our community through ideas, creativity, and connection" (https://www.hhpl.ca/library-board-policies; https://www.hhpl.ca/about). Current direction is set by a 2023-2027 Strategic Plan organized around three priorities - Welcoming and Inclusive, Community Connection, and Enriching Lives (https://www.hhpl.ca/about; registry doc hhil-halton-hills-public-library-sp-2023; ⚠️ VERIFY exact plan horizon).

Governance & reporting line

The Library Board is appointed by Halton Hills Town Council under By-law 2025-0071 pursuant to the Public Libraries Act's council-appointment framework (ss.9-10) (https://www.hhpl.ca/library-board-policies; https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44). ⚠️ VERIFY - current board member count/composition was not confirmed from a primary source this pass. The Town of Halton Hills (hhil-town-of-halton-hills) is the registry-listed parent municipality (registry row hhil-halton-hills-public-library).

Budget scale

Budget scale: ⚠️ VERIFY - not grounded this pass. The Library's FY2025 Annual Report discusses program activity and a Foundation donation of $4,400 but does not state a consolidated operating-budget or expenditure total (doc_id hhil-halton-hills-public-library-ar-2025, p.4-5,15); it does reference "most of the materials budget" being devoted to collections without a dollar figure (same source, p.3).

Institutional history

The current Board is the product of a 1974 consolidation of three predecessor boards - the Acton Library Board (established 1898), the Georgetown Library Board (established 1895), and the Esquesing Library Board (established 1973) - into the Halton Hills Public Library Board under Town of Halton Hills By-law 74-43 upon the Town's formation (https://www.hhpl.ca/library-board-policies).

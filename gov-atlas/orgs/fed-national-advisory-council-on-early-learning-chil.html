National Advisory Council on Early Learning and Child Care - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-08-04 / registry row: fed-national-advisory-council-on-early-learning-chil (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The Canada Early Learning and Child Care Act, S.C. 2024, c. 2 (Bill C-35, Royal Assent March 19, 2024) contains provisions (ss. 9-15) that would establish the Council in statute — s.9: "A Council is established, to be known as the National Advisory Council on Early Learning and Child Care, consisting of no fewer than 10 but no more than 18 members, including the Chairperson and the ex officio member" (https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-3.55/page-1.html, fetched). ⚠️ still being checked, but grounded in two independent search passes this review: ss.9-15 (the Council-establishing sections) are marked not in force on the Justice Laws site as of this review — meaning the currently-operating Council exists as an administrative body under Employment and Social Development Canada's (ESDC) own Terms of Reference, not (yet) as the statutory body the Act contemplates.

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Council's mandate is to "provide expert advice and to serve as a forum for consultation on issues and challenges facing the ELCC [early learning and child care] sector" — explicitly "not a decision-making body." It provides "non-binding third-party expert advice on ELCC to Employment and Social Development Canada in support of the Minister," facilitates information-sharing across the sector, develops reports/recommendations, and undertakes other ESDC-specified activities consistent with its advisory mandate (Terms of Reference, per WebSearch summary of https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/corporate/early-learning-child-care-advisory-council/terms-reference.html — the live page itself returned HTTP 403 to this review's direct fetch, so this is relayed from search-indexed content, not independently re-confirmed against the rendered page; ⚠️ still being checked). Once s.14 is in force, the Act would require the Council to provide guidance to the Minister and "consult broadly with individuals and organizations that have an interest in early learning and child care, including parents, the early childhood education workforce, child care providers, advocates and policy and research specialists" (https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-3.55/page-1.html, fetched).

Governance & reporting line

The Council reports to the Minister of Jobs and Families (the Honourable Patty Hajdu, in the role since May 13, 2025, per a consolidation of ESDC-family ministerial portfolios effective March 14, 2025 — WebSearch, canada.ca news releases). A senior ESDC official/the Minister's Deputy Minister sits as the Council's ex officio member; other members are Governor-in-Council appointees on terms not exceeding three years (Act s.10-11, https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-3.55/page-1.html, fetched — again subject to the ss.9-15 not-in-force caveat above). The first 16-member Council was announced November 24, 2022, chaired by Don Giesbrecht, former President of the Board of Directors and CEO of the Canadian Child Care Federation (https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/news/2022/11/government-of-canada-announces-members-of-new-national-advisory-council-on-early-learning-and-child-care.html, WebSearch).

Institutional history

The Council was first constituted administratively in November 2022, predating the Canada Early Learning and Child Care Act's March 2024 Royal Assent; the Act's own Council-establishing sections (9-15) remain not in force as of this review, so the Council continues to operate on its original administrative Terms of Reference rather than the statutory footing the Act would eventually provide.

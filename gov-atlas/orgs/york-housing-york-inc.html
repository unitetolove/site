Housing York Inc. - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: york-housing-york-inc (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Housing York Inc. (HYI) is incorporated under the Ontario Business Corporations Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. B.16 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90b16 — fetched and verified as the current consolidated text, last amendment 2024, c. 15, Sched. 1, s. 111), as a corporation wholly owned by York Region, governed alongside an Amended and Restated Shareholder Direction (2024 Housing York Inc. Annual Report, document id: york-housing-york-inc-ar-2024, fetched). HYI also files an Annual Information Return "mandated under the Housing Services Act, 2011" for its provincially-downloaded ("Provincial Reform") portfolio (Housing Services Act, 2011, S.O. 2011, c. 6, Sched. 1; title/citation confirmed via direct fetch of https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/11h06; 2024 Annual Report, document id: york-housing-york-inc-ar-2024).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

HYI owns and operates a community, emergency and transitional housing portfolio across York Region's nine local municipalities: at year-end 2024, 37 properties totalling 3,027 rental units (family townhomes, mixed-household buildings, seniors' residences) plus five condo units in Richmond Hill and seven Regionally-funded emergency/transitional housing sites, supporting over 4,500 residents (2024 Annual Report, document id: york-housing-york-inc-ar-2024). York Region funds HYI's emergency-housing operations on a cost-recovery basis and separately funds client services delivered by external agencies (same source). Registry notes describe it as a "publicly owned housing corp; no independent website" (registry row: york-housing-york-inc, notes field).

Governance & reporting line

HYI's Board of Directors approves the Corporation's Annual Report, audited financial statements, and Annual Information Return for submission to York Region, which acts as both sole shareholder (under the Business Corporations Act/Shareholder Direction) and Service Manager (under the Housing Services Act, 2011) (2024 Annual Report, document id: york-housing-york-inc-ar-2024). HYI's financial statements are consolidated with the Region's and audited concurrently by the same external auditor — KPMG LLP for 2024 — to ensure transparency in related-party transactions (same source). ⚠️ still being checked exact Board of Directors composition/appointment process — not detailed in the sources reviewed this review.

Budget scale

Total revenue $49.6 million and total expenses $48.7 million in fiscal 2024, producing an operating surplus of $0.9 million allocated to the Capital Reserve per HYI's Operating Surplus Policy (2024 Annual Report and Financial Statements, document id: york-housing-york-inc-ar-2024, fetched/converted via pdftotext).

Institutional history

Housing York Inc. was established in January 2003 as York Region's housing corporation (york.ca/support/housing/housing-york-inc, fetched), with part of its portfolio consisting of buildings "downloaded from the Province in the early 2000s" under Ontario's social-housing devolution (2024 Annual Report, document id: york-housing-york-inc-ar-2024); the 2021-2024 HYI Strategic Plan cycle concluded in 2024 (same source). ⚠️ still being checked the specific incorporating instrument/council resolution establishing HYI in 2003.

Council of the College of Denturists of Ontario - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-Q10-WAVE1-d1 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: on-council-college-denturists-ontario (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The College of Denturists of Ontario (CDO) is established and governed under the Denturism Act, 1991, the Health Professions Procedural Code (a schedule to the Regulated Health Professions Act, 1991), and the Regulated Health Professions Act, 1991 (RHPA) itself (https://denturists-cdo.com/About-Us/Mission,-Mandate-Objectives.aspx, fetched this review). The College's stated mission is "To regulate and govern the profession of Denturism in the public interest," and its objects are "to regulate the practice of the profession and to govern the members in accordance with the Denturism Act, 1991, the Health Professions Procedural Code and the Regulated Health Professions Act, 1991 and the regulations and by-laws" (same source). The College describes itself as "The Public Regulator for the Profession of Denturism" (https://denturists-cdo.com/, fetched this review).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The College's registrar functions administer qualifying examinations, process registrations and renewals, and maintain a public register of registered denturists (https://denturists-cdo.com/, fetched this review). Its public-protection mandate includes investigating complaints and conducting discipline hearings, quality assurance through continuing professional development requirements and peer assessment, and public-protection initiatives including sexual-abuse-prevention programming (same source). Its statutory objects, as set out on the College's Mission, Mandate & Objects page, also include establishing qualification standards for certificate holders, maintaining practice standards, developing continuing-competence and ethics standards, and promoting interprofessional collaboration (https://denturists-cdo.com/About-Us/Mission,-Mandate-Objectives.aspx, fetched this review).

Governance & reporting line

The College's governing Council/Board is composed of "Denturists elected by their peers (the registrants of the College)" and "public members appointed by the Lieutenant Governor in Council," with the Board Chair "elected by the Board from among the public members" (https://denturists-cdo.com/About-Us/Council-Committees/Council.aspx, fetched this review; exact numeric split between elected and appointed members and term lengths were not stated on this page — ⚠️ still being checked). The Board meets three to four times per year (same source). As an Ontario health regulatory college operating under the RHPA scheme, the College reports to the provincial government through the Minister of Health, and its public-member appointments are coordinated through Ontario's Public Appointments Secretariat (registry row notes; https://www.pas.gov.on.ca/Home/Agency/269, cited via search this review).

Budget scale

The College's registered annual reports (2007/08 through 2024/25, all linked from the same Annual Reports landing page at https://denturists-cdo.com/About-Us/Annual-Reports-(1).aspx per this library's government-document registry) list report titles but do not surface summary financial figures directly on that landing page, and a College Performance Measurement Framework report PDF fetched this review could not be text-extracted by this tool. As with other Ontario RHPA colleges, the College is presumed to be funded principally through registrant fees set under its by-laws and fee schedule (https://denturists-cdo.com/, fetched this review, references "By-laws & Fee Schedule" as a resource category), but no dollar figure is confirmed here.

Institutional history

The College operates under the Denturism Act, 1991, part of the same 1991 Ontario legislative package (alongside the Regulated Health Professions Act, 1991) that established Ontario's current health-profession regulatory college scheme (https://denturists-cdo.com/About-Us/Mission,-Mandate-Objectives.aspx, fetched this review, which references legislative amendments in "1991, 2007, 2009" without stating a specific College founding/proclamation date). ⚠️ still being checked — the College's precise founding/proclamation-in-force date was not confirmed this review; Ontario's RHPA-scheme colleges generally came into force in the early-to-mid 1990s following the Acts' 1991 passage, but this specific date for the CDO was not independently verified against a primary source this review.

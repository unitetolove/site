Georgina Public Library - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: geor-georgina-public-library (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Georgina Public Library is a statutory public library board under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (consolidated text verified at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 via text-rendering fallback), which under s.3 lets a municipal council establish a library board by by-law and under s.20 requires the board to operate the library "in accordance with this Act." The Library's own board page states the Act "defines the library's roles and responsibilities to the municipality and the province, and defines the services it is required to deliver" (https://www.georginalibrary.ca/about-your-library/about-us/library-board/).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The board is described on its own page as "the governing body, responsible for setting policies and directing the affairs of the Library," including hiring the CEO/Director who runs day-to-day operations (https://www.georginalibrary.ca/about-your-library/about-us/library-board/). Current direction is set by a 2023-2027 Strategic Plan (registry doc geor-georgina-public-library-sp-2023; ⚠️ still being checked exact plan horizon).

Governance & reporting line

The board comprises 9 members appointed by Town of Georgina Council to four-year terms coincident with the municipal term, including one Councillor and one Regional Councillor among nine community-and-council members, consistent with s.9-10 of the Public Libraries Act (https://www.georginalibrary.ca/about-your-library/about-us/library-board/; https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44; registry row geor-georgina-public-library, notes field). The Town of Georgina (geor-town-of-georgina) is the registry-listed parent municipality (registry row).

Budget scale

The Library's own 2025 funding chart reports the Town of Georgina supplies 95% of Library funding (with 2% from user fees/room rentals/lease and 3% from donations and grants), and a companion chart shows the Library accounting for approximately 3% of the Town of Georgina's total 2025 municipal budget (Georgina Public Library, "2025 Year in Review," document id geor-georgina-public-library-ar-2025, p.5-6). Against the Town's ~$167.3M 2026 total operating and capital budget (Town of Georgina 2026 Budget Book, document id geor-town-of-georgina-bud-2026), this implies a library budget on the order of several million dollars; ⚠️ still being checked - an exact library dollar total was not located this review.

Institutional history

Georgina Public Library operates as the statutory library board for the Town of Georgina under the Public Libraries Act (registry row geor-georgina-public-library); ⚠️ still being checked - the specific founding year/instrument (predecessor village/township libraries predating Georgina's 1971 municipal amalgamation) was not confirmed from a primary source this review.

Aurora Public Library - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: auro-aurora-public-library (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Governed under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44, consolidated text at ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 (link fetch-verified this review). The library's own board page states: "In accordance with the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, chapter P.44, Aurora Public Library is governed by its Public Library Board" (aurorapl.ca/library-board-and-administration/, fetched this review). Section 20 of the Act requires the board to operate one or more libraries and provide a comprehensive and efficient public library service (ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Delivers public library service to the Town of Aurora; the Board meets eight times yearly (third Wednesday of the month, in person and via Zoom) to conduct its regulatory and policy functions (aurorapl.ca/library-board-and-administration/, fetched this review).

Governance & reporting line

A statutory library board of seven members: five community members plus two sitting Town Councillors, with a Secretary-Treasurer/CEO supporting the Board (aurorapl.ca/library-board-and-administration/, fetched this review; registry row registry id: auro-aurora-public-library, parent_org: auro-town-of-aurora, notes: "statutory library board of 7 incl 2 councillors"). Council-member representation on the board is capped under Public Libraries Act s. 10 at "one less than a majority of the board" (ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44).

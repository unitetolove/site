CBC/Radio-Canada - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-Q10-WAVE1-d1 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: fed-cbc-radio-canada (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

CBC/Radio-Canada "was first established by the 1936 Broadcasting Act. The Corporation, a federal Crown Corporation domiciled in Canada, is an agent of His Majesty and all assets and liabilities are those of the Government" (CBC/Radio-Canada Annual Report 2023-2024, "General Information" note, https://site-cbc.radio-canada.ca/documents/impact-and-accountability/finances/2024/annual-report-2023-2024.pdf). Its current legislated mandate, drawn from the Broadcasting Act, is "to inform, enlighten and entertain all Canadians," with programming required to be predominantly and distinctively Canadian, available in English and French reflecting the needs of both linguistic communities, reflective of Canada's regions and multicultural character, and contributing to a shared national consciousness (same source, "About Us"). Under Broadcasting Act s.46(2) the Corporation must provide an international service (Radio Canada International); under s.46.1 it may borrow up to $220.0 million; and under s.47(1) it is designated an agent of the Crown (same source).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

CBC/Radio-Canada is Canada's national public broadcaster, delivering radio, television, and digital services in English and French, including programming in eight Indigenous languages via CBC North, and complies with licensing and regulatory obligations under the Broadcasting Act, the Telecommunications Act, the Radiocommunication Act, and CRTC requirements (Annual Report 2023-2024). The Corporation is governed by a Board of Directors, with the President and CEO reporting to the Board; the Corporation as a whole "is accountable to Parliament through the Minister of Canadian Heritage," and under Financial Administration Act s.85(1.1) is exempt from certain provisions of that Act (Annual Report 2023-2024, "General Information"). ⚠️ still being checked - the specific process by which Board members and the President/CEO are appointed (e.g., Governor in Council appointment on ministerial recommendation) was not confirmed by direct fetch of a primary CBC/Radio-Canada or Canadian Heritage page this review; the Corporation's own corporate-governance pages (cbc.radio-canada.ca/en/vision/*) returned blank or 404 responses to repeated fetch attempts this review.

Budget scale

For the year ended March 31, 2024, CBC/Radio-Canada reported total commercial revenue of $493.460 million (advertising $270.031 million, subscriber fees $120.868 million, other income $102.561 million) plus government funding of $1,436.652 million, comprising a Parliamentary appropriation for operating expenditures of $1,340.114 million, a working-capital appropriation of $4.000 million, and amortization of deferred capital funding of $92.538 million (Consolidated Statement of Income (Loss), Annual Report 2023-2024). The comparable prior year (2022-2023) figures were $515.584 million in commercial revenue and $1,271.846 million in government funding (same source), meaning government funding grew roughly 13% year over year.

Institutional history

CBC/Radio-Canada was established by the 1936 Broadcasting Act as a federal Crown corporation (Annual Report 2023-2024, "General Information").

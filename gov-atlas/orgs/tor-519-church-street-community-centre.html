519 Church Street Community Centre - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-Q10-WAVE1-d1 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: tor-519-church-street-community-centre (research/atlas/government_orgs.csv) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The 519 Church Street Community Centre ("The 519") is a City of Toronto agency and registered charity (registration #119310761-RR0001) governed by an independent Board of Management, whose external auditors are appointed by Toronto City Council (Board of Management for The 519 Church Street Community Centre, Financial Statements for the year ended December 31, 2024, Independent Auditor's Report, p.2, https://www.the519.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/519-Church-Street-Community-Centre-FS-2024-FINAL-1.pdf; https://www.the519.org/, fetched this pass). ⚠️ VERIFY - the specific municipal by-law or Municipal Code chapter establishing the Board of Management was not confirmed on the Centre's site or in registered documents this pass.

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The 519 operates a community centre at 519 Church Street serving Toronto's 2SLGBTQ+ communities, with programming spanning arts and culture, community wellness, youth programming, family and children's services, housing and legal support, health services, and trans-specific and older-adult programming (https://www.the519.org/, fetched this pass). Its 2024 Annual Report, titled "Build. Belong. Become.," marked the organization's 50th-anniversary year (The 519, 2024 Annual Report, https://www.the519.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/The-519-2024-AR_AODA_July-10.pdf; https://www.the519.org/, fetched this pass).

Per this project's identity/neutrality firewall, no individual client, program-participant, or board-member names are reproduced in this backgrounder.

Governance & reporting line

Governed by a Board of Management (whose Chairperson and Treasurer sign the annual financial statements) that reports to Toronto City Council as a City agency; its financial statements are audited by an external firm appointed by City Council under the City's Agencies and Corporations protocol (Board of Management for The 519 Church Street Community Centre, FY2024 Financial Statements, "Management's Responsibility for the Financial Statements" and "Independent Auditor's Report," pp.1-2, https://www.the519.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/519-Church-Street-Community-Centre-FS-2024-FINAL-1.pdf).

Budget scale

Total 2024 revenue (unrestricted fund) was $11,450,040, including $6,462,643 in government/institutional grants - City of Toronto $3,844,666, Government of Canada $990,867, United Way $532,472, Province of Ontario $479,088, and other grants $615,550 - plus $2,469,814 in fundraising revenue and $1,920,808 in donations. Total 2024 expenses were $11,423,863 (salaries and wages $5,238,738; employee benefits $1,385,546; purchase of services $3,253,315), leaving an excess of revenue over expenses of $26,177 (Board of Management for The 519 Church Street Community Centre, Statement of Operations (Unrestricted Fund), FY2024, p.7, https://www.the519.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/519-Church-Street-Community-Centre-FS-2024-FINAL-1.pdf). The auditor's opinion on the FY2024 statements was qualified because, as with most non-profits, the completeness of donation and fundraising revenue is not fully verifiable by audit (same source, "Basis for Qualified Opinion," p.2).

Institutional history

The 519 opened in 1976, making 2024 its 50th-anniversary year; it describes itself as having emerged during a period of activism driving social change for LGBTQ+ rights (https://www.the519.org/, fetched this pass; The 519, 2024 Annual Report "Build. Belong. Become.," https://www.the519.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/The-519-2024-AR_AODA_July-10.pdf).

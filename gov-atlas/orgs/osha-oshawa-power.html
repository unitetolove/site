Oshawa Power (OPUC Networks Inc.) - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: osha-oshawa-power (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Oshawa PUC Networks Inc. is a corporation incorporated under the Ontario Business Corporations Act, as authorized for municipal electricity businesses by s. 142 of the Electricity Act, 1998, S.O. 1998, c. 15, Sched. A ("Incorporation of municipal electricity businesses," Part XI - Transition: Municipal Electricity Utilities); consolidated text verified via ontario.ca/laws/statute/98e15 (fetched 2026-07-30, HTTP 200). Municipal councils could transfer former public-utility-commission assets/staff to such an OBCA corporation under s. 145(1) (same Act; confirmed via search of CanLII/vLex citation 2026-07-30). It is licensed and rate-regulated by the Ontario Energy Board under the Ontario Energy Board Act, 1998 (search-confirmed 2026-07-30; ⚠️ still being checked by direct OEB licence-lookup fetch).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Oshawa Power distributes electricity to over 63,000 residential and commercial customers across roughly 145 km² in Oshawa (search snippet, 2026-07-30 - ⚠️ still being checked against oshawapower.ca directly). Registry: municipal-level Tier 1 corporation, wholly owned by the City of Oshawa, standalone electric utility (osha-oshawa-power, parent_org: osha-city-of-oshawa).

Governance & reporting line

Oshawa Power is "wholly owned by the...City of Oshawa," its "sole shareholder," governed by a Board of Directors of 13 members drawn from finance, energy, law, technology and HR backgrounds (oshawapower.ca/corporate/, fetched 2026-07-30). As an OBCA corporation it reports to its municipal shareholder rather than to a provincial ministry, while its distribution rates and licence are set by the OEB.

Budget scale

Budget scale: ⚠️ not yet confirmed against an original source. (Registry doc-shelf lists a single 2025 annual report, osha-oshawa-power; its financial figures were not fetched this review.)

Institutional history

Traces to 19th-century municipal electric-utility origins in Oshawa (oshawapower.ca/corporate/ states "more than 130 years" of operation as of 2026, implying founding roughly in the 1890s; exact founding year and original utility name ⚠️ still being checked), later corporatized as Oshawa PUC Networks Inc. under Ontario Business Corporations Act authority pursuant to the Electricity Act, 1998, s. 142, as part of Ontario's province-wide 1998-2000 electricity-market restructuring.

Ontario/Toronto relevance

Direct — Oshawa Power is a municipal electric local distribution company (LDC) wholly owned by the City of Oshawa, part of the Greater Toronto Area, serving 64,000+ residential/commercial/industrial customers across a 145 km² territory within Oshawa itself (d5e76ab4f14c). Beyond the core Oshawa distribution business, the report documents an explicit GTA-wide expansion: EnerFORGE's 2025 minority-stake investment in Brass Inc. is described as extending the Oshawa Power Group's reach into utility-contracting services "across the Greater Toronto Area" (d5e76ab4f14c) — making this org one of the few in this lane with a documented, expanding footprint beyond its home municipality within the GTA.

Township of Uxbridge - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: uxbr-township-of-uxbridge (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The Township of Uxbridge is a lower-tier municipality under the Municipal Act, 2001, S.O. 2001, c. 25 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/01m25, verified — title/table of contents confirmed). Section 4 provides that "the inhabitants of every municipality are incorporated as a body corporate" (2017, c. 20, Sched. 8, s.99(1)), and s.1(1) defines a "lower-tier municipality" as one forming part of an upper-tier municipality — here, the Regional Municipality of Durham — for municipal purposes (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/01m25, verified).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Uxbridge delivers general local municipal services: property taxation, recreation and facilities, animal services, building permits and zoning, fire and emergency services, parks/trails, and community programming (registry id: uxbr-township-of-uxbridge, registry row; https://www.uxbridge.ca/, fetched). Its 2023–2026 Strategic Plan organizes activity under five priorities: Good Governance, A Vibrant and Growing Economy, Collaboration and Partnerships, Community Engagement, and Community Well-Being (Uxbridge Strategic Plan 2023-2026, document id: uxbr-township-of-uxbridge-sp-2023).

Governance & reporting line

Uxbridge is governed by an elected Mayor and Council; the Township's site references "Strong Mayor Powers" as a distinct governance topic, and Council appoints members to township boards/committees for four-year terms (https://www.uxbridge.ca/town-hall/boards-and-committees, fetched). Council size/ward structure is ⚠️ still being checked — not confirmed from the pages fetched this review. As a lower-tier municipality, Uxbridge sits within the upper-tier Regional Municipality of Durham for regional services (https://www.uxbridge.ca/, fetched; registry row notes).

Budget scale

Total revenues were $36,507,527 and total expenses were $27,794,572 for the 2025 fiscal year (actual, consolidated), against a 2025 council-approved budget of $28,934,485 in revenue (Consolidated Financial Statements of the Corporation of the Township of Uxbridge, December 31, 2025, document id: uxbr-township-of-uxbridge-fs-2025).

Institutional history

Uxbridge continues today as a body corporate under the Municipal Act, 2001 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/01m25, verified); the Township's specific founding date/instrument and any prior reorganizations (e.g., 19th-century township formation, subsequent Durham Region restructuring) are ⚠️ still being checked - not confirmed this review.

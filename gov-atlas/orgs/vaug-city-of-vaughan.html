City of Vaughan - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: vaug-city-of-vaughan (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The City of Vaughan is a lower-tier municipality under the Municipal Act, 2001, S.O. 2001, c. 25 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/01m25, verified — title/table of contents confirmed). Section 4 continues municipalities as bodies corporate, and s.1(1) defines a "lower-tier municipality" as one forming part of an upper-tier municipality — here, York Region — for municipal purposes (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/01m25, verified).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Vaughan delivers general municipal services spanning building and planning, by-law enforcement, parks/forestry, waste collection, water/wastewater/stormwater, property tax administration, and recreation (registry id: vaug-city-of-vaughan, registry row; https://www.vaughan.ca/, fetched). Its governing framework for the current term is the 2022–2026 Term of Council Service Excellence Strategic Plan (strategy_current_url in registry row).

Governance & reporting line

Vaughan is governed by an elected Mayor and Members of Council (https://www.vaughan.ca/council/mayor-and-members-council, fetched); exact council/ward composition is ⚠️ still being checked — not confirmed from the pages fetched this review. Less than 30% of a Vaughan property-tax bill funds City services, reflecting the split with York Region (upper-tier) and school boards (https://www.vaughan.ca/residential/property-tax-assessment/forms-applications-and-resources [tax-dollars page], fetched). As a lower-tier city, Vaughan sits within upper-tier York Region for regional services (registry row notes; https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/01m25, verified).

Budget scale

Consolidated total revenue was $1,112,261,776 (~$1.1B) and total expenses were $630,530,408 (~$630.5M) for the year ended December 31, 2024, producing an annual surplus of $481,731,368, which includes a $338.4M non-cash contributed-assets item and Vaughan's $36.0M share of Hydro Vaughan Corporations' net earnings (Consolidated Financial Statements of the City of Vaughan, Year Ended December 31, 2024, document id: vaug-city-of-vaughan-fs-2024).

Institutional history

Vaughan continues today as a body corporate under the Municipal Act, 2001 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/01m25, verified). Its transition from Town to City status and the specific year of that change are ⚠️ still being checked - not confirmed this review.

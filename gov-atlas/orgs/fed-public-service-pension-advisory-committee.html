Mandate & statutory basis

The Committee is established under the Public Service Superannuation Act (PSSA); its mandate is to "review matters respecting the administration, design and funding of the benefits provided under the Public Service Superannuation Act, make recommendations to the Minister about those matters, and review any other pension-related matters that the Minister may refer to it" (WebSearch summary of Treasury Board Secretariat "Roles and responsibilities" page, https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/pension-plan/plan-information/roles-responsibilities.html — the live TBS page was not independently re-fetched this pass; ⚠️ VERIFY). It additionally recommends candidates to serve on the Public Sector Pension Investment Board Nominating Committee (same source).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

A legislated 13-member committee: six members represent the employer (nominated by the President of the Treasury Board), six represent employees (nominated by the National Joint Council, Bargaining Agent Side), and one represents pensioners (nominated by a pensioner association) (WebSearch, TBS roles-and-responsibilities page and Canada Gazette/newswire appointment notices). The registry's own federal-organizations.canada.ca profile confirms a dedicated organization record (OrgID=PSPAC, https://federal-organizations.canada.ca/profil.php?OrgID=PSPAC&t=&lang=en — link located via WebSearch, not independently fetched this pass).

Governance & reporting line

The Committee advises the President of the Treasury Board on Public Service Pension Plan administration, benefit design, and funding matters (WebSearch, TBS pages). It is administratively supported by the Treasury Board Secretariat's pension-plan branch. ⚠️ VERIFY: current membership/chair not confirmed this pass — historical newswire appointment notices exist (e.g. 2010, 2006) but no current-as-of-2026 membership list was independently verified.

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: fed-immigration-refugees-citizenship-canada (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

IRCC is established under the Department of Citizenship and Immigration Act, S.C. 1994, c. 31 (chapter designation C-29.4), consolidated text verified at https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-29.4/. Section 2(1) provides "there is hereby established a department of the Government of Canada called the Department of Citizenship and Immigration over which the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration...shall preside," and s.4 extends the Minister's powers, duties and functions to "all matters over which Parliament has jurisdiction relating to citizenship and immigration...not by law assigned to any other department" (same source).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Per its 2026-27 Departmental Plan, IRCC organizes its work under three core responsibilities: Visitors, International Students and Temporary Workers; Immigrant and Refugee Selection and Integration; and Citizenship and Passports (document id fed-immigration-refugees-citizenship-canada-dp-2027, https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/departmental-plans/2026-27-departmental-plan/departmental-plan-2026-2027-full.html). This spans visa/permit processing, economic and family-class immigrant selection, refugee protection, and citizenship/passport issuance (same source; statute s.4).

Governance & reporting line

IRCC reports to the Minister of Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship under s.2(1)-(2) of the Act (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-29.4/; 2026-27 Departmental Plan, document id fed-immigration-refugees-citizenship-canada-dp-2027).

Budget scale

~$4.4B total planned spending for 2026-27 (11,476 planned FTEs), per the department's 2026-27 Departmental Plan (document id fed-immigration-refugees-citizenship-canada-dp-2027, https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/departmental-plans/2026-27-departmental-plan/departmental-plan-2026-2027-full.html), which also discloses planned reductions of ~$155M (2026-27) rising to ~$285M (2028-29) under a Comprehensive Expenditure Review (same source).

Institutional history

The department was established by statute in 1994 as the Department of Citizenship and Immigration under S.C. 1994, c. 31 (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-29.4/). ⚠️ still being checked - the department's operating name change to "Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada" (IRCC), commonly dated to 2015, was not confirmed against a primary source this review.

Competition Bureau Canada - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-Q10-WAVE1-d1 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: fed-competition-bureau-canada (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The Competition Bureau operates under the Competition Act, R.S.C. 1985, c. C-34, whose purpose clause (s.1.1) is "to maintain and encourage competition in Canada in order to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy," while expanding opportunities in world markets, ensuring small and medium enterprises an equitable opportunity to participate, and giving consumers competitive prices and product choices (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-34/page-1.html, fetched this review). Section 7(1) of the Act establishes the position of Commissioner of Competition, appointed by the Governor in Council, responsible for administering and enforcing the Act (same source).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Bureau describes itself as "an independent law enforcement agency that protects and promotes competition for the benefit of Canadian consumers and businesses," headquartered in the National Capital Region with regional offices in Montreal, Toronto and Vancouver (Annual Report of the Commissioner of Competition 2023-24, "About the Bureau," https://web.archive.org/web/20241210152116id_/https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2024/isde-ised/Iu50-2024-eng.pdf). Headed by the Commissioner, it administers and enforces the Competition Act, the Consumer Packaging and Labelling Act (except as it relates to food), the Textile Labelling Act, and the Precious Metals Marking Act (same source, "Responsibilities").

Governance & reporting line

The Bureau is headed by the Commissioner of Competition (Matthew Boswell as of the 2023-24 report); its annual report is issued "as represented by the Minister of Industry," reflecting a reporting relationship through the Minister of Industry / ISED portfolio (2023-24 AR, copyright line and title page).

Budget scale

For fiscal 2023-24 (April 1, 2023 - March 31, 2024), the Bureau reported an annual operating budget of $71,365,658 and a staff of 422 full-time employees; total expenditures across branches for the year were $75,974,625.39 (2023-24 AR, "Budget and people," p.4, and Annex A).

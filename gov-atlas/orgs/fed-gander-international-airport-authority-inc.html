Gander International Airport Authority Inc. - backgrounder

Mandate & statutory basis

The Gander International Airport Authority Inc. (GIAA) was incorporated as a not-for-profit corporation without share capital by Letters Patent under the Canada Corporations Act, and is exempt from income tax (GIAA 2025 Annual Report, Note 1 to Financial Statements, https://ganderairport.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/GIAA-2025-Annual-Report.pdf, fetched and text-extracted this review). It was later continued as a non-soliciting not-for-profit corporation under the federal Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act, with a continuance registered January 17, 2014 under corporation number 331199-6 (Corporations Canada federal corporate registry, https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cc/lgcy/fdrlCrpDtls.html?corpId=3311996, fetched this review). GIAA's stated objectives are "to manage, operate and develop the Gander International Airport in a safe, secure, efficient, cost effective and financially viable manner with reasonable airport user charges and equitable access to all carriers," and to undertake and promote development of the airport lands for which it is responsible (GIAA 2025 Annual Report, Note 1, https://ganderairport.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/GIAA-2025-Annual-Report.pdf).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

GIAA operates under the terms of a Ground Lease with Transport Canada covering the airport real estate, governed by the Ground Lease and the Canadian Airports Act; lease payments are calculated as 1% of defined revenue (GIAA 2025 Annual Report, financial statement notes, https://ganderairport.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/GIAA-2025-Annual-Report.pdf, fetched and text-extracted this review). Day-to-day operations include commercial aviation services (arrivals/departures), passenger ground services, retail and duty-free, dining, an international lounge, and a business-park component (https://ganderairport.com, fetched this review).

Governance & reporting line

GIAA is governed by a Board of Directors whose members are nominated by various regional stakeholders (GIAA 2025 Annual Report, Note 1, https://ganderairport.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/GIAA-2025-Annual-Report.pdf). The federal corporate registry lists the governing board size as ranging from 7 to 13 members (https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cc/lgcy/fdrlCrpDtls.html?corpId=3311996, fetched this review). GIAA's own site describes the Board's role as ensuring "sound oversight of the Authority's business" and setting standards to "shape the future of Gander International Airport and economic growth in Central Newfoundland" (https://ganderairport.com, fetched this review). Per the registry row, GIAA's parent relationship is recorded against Transport Canada (registry row registry id: fed-gander-international-airport-authority-inc, parent_org: fed-transport-canada), consistent with its Ground Lease arrangement for federally owned airport lands.

Budget scale

For the year ended December 31, 2025, GIAA reported revenue of $10,425,015 (2024: $8,642,679) against total expenses of $9,542,085 (2024: $9,370,291), producing an excess of revenue over expenses of $882,930 (2024: a deficit of $727,612) (GIAA 2025 Annual Report, Statement of Operations, https://ganderairport.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/GIAA-2025-Annual-Report.pdf, fetched and text-extracted this review; registry doc id fed-gander-international-airport-authority-inc-ar-2025). Wages and employee benefits were the largest expense line at $3,109,800 for 2025 (same source).

Institutional history

GIAA was incorporated as a not-for-profit airport authority in 1996 (⚠️ still being checked - sourced from a secondary federal-corporate-registry aggregator, not independently confirmed against a primary Corporations Canada historical filing this review) and subsequently assumed operational control of Gander International Airport from Transport Canada under the National Airports Policy framework for long-term-leased NAS airports (⚠️ still being checked exact transfer date - web search indicated March 1, 2001, but this was not independently confirmed against a primary source this review). It was continued under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act effective January 17, 2014, per the official federal corporate registry (https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cc/lgcy/fdrlCrpDtls.html?corpId=3311996, fetched this review).

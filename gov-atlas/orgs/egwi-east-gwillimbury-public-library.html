East Gwillimbury Public Library - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: egwi-east-gwillimbury-public-library (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

East Gwillimbury Public Library operates as a statutory public library board under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (consolidated text verified at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 via text-rendering fallback). Section 3 of the Act permits a municipal council to establish a public library by by-law, upon which the library operates through a board that is a corporation in its own right; s.20 requires the board to "seek to provide, in co-operation with other boards, a comprehensive and efficient public library service" and to operate the library "in accordance with this Act" (same source). The Library's own materials confirm it operates "under the legislative authority of the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990" (https://www.egpl.ca/about-your-library/library-board).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Library delivers public library service to the Town of East Gwillimbury through branches including Holland Landing, Mount Albert and (as of 2025) a re-opened Queensville branch located in the Health and Active Living Plaza (East Gwillimbury Public Library, Annual Report 2025, document id egwi-east-gwillimbury-public-library-ar-2025, p.5-6). Its current direction is set by a 2023-2026 Strategic Plan (registry doc egwi-east-gwillimbury-public-library-sp-2023; ⚠️ still being checked exact plan horizon).

Governance & reporting line

The Board is composed of 13 residents appointed by East Gwillimbury Town Council, consistent with s.9-10 of the Public Libraries Act which requires appointment by the municipal council and caps council-member representation at less than a majority of the board (https://www.egpl.ca/about-your-library/library-board; https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44; registry row egwi-east-gwillimbury-public-library, notes field). The Town of East Gwillimbury (egwi-town-of-east-gwillimbury) is the registry-listed parent municipality (registry row).

Budget scale

Budget scale: ⚠️ not yet confirmed against an original source. The Library's FY2025 Annual Report reports usage statistics (visits, program attendance, computer/wireless use) but no consolidated operating-budget or expenditure total (document id egwi-east-gwillimbury-public-library-ar-2025); the Library's funding flows through the Town of East Gwillimbury's tax-supported budget, which totalled $73.5M in 2025 town-wide, but no library-specific line item was located this review (Town of East Gwillimbury 2025 Budget release, https://www.eastgwillimbury.ca/en/news/east-gwillimbury-s-2025-budget-maintains-the-lowest-relative-taxes-in-york-region.aspx).

Institutional history

The Library was created through a Town by-law with its first Board members appointed effective January 1, 1975 (East Gwillimbury Public Library, Annual Report 2025, "Celebrating 50 Years of EGPL," document id egwi-east-gwillimbury-public-library-ar-2025, p.5), the branch network having since expanded and contracted (e.g., the original Queensville branch operated 1976-1992 before its 2025 re-opening; same source, p.6).

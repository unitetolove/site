Justice Canada (Department of) - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-STRAT-EVOL-d1 Wave 1 / 2026-07-30 / registry row: fed-justice-canada (research/atlas/government_orgs.csv) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The Department of Justice is governed by the Department of Justice Act (R.S.C., 1985, c. J-2), consolidated and current to 2026-06-14 at laws-lois.justice.gc.ca (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/J-2/, fetched and verified directly). Section 2 establishes the Department and the office of Attorney General; section 4 sets out the powers, duties and functions of the Minister; section 5 covers the Attorney General's duties (same source). The Department supports the dual roles of the Minister of Justice and Attorney General of Canada (https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/abt-apd/index.html, fetched directly).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Justice Canada functions as (1) a policy department overseeing federal-domain administration-of-justice matters, (2) a legal-services provider delivering advisory, litigation, and legislative/regulatory drafting services to other government departments and representing the Crown in civil litigation, and (3) a central legal adviser to Cabinet (https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/abt-apd/index.html). Its 2026-27 Departmental Plan splits work into Legal Services ($273.5M planned) and Justice System Support ($809.3M planned, covering criminal law reform, victims' rights, and access-to-justice initiatives) (https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/rp-pr/cp-pm/rpp/2026_2027/rep-rap/index.html, fetched directly). The Department administers 49 federal statutes spanning criminal justice, family law, and access to justice (https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/abt-apd/index.html).

Governance & reporting line

The Department operates under the Minister of Justice, who concurrently holds the office of Attorney General of Canada (Department of Justice Act ss. 2, 4-5, https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/J-2/; https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/abt-apd/index.html).

Budget scale

~$1.17 billion total planned spending for 2026-27 (including internal services): Legal Services $273.5M, Justice System Support $809.3M, Internal Services $90.9M; the Department also flags a planned $22.8M reduction in 2026-27 and roughly 234 FTE reduction by 2028-29 under an expenditure review (Justice Canada 2026-27 Departmental Plan, https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/rp-pr/cp-pm/rpp/2026_2027/rep-rap/index.html, url_status for this registry row's strategy_current_url is flagged ⚠️ VERIFY in the registry).

Institutional history

The Department was officially established in 1868 when Parliament passed the Department of Justice Act, which defined the roles of the Department and the Minister/Attorney General (https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/abt-apd/index.html); it continues to operate under the consolidated version of that Act (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/J-2/).

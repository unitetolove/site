Burlington Public Library - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: burl-burlington-public-library (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Governed under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44, consolidated text at ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 (link fetch-verified this review; bpl.on.ca/about/bpl/board confirms "the board's governance framework is outlined in Ontario's Public Libraries Act," fetched this review). Act s. 20 requires the board to provide "a comprehensive and efficient public library service" (ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44). Stated mission: "Inspire imagination, collaboration, and compassion to support an informed and literate society" (bpl.on.ca/about/bpl, fetched this review).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Delivers public library service to the City of Burlington across seven branches; Board responsibilities include setting policy, directing major initiatives, preparing annual budgets, monitoring finances, advocacy, and hiring/evaluating the Chief Executive Officer (bpl.on.ca/about/bpl/board, fetched this review).

Governance & reporting line

A statutory library board of seven members serving four-year terms - one City Councillor (Lisa Kearns at time of research) and six community volunteers appointed by Burlington City Council through a competitive selection process (bpl.on.ca/about/bpl/board, fetched this review; registry row registry id: burl-burlington-public-library, parent_org: burl-city-of-burlington). The Board presents its annual budget to City Council for approval (same source).

Budget scale

Budget scale: ⚠️ not yet confirmed against an original source. Board-approved budget figures were not located on the library's public web pages fetched this review.

Institutional history

The library dates its service "over 150 years," having evolved from an original school-hallway location to its current seven-branch system (bpl.on.ca/about/bpl, fetched this review); the precise founding year is ⚠️ still being checked - not confirmed in accessible page text this review.

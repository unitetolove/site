Vaughan Public Libraries - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-STRAT-EVOL-d1 Wave 1 / 2026-07-30 / registry row: vaug-vaughan-public-libraries (research/atlas/government_orgs.csv) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Vaughan Public Libraries (VPL) operates under the Ontario Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (verified at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44, HTTP 200, fetched 2026-07-30). VPL's own board page states its purpose is to "ensure the delivery of library services to people who live, work or study in Vaughan, as required by the Public Libraries Act" (https://www.vaughanpl.info/about/board, fetched 2026-07-30).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

VPL operates thirteen full-service branches plus one express self-serve storefront location, with a collection exceeding 550,000 items and roughly 2.5 million visitors annually (https://www.vaughanpl.info/about, fetched 2026-07-30). The Board "provides strategic guidance and oversight to VPL's Executive Management Team," which runs operations (https://www.vaughanpl.info/about/board, fetched 2026-07-30).

Governance & reporting line

Sixteen trustees serve the 2023-2026 term: the Mayor (Steven Del Duca), several ward and regional councillors, and appointed civilian trustees, chaired by Gary Sangha with Antonella Nicaso as Vice Chair (https://www.vaughanpl.info/about/board, fetched 2026-07-30). This mayor-plus-councillors-plus-citizens composition matches the registry's characterization of VPL as governed by a "statutory library board mayor plus councillors plus citizens" (registry row vaug-vaughan-public-libraries, notes field). Parent municipality is the City of Vaughan (registry row, parent_org: vaug-city-of-vaughan).

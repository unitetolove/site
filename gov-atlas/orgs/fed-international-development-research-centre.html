International Development Research Centre - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-STRAT-EVOL-d1 Wave 1 / 2026-07-30 / registry row: fed-international-development-research-centre (research/atlas/government_orgs.csv) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

IDRC is a federal Crown corporation continued under the International Development Research Centre Act, R.S.C., 1985, c. I-19, consolidated text verified at https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-19/index.html (fetched 2026-07-30; current to 2026-03-17). The Act's objects provision directs the Centre "to initiate, encourage, support and conduct research into the problems of the developing regions of the world and into the means for applying and adapting scientific, technical and other knowledge to the economic and social advancement of those regions" (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-19/fulltext.html, fetched 2026-07-30).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

IDRC funds and conducts locally-led research in low- and middle-income countries across economic development, health, education, food systems, science/technology, and human rights (https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/about-idrc, fetched 2026-07-30). It is headquartered in Ottawa with regional offices in Montevideo, Nairobi, Dakar, Amman, and New Delhi (same source). Registry notes: no current public strategic plan located, last one covered 2015-2020 (fed-international-development-research-centre, research/atlas/government_orgs.csv).

Governance & reporting line

The Centre is governed by a Board of Governors consisting of a Chairperson, the President, and up to 12 other governors appointed by the Governor in Council, with a majority required to be Canadian citizens (International Development Research Centre Act, ss. as fetched at laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-19/fulltext.html, 2026-07-30). The Chairperson reports to Parliament and presents an annual report to the Minister of Foreign Affairs through the Secretary of State (International Development) (https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/board-governors, fetched 2026-07-30). The Act requires the annual report, including audited financial statements and the Auditor General's report, be tabled in Parliament within four months of fiscal year-end (laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-19/fulltext.html).

Institutional history

Established in 1970 by Act of Parliament as, per IDRC's own account, "the world's first institution dedicated to supporting and funding high-quality research led by low- and middle-income countries" (https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/about-idrc, fetched 2026-07-30); no reorganization or rename is indicated by the current consolidated Act.

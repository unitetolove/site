Durham Region Local Housing Corporation (Housing Durham) - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-08-02 / registry row: durh-housing-durham (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The Durham Regional Local Housing Corporation (operating publicly as "Housing Durham") is a local housing corporation under the Housing Services Act, 2011 (HSA), S.O. 2011, c. 6, Sched. 1 (consolidated text at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/11h06). The HSA defines "local housing corporation" as "a corporation that was incorporated in accordance with section 23 of the former Act [the Social Housing Reform Act, 2000], including a corporation that results from an amalgamation with such a corporation, subject to section 30" (fetched 2026-08-02). Local housing corporations are share-capital corporations whose common shares are deemed issued to a "related service manager" per shares "deemed to have been issued under subsection 23(7) of the former Act" (fetched 2026-08-02) — the statutory mechanism making the Regional Municipality of Durham, as service manager, the Corporation's effective owner (registry row: durh-housing-durham, org_type: corporation, parent_org: durh-durham-region; notes: "incorporated local housing corp; council serves as board").

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Corporation owns and manages social/community housing properties across Durham Region. Regional budget documents reference "Durham Regional Local Housing Corporation properties, Durham Region Transit Westney" in the same capital-planning context as other Regional infrastructure (Proposed 2022 Property Tax Supported Business Plans and Budgets, Report #2022-F-4, https://www.durham.ca/media/3calh04a/proposed-2022-property-tax-supported-business-plans-and-budgets.pdf, fetched 2026-08-03), indicating its housing stock is treated as Regional capital infrastructure for planning purposes. ⚠️ still being checked — a detailed service/program breakdown (unit count, tenant demographics, program offerings analogous to HCHC's Community Development Strategy) was not located in the captured document set this review; the only captured document is a budget section within a larger Regional business-plan report.

Governance & reporting line

The registry records that "council serves as board" for this Corporation (registry row: durh-housing-durham, notes), consistent with the HSA's service-manager shareholding structure: Durham Regional Council holds the Corporation's shares and functions as its governing authority. The Corporation's budget is approved by Regional Council as a discrete line item within the Region's consolidated annual budget process, distinct from but adjacent to other Regional service budgets (Report #2022-F-4, Recommendation G, fetched 2026-08-03) — Regional Council "delegates authority to the Chief Administrative Officer and [named officers]" for certain in-year actions affecting Regional properties including the Local Housing Corporation, "and that such actions taken shall subsequently be reported upon to Regional Council" (Report #2022-F-4, fetched 2026-08-03), indicating routine administrative authority is delegated to staff with Council retaining oversight.

Budget scale

The 2022 Budget for the Durham Regional Local Housing Corporation was approved "at a total net property tax requirement of $14,019,000" (Proposed 2022 Property Tax Supported Business Plans and Budgets, Report #2022-F-4, Recommendation G, https://www.durham.ca/media/3calh04a/proposed-2022-property-tax-supported-business-plans-and-budgets.pdf, fetched 2026-08-03). ⚠️ still being checked — a current (2026) budget figure was not located in the captured document set this review; the 2022 figure is the most recent confirmed from primary sources on file.

Budget & mandate inflection points

  • 2022 net property tax requirement: $14,019,000, up $936,000 (7.2%) from the 2021 approved budget of $13,083,000, per the Council approval resolution ("The 2022 Budget for the Durham Regional Local Housing Corporation be approved at a total net property tax requirement of $14,019,000") and the Financial Details summary table (e011929b4e9e).
  • Total 2022 gross expenses of $42,498,000 (operating $16,827,000 + capital $25,671,000), against 2021 estimated actuals of $21,615,000 — driven almost entirely by a one-year capital spike (2022 capital of $25,671,000 vs. 2021's $6,000,000) (e011929b4e9e).
  • $22.24 million deep energy-efficiency retrofit project at four DRLHC seniors' housing properties, approved by Regional Council under resolution 2021-COW-35, with the Region having applied for up to $10 million in federal funding (a $5 million grant plus a $5 million low-interest loan) under the Federation of Canadian Municipalities' Green Municipal Fund Sustainable Affordable Housing program (e011929b4e9e) — this single project accounts for the large majority of the 2022 capital spending increase.
  • Three new positions added in 2022 (a Program Assistant, a Facilities Maintenance Mechanic, and a Senior Project Coordinator, all in Housing Services), explicitly to support introduction of the Maximo Enterprise Maintenance Management system and to advance Major Repairs and Renovations initiatives; their cost ($156,000 in 2022) is cross-charged from the Region's Social Services Department but recovered from the DRLHC budget (e011929b4e9e).
  • Key performance targets for 2022 disclosed against explicit prior-year comparators: property management operating cost per unit of $10,465 (a 13% increase over 2021 budgeted costs); monthly arrears at 15% of revenues ("consistent with 2021, reflecting challenges during the pandemic"); and 45 days average vacancy length on unit turnover (also "consistent with 2021, reflecting challenges during the pandemic") (e011929b4e9e) — these are the only year-over-year comparative figures the document itself discloses.
  • Income Tax Clinics delivered over $780,000 in tax returns to DRLHC tenants, cited in the "Looking Forward" section as a community-outreach program continuing post-pandemic (e011929b4e9e).

Ontario/Toronto relevance

Direct GTA regional-municipal relevance: DRLHC is the Regional Municipality of Durham's own local housing corporation, operating public and affordable housing exclusively within Durham Region (a GTA regional municipality east of Toronto) (e011929b4e9e).

Education Quality and Accountability Office - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: on-education-quality-accountability-office (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

EQAO is established by the Education Quality and Accountability Office Act, 1996, S.O. 1996, c. 11, confirmed via Ontario's consolidated e-Laws text (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/96e11). The Act sets out seven statutory objects: evaluating the quality of elementary and secondary education, developing and administering province-wide tests, creating evaluation systems, researching student-achievement assessment, evaluating school-board accountability, and reporting findings to the Minister and the public (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/96e11; registry id: on-education-quality-accountability-office).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

EQAO designs and administers Ontario's standardized elementary and secondary student assessments (e.g., primary/junior division literacy and numeracy tests, the OSSLT) and publishes school- and board-level results used for public accountability (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/96e11; https://www.eqao.com). It is described on its own site as "an evidence-based research-informed organization" (https://www.eqao.com/about-eqao/).

Governance & reporting line

EQAO is a board-governed Crown agency of the Government of Ontario, run by a board of a minimum of seven and a maximum of nine directors appointed by the Lieutenant Governor in Council; the board appoints a CEO after ministerial consultation (Education Quality and Accountability Office Act, 1996, S.O. 1996, c. 11, https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/96e11). It reports to the Minister of Education, submits annual budgets and operational plans for ministerial approval, and its annual report is tabled by the Minister in the Legislative Assembly (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/96e11; EQAO Business Plan 2025-2028, https://www.eqao.com/eqao-business-plan-2025-2028/).

Budget scale

~$32.69 million in projected total program operating expenses for 2025–26, with Ministry of Education allocation set at $31.39 million annually across 2025–28 (EQAO Business Plan 2025-2028, https://www.eqao.com/eqao-business-plan-2025-2028/).

Institutional history

Established in 1996 by the Education Quality and Accountability Office Act, 1996, S.O. 1996, c. 11, creating a dedicated provincial testing and accountability agency separate from the Ministry of Education (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/96e11).

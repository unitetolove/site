Canadian Space Agency - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-Q10-WAVE1-d1 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: fed-canadian-space-agency (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The Canadian Space Agency (CSA) is a federal government agency operating under the Canadian Space Agency Act, S.C. 1990, c. 13 (Canadian Space Agency, 2024-25 Departmental Results Report, "Statutory Basis," https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/publications/drr-2024-2025.asp, fetched this review). Its stated mission is: "We promote the peaceful use and development of space, advance the knowledge of space through science, and ensure that space science and technology provide social and economic benefits for Canadians" (https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/, fetched this review).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

CSA delivers Canada's federal space program, spanning Earth observation, space exploration and science, and satellite communications, and it maintains Canadian contributions to international programs such as the Canadarm (https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/, fetched this review). Departmental reporting is organized under two core responsibilities, "Canada in Space" and "Internal Services" (Canadian Space Agency, 2024-25 Departmental Results Report, "Financial Summary," same URL). Its most recent forward-looking plan is the 2026-27 Departmental Plan (Canadian Space Agency's 2026-27 Departmental Plan, https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/publications/dp-2026-2027.asp).

Governance & reporting line

CSA reports to Parliament through the Minister of Industry and is led by a President who serves as the Agency's chief executive; it is headquartered in Longueuil, Quebec (https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/, fetched this review; Canadian Space Agency, 2024-25 Departmental Results Report, https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/publications/drr-2024-2025.asp).

Budget scale

CSA reported total spending authorities available for use of $741,978,670 in fiscal 2024-25, against actual spending of $634,726,541 - comprising $549,195,233 for "Canada in Space" and $85,531,308 for "Internal Services" - supported by 986.7 full-time equivalents (Canadian Space Agency, 2024-25 Departmental Results Report, "Spending Authorities & Actual Expenditures," https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/publications/drr-2024-2025.asp, fetched this review). The Agency noted it "reprofiled $107.2M in 2024-25," with 94% of that amount related to capital-project funding carried forward to future years as "an inherent part of project management within the Canadian Space Program" (same source).

Institutional history

The Canadian Space Agency's own site dates its establishment to March 1989, operating under the Canadian Space Agency Act, S.C. 1990, c. 13 (Canadian Space Agency, 2024-25 Departmental Results Report, "Statutory Basis," same URL as above; https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/, fetched this review, which references "the history of Canada in space" including the Canadarm program). ⚠️ still being checked - the precise date the founding Act received royal assent, versus the Agency's administrative establishment in March 1989, was not fully reconciled this review (the Act's citation year is 1990 while operational establishment is dated to March 1989 on the Agency's own site).

Canadian Air Transport Security Authority - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: fed-canadian-air-transport-security-authority (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

CATSA is established by the Canadian Air Transport Security Authority Act, S.C. 2002, c. 9, s. 2, confirmed current to 2026-06-14 on Justice Laws and last amended 2018-05-23 (https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-11.2/). Section 5 provides "there is hereby established a body corporate to be called the Canadian Air Transport Security Authority," operating as an agent of the Crown for all legal purposes, with the Governor in Council designating its headquarters (CATSA Act, s. 5, Justice Laws).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

CATSA delivers aviation security screening functions, including passenger and non-passenger screening programs such as its Facilitator program; its own site notes it "achieved service improvements while maintaining budgetary restraint" through that program (catsa-acsta.gc.ca/en/about-us). Its current strategy document is the Summary of the 2025/26 to 2029/30 Corporate Plan (registry row: fed-canadian-air-transport-security-authority).

Governance & reporting line

CATSA is governed by an 11-member Board of Directors, including the Chairperson, appointed by the Governor in Council on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport, with two directors drawn from the airline industry and two from the airport industry; a permanent Audit Committee and a Governance, Human Resources and Pension Committee provide oversight (catsa-acsta.gc.ca/en/about-us/leadership-and-governance). It reports to the Minister of Transport (same source).

Budget scale

CATSA's Corporate Plan 2025-26 to 2029-30 Financial Overview shows total expenditures funded by parliamentary appropriations of $1,201,924 thousand (~$1.2 billion) for 2025-26, falling to a planned $556,727 thousand (~$557 million) for 2026-27; the plan flags a 2026/27 funding shortfall of roughly $737.0 million against mandated activities, stating CATSA "will require supplemental funding to continue to deliver its mandated activities" for 2026/27-2029/30 (CATSA Corporate Plan 2025-26 to 2029-30, Financial Overview, catsa-acsta.gc.ca/en/publications/corporate-plan-2025-26-2029-30/financial-overview).

Institutional history

CATSA was established in 2002 by the Canadian Air Transport Security Authority Act, S.C. 2002, c. 9, s. 2, as a Crown corporation and agent of the Crown responsible for aviation security screening (Justice Laws, https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-11.2/).

Mandate & statutory basis

Whitchurch-Stouffville Public Library operates under the Ontario Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (verified at https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44, HTTP 200, fetched 2026-07-30), which requires municipal library boards to "provide...a comprehensive and efficient public library service reflecting the community's unique needs" (s. 20).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Board sets policy and governs the library corporation; a CEO serves as Board Secretary and Treasurer and runs operations (currently Margaret Wallace) (https://wsplibrary.ca/library-board/, fetched 2026-07-30). The Board publishes meeting agendas, minutes, and formal governing policies (same source).

Governance & reporting line

Nine members plus two Town councillors (Ward 1 and Ward 2 designates) sit on the Board, chaired by Sandra Liaros with Nadine Williams as Vice-Chair (https://wsplibrary.ca/library-board/, fetched 2026-07-30), matching the registry's "9-member board plus 2 councillors" description (registry row wsto-whitchurch-stouffville-public-library, notes field). Parent municipality is the Town of Whitchurch-Stouffville (registry row, parent_org: wsto-town-of-whitchurch-stouffville).

Toronto Pan Am Sports Centre Inc. - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: tor-toronto-pan-am-sports-centre-inc (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

TPASC Inc. is a "Partnered Corporation," incorporated under the Business Corporations Act (Ontario), R.S.O. 1990, c. B.16 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90b16, verified — title/table of contents confirmed), under authority the City of Toronto Act, 2006, S.O. 2006, c. 11, Sched. A gives Council to incorporate corporations (https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/accountability-operations-customer-service/city-administration/city-managers-office/agencies-corporations/corporations/, fetched; https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/06c11, verified). The exact incorporating by-law/date is ⚠️ still being checked.

Roles, responsibilities & scope

TPASC "operates the Toronto Pan Am Sports Centre on behalf of the City and the Governing Council of the University of Toronto" (https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/accountability-operations-customer-service/city-administration/city-managers-office/agencies-corporations/corporations/, fetched). The 312,000-square-foot facility (registry id: tor-toronto-pan-am-sports-centre-inc, registry row) provides competitive aquatics, fitness, sport-league and instructional programming to City of Toronto residents, University of Toronto Scarborough (UTSC) students, and general members, and hosts national teams including Volleyball Canada's programs (https://www.tpasc.ca/, fetched; TPASC 2024 Annual Report, document id: tor-toronto-pan-am-sports-centre-inc-ar-2024).

Governance & reporting line

TPASC is governed by a Board of Directors with members appointed by both City Council and the University of Toronto (https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/accountability-operations-customer-service/city-administration/city-managers-office/agencies-corporations/corporations/, fetched); the 2024 Annual Report lists Howie Dayton (City of Toronto) as Chair of the Board of Directors and records "shareholder contributions" toward major facility capital, confirming a share-corporation structure with the City and University as shareholders (TPASC 2024 Annual Report, document id: tor-toronto-pan-am-sports-centre-inc-ar-2024).

Budget scale

Total operating revenues for 2024 were $20,653,313; total operating expenses for 2024 were $20,844,701 (TPASC 2024 Annual Report, document id: tor-toronto-pan-am-sports-centre-inc-ar-2024).

Institutional history

TPASC was managed as a corporation beginning in 2014, opening as a legacy venue of the 2015 Toronto Pan Am/Parapan Am Games, with the 2024 Annual Report crediting a Managing Director in place "2014–2024" (TPASC 2024 Annual Report, document id: tor-toronto-pan-am-sports-centre-inc-ar-2024). Exact incorporation date and any subsequent reorganizations are ⚠️ still being checked.

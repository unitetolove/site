Thunder Bay International Airports Authority Inc. - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-Q10-WAVE1-d1 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: fed-thunder-bay-international-airports-authority-inc (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Thunder Bay International Airports Authority Inc. (TBIAA) is a not-for-profit corporation responsible for the management, operation, and development of Thunder Bay International Airport (YQT) on behalf of the public, "in a way that is safe, efficient and financially viable" (cited via search this review; TBIAA's own site, tbairport.on.ca, could not be reached by this tool this review — its TLS certificate returned as expired on every fetch attempt, both with and without the www. prefix, over https, and the http variant returned HTTP 403). This is consistent with the registry's own note on this org ("tbairport.on.ca TLS cert invalid/expired on all attempts this review; could not verify live site," registry row fed-thunder-bay-international-airports-authority-inc, this library's government-document registry) from a prior research session, indicating the certificate problem is a standing site-side issue rather than a one-off fetch failure.

Roles, responsibilities & scope

TBIAA manages, operates, and develops Thunder Bay International Airport under a long-term ground lease from the federal government, following the airport's 1997 transfer from Transport Canada as part of the National Airports System devolution policy that moved a number of Canadian airports to local, not-for-profit airport authorities (cited via search this review). Search-indexed 2024 traffic figures for the airport show 728,077 total passengers (a 2% increase year-over-year) and 101,080 aircraft movements (cited via search this review; ⚠️ still being checked — these figures were not confirmed against TBIAA's own 2024 annual report, which is registered at fed-thunder-bay-international-airports-authority-inc-ar-2024 but could not be fetched this review due to the certificate issue above).

Governance & reporting line

The Authority is "headed by nine directors" representing the City of Thunder Bay, the Thunder Bay Chamber of Commerce, the federal government, the provincial government, and labour interests, with directors described as community leaders nominated by government and non-government organizations (cited via search this review, referencing TBIAA's board-of-directors page). Ontario's provincial-nominee director is put forward through the province's Public Appointments Secretariat process, serves a three-year term (reappointable), and the Board meets monthly, with additional sessions during airport-transfer negotiations (Ontario Public Appointments Secretariat agency profile for TBIAA, https://www.pas.gov.on.ca/Home/Agency/479, fetched this review — this page covers only the Ontario-nominated seat, not TBIAA's full nine-member board or its Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act status generally). The Board has "overall responsibility for the management of the affairs of the TBIAA," operating under the Authority's General Operating By-Law and Letters Patent (cited via search this review).

As external funding context (not TBIAA's own operating budget), the federal government announced $35 million in FedNor funding to Thunder Bay International Airports Authority in 2021 "to maintain regional connectivity and jobs" (Canada.ca news release, https://www.canada.ca/en/fednor/news/2021/08/thunder-bay-international-airports-authority-is-receiving-35m-from-federal-government-to-maintain-regional-connectivity-and-jobs.html, cited via search this review), and further federal infrastructure investment was announced in 2022 (Transport Canada news release, cited via search this review).

Institutional history

Management of Thunder Bay International Airport "was transferred from Transport Canada to the newly formed Thunder Bay International Airports Authority Inc.," a not-for-profit organization, in 1997, "under a 60-year ground lease," as part of Transport Canada's National Airports System policy of devolving airport management to local airport authorities across Canada (cited via search this review). ⚠️ still being checked — TBIAA's own registered 2010-2024 annual reports, which would be the primary source for a fuller institutional history and precise incorporation date, could not be accessed this review due to the certificate issue described above.

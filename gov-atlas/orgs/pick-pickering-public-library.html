Pickering Public Library - backgrounder

Backgrounder / 2026-07-30 / registry row: pick-pickering-public-library (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Pickering Public Library is a statutory public library board operating under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 — fetched via text-render fallback and verified 2026-07-30, title confirmed as "Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44"), supplemented locally by City By-law 2121/85 (pickering.ca board page, fetched). The Act requires council-established boards to have at least five council-appointed members (s. 9(1)) who cannot form a majority (s. 10(2)), and to provide "comprehensive and efficient public library service" (s. 20).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Library is an agency of the City of Pickering (registry row: pick-pickering-public-library, parent_org field), operating a multi-level system anchored by a Central Library resource collection (pickering.ca, board page, fetched). Its stated mission is to "provide equitable access to the resources and ideas that inspire lifelong learning and well-being," with a vision of Pickering as "a city where learning is promoted in all its forms" (pickering.ca board page, fetched). The Library's Strategic Plan 2024-2027 sets multi-year priorities (document id: pick-pickering-public-library-sp-2024).

Governance & reporting line

The Board has 11 members: three City Councillors (one per Ward), eight citizen appointees, and the Mayor ex-officio, all Council-selected, with terms running through November 14, 2026 (pickering.ca board page, fetched; registry row notes: "statutory library board appointed by City Council"). Jackie Flowers serves as Library CEO (pickering.ca board page, fetched).

Budget scale

~$7.1M annual operating budget: the Board's Deloitte-audited financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2024 report total revenue of $7,118,267 (including $6,775,245 in City of Pickering grants) against total expenses of $7,028,616, producing an annual surplus of $89,651 (document id: pick-pickering-public-library-fs-2024).

Institutional history

The Library operates today as a statutory board of the City of Pickering under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 and City By-law 2121/85 (registry row: pick-pickering-public-library); its founding year and any prior reorganizations are ⚠️ not yet confirmed against an original source.

Community Centre 55 - backgrounder

Mandate & statutory basis

Community Centre 55 ("Centre 55") operates as "an agency of the City of Toronto and a registered charity," governed by an independent Board of Management (https://www.centre55.com/, fetched this review; registry source: City of Toronto Agencies & Corporations Chart, March 2025, https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/96db-Agencies-Corps-Chart-March-2025.pdf, which describes it as a "micro body - community recreation facility managed through Parks, Forestry and Recreation"). ⚠️ still being checked - the specific municipal by-law or Municipal Code chapter establishing the Board was not confirmed this review.

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Centre 55 is a multi-service community agency serving the Beaches-East York neighbourhood, operating Ministry-licensed childcare for ages 18 months to 13 years, recreational and educational programming for adults and seniors, a youth summer camp (ages 5-12), and community events including the Beaches Santa Claus Parade and the "Share A Christmas" gift-card and toy program for families in need (https://www.centre55.com/, fetched this review). It offers over 30 adult fitness and creative-arts classes in addition to its childcare and youth programming (same source).

Governance & reporting line

Governed by a Board of Management and led by a Leadership Team with an Executive Director overseeing day-to-day operations; as a City of Toronto agency, its financial statements and management letters are submitted annually to Toronto's Audit Committee under the City's Agencies and Corporations protocol (https://www.centre55.com/, fetched this review; City of Toronto Auditor General, "Community Centres - 2022 and 2021 Audited Financial Statements and Status of Outstanding 2022 Audited Financial Statements," p.2, https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2023/au/bgrd/backgroundfile-237729.pdf).

Budget & mandate inflection points

  • FY2026 budget: $21.102M gross / $1.046M revenue / $20.057M net, part of a $77.312M gross FOC total — the largest net cost of the FOC book's three divisions (05ad8c968889).
  • SAP-ECC end-of-life risk and Payroll Modernization project launch: "The SAP Enterprise Central Component (SAP-ECC) is nearing its end of life. The Payroll Modernization project design and implementation commenced in the second quarter of 2025, requiring significant support from PPEB" — named as a Key Challenge in the same document that reports the modernization project as newly underway (05ad8c968889).
  • Growth pressure disclosed with stable headcount: "The number of employees and retirees receiving benefits by the end of 2025 is projected to be 3.5% higher than in 2024," with 2026-2027 targets assuming "an annual growth rate of about 1.5%, driven by an increasing number of retirements with continued eligibility for retiree benefits" — the document reports this growth absorbed "through ongoing process improvements and workflow efficiencies" rather than headcount increases (05ad8c968889).
  • Reported FY2025 accomplishments: two new DEI-aligned benefit offerings funded through "savings from reduced drug and paramedical fees"; a TEAM Central operations revamp (productivity targets, staff-schedule adjustments, a Quality Assurance Program, streamlined retirement planning, improved escalation tracking); a modernized WSIB (non-Toronto Fire) process; a T2200 system upgrade enabling "24/7 on-demand employee access" for 13,000 forms in the 2025 tax season; and a completed PPEB Training Curriculum (05ad8c968889).

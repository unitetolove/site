Finance Shared Services has no own enabling statute; it is an administrative unit of the City of Toronto operating under Council-delegated authority. Roles, responsibilities & scope

Per the division's own staff-directory PDF (fetched live this review via direct download + pdftotext, since this document was never registered/archived — see Residuals note below): "Finance Shared Services supports the transformation and modernization of transactional finance activities while ensuring consistent processes and strong financial controls, with a focus on improving efficiency, standardization, and service delivery across finance operations." Its Workforce Planning and Development team "resides within Finance Shared Services and provides workforce planning, onboarding and training, and employee development initiatives to Shared Services divisions, including PPEB [Pension, Payroll and Employee Benefits] and RSD [Revenue Services Division]" (https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/99d9-officeoffinancesharedservices.pdf, fetched live 2026-08-04). The division sits within the "Finance and Treasury Services" administrative grouping headed by the Office of the CFO and Treasurer, distinct from (though adjacent to) the "Finance Shared Services" name's near-match to tor-finance-treasury-services, a separate registry registry id.

Governance & reporting line

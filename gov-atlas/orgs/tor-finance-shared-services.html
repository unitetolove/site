Finance Shared Services (City of Toronto) - backgrounder

Mandate & statutory basis

Finance Shared Services has no own enabling statute; it is an administrative unit of the City of Toronto operating under Council-delegated authority. The City's general corporate powers derive from the City of Toronto Act, 2006, S.O. 2006, c. 11, Sched. A, ss.6(1) and 7 — the citation already established for other Toronto divisions in this corpus (_backgrounders/tor-corporate-finance-revenue-function.md, _backgrounders/tor-corporate-real-estate-management.md); not independently re-verified against a rendered e-Laws page this pass (⚠️ VERIFY, relayed from corpus precedent).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Per the division's own staff-directory PDF (fetched live this pass via direct download + pdftotext, since this document was never registered/archived — see Residuals note below): "Finance Shared Services supports the transformation and modernization of transactional finance activities while ensuring consistent processes and strong financial controls, with a focus on improving efficiency, standardization, and service delivery across finance operations." Its Workforce Planning and Development team "resides within Finance Shared Services and provides workforce planning, onboarding and training, and employee development initiatives to Shared Services divisions, including PPEB [Pension, Payroll and Employee Benefits] and RSD [Revenue Services Division]" (https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/99d9-officeoffinancesharedservices.pdf, fetched live 2026-08-04). The division sits within the "Finance and Treasury Services" administrative grouping headed by the Office of the CFO and Treasurer, distinct from (though adjacent to) the "Finance Shared Services" name's near-match to tor-finance-treasury-services, a separate registry org_id.

Governance & reporting line

The directory PDF names an Executive Director (David McIsaac, cited institutionally as titled officeholder, per the private-individual/donor-name house rule allowance for titled officeholders) leading two sub-teams: "Shared Services Transformation & Modernization" and "Workforce Planning & Development," based at Metro Hall, 13th floor, 55 John St., Toronto (https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/99d9-officeoffinancesharedservices.pdf, fetched live 2026-08-04). The registry's parent_org field lists tor-deputy-city-manager-corporate-services (registry row tor-finance-shared-services). ⚠️ VERIFY: this session did not independently confirm whether Corporate Services or the CFO's own office is the more precise day-to-day reporting line (the same ambiguity flagged for the adjacent CFO/FOC division cluster in _backgrounders/tor-corporate-finance-revenue-function.md).

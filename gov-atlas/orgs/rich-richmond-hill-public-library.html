Mandate & statutory basis

Richmond Hill Public Library (RHPL) is a statutory public library board operating under the Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44 (https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90p44 — fetched via text-render fallback and verified 2026-07-30, title confirmed as "Public Libraries Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.44"). The Act requires council-established boards to have at least five council-appointed members (s. 9(1)) who cannot form a majority (s. 10(2)), and to provide "comprehensive and efficient public library service" (s. 20).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

RHPL is an agency of the City of Richmond Hill (registry row: rich-richmond-hill-public-library, parent_org field). The Board's role is to "oversee the mission, plans, and policies of the library" and ensure "the delivery of library services and programs represent the changing needs of the community" (rhpl.ca/board, fetched). RHPL publishes a Strategic Plan (registry row: rich-richmond-hill-public-library, strategy_current_url field: https://www.rhpl.ca/about-the-library/strategic-plan-reports) and an annual report (doc_id: rich-richmond-hill-public-library-ar-2024, flagged ⚠️ VERIFY:fetch-failed-2026-07-28 in the registry; the direct report URL also returned HTTP 404 when re-checked this pass).

Governance & reporting line

Board leadership includes Chair Mona Shahnazari, Vice-Chair Jennifer Paige, and Library CEO Bruce Gorman, alongside municipal councillors and community representatives such as Stephen Chait, Shelly Wu, Sadra Nasseri and Jason Cherniak (rhpl.ca/board, fetched; registry row notes: "statutory library board with councillors and citizen appointees"). The Board meets monthly, on the third Thursday, at the Central Branch, with public delegation rights (rhpl.ca/board, fetched).

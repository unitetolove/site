Centennial Centre of Science and Technology - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-Q10-WAVE1-d1 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: on-centennial-centre-science-technology (this library's government-document registry) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

The organization publicly branded as the Ontario Science Centre is legally the Centennial Centre of Science and Technology, incorporated without share capital under the Centennial Centre of Science and Technology Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. C.5 (Public Appointments Secretariat agency profile, https://www.pas.gov.on.ca/Home/Agency/383, fetched this review; confirmed in the organization's own 2023-24 financial statements, which describe the entity as incorporated "pursuant to the Centennial [Centre of Science and Technology Act]," https://www.ontariosciencecentre.ca/media/bbmdx5ma/2023-2024-financial-statements-english_final-s.pdf). Section 2(1) of the Act sets its statutory purposes as depicting to the public, and conducting education programs on, "the origins, development and progress of science and technology and their relationship to society," showing Ontario's role in furthering science and technology, and stimulating public interest in the Centre's work (https://www.pas.gov.on.ca/Home/Agency/383).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

The Centre collects and exhibits objects and displays, and maintains and operates its facilities under the branding "Ontario Science Centre" (https://www.pas.gov.on.ca/Home/Agency/383). Its own site describes its purpose as bringing "science to life through one-of-a-kind experiences, sparking curiosity, creativity and wonder" (https://www.ontariosciencecentre.ca/about-us/, fetched this review), and identifies itself as "an agency of the Government of Ontario" (same source).

Governance & reporting line

The Centre's affairs are under the control of a Board of Trustees; the Lieutenant Governor in Council appoints not fewer than sixteen and not more than twenty-six trustees, designating one as chair and one as vice-chair, for terms not exceeding three years with the possibility of reappointment (https://www.pas.gov.on.ca/Home/Agency/383). The Board meets four times annually (same source). The Centre reports to the Ontario Minister of Tourism, Culture and Gaming and, through that Minister, to the Legislative Assembly of Ontario (https://www.pas.gov.on.ca/Home/Agency/383; corroborated by The Canadian Encyclopedia, https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/ontario-science-centre).

Budget scale

For the fiscal year ended March 31, 2024, the Centre reported total revenue of $34,328,000 - including a $19,272,000 operating grant from the Province of Ontario (Ministry of Tourism, Culture and Sport), $297,000 in other provincial funding, $248,000 in grants for ancillary operations, $4,391,000 in general admission revenue, $1,198,000 in parking fees, and $8,922,000 in ancillary-operations revenue - against total expenses of $40,922,000, producing a net loss of $7,743,000 for the year (Ontario Science Centre 2023-24 Financial Statements, p.4-5, https://www.ontariosciencecentre.ca/media/bbmdx5ma/2023-2024-financial-statements-english_final-s.pdf, fetched this review; registry doc shelf for registry id on-centennial-centre-science-technology). The prior year, FY2022-23, is reported in the same statements at $38,532,000 total revenue and $38,104,000 total expenses.

Institutional history

Plans for the Centre were announced in 1964 as an Ontario project for the Canadian Centennial; construction began in 1966 with an original target of a 1967 opening, but the Centre did not open to the public until September 26, 1969, at its Don Mills Road site in Toronto (The Canadian Encyclopedia, https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/ontario-science-centre, fetched this review). It has continued to operate as the Centennial Centre of Science and Technology, a Government of Ontario agency, since that opening (https://www.pas.gov.on.ca/Home/Agency/383; registry row registry id: on-centennial-centre-science-technology). ⚠️ still being checked - the organization's relocation/closure disruption referenced in the registry notes for this registry id (no newer business plan published since) is noted in the registry but not independently re-confirmed against the org's own site this review.

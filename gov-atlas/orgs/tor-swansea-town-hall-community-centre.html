Swansea Town Hall Community Centre - backgrounder

Backgrounder / GOV-Q10-WAVE1-d1 / 2026-08-04 / registry row: tor-swansea-town-hall-community-centre (research/atlas/government_orgs.csv) / lens file for this org's series briefs

Mandate & statutory basis

Swansea Town Hall describes itself as "an agency of the City of Toronto" and "one of ten AOCC's [Association of Community Centres] in the city" (https://www.swanseatownhall.ca/, fetched this pass) - City-owned/City-affiliated facilities governed by independent volunteer Boards of Management under the City of Toronto Act, 2006, the former City of Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 25, and a Council-approved Relationship Framework (https://www.toronto.ca/community-people/get-involved/get-involved-my-community/association-of-community-centres/, fetched this pass).

Roles, responsibilities & scope

Located west of High Park near Lake Ontario, the Centre promotes community building by "hosting public services and providing community programming" through children's, adult, and seniors' (S.A.S.A.) programs, eight rentable venue spaces for events and meetings, and by housing a Toronto Public Health Dental Clinic and the Swansea Memorial Library (https://www.swanseatownhall.ca/, fetched this pass).

Governance & reporting line

Governed by a 16-member Board of Management: six members appointed by founding community organizations (Swansea Horticultural Society, Swansea Historical Society, Swansea Area Seniors Association, Swansea Area Ratepayers Association, James T. Bonham Residence, and Swansea Community Recreation Centre Advisory Council), nine community-elected members recruited annually, and one City representative (the Ward's City Councillor) (https://www.swanseatownhall.ca/our-governance/, fetched this pass; officeholder names omitted per house style - roles only). Day-to-day operations are led by an Executive Director and Assistant Executive Director reporting to the Board (same source).

Budget scale

The Centre publishes its audited financial statements from 2018 through 2025 via a dedicated "Our Financial Structure" page, but that page itself does not disclose summary revenue, expense, or City-funding-share figures (https://www.swanseatownhall.ca/our-financial-structure/, fetched this pass). The most recently registered filing - the 2024 Audited Financial Statements report (https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2025/au/bgrd/backgroundfile-256790.pdf, fetched and parsed this pass) - is likewise the Auditor General's four-page cover report only; the per-centre financial-statement attachment (Attachment 8, "Swansea Town Hall Community Centre") that would carry specific figures is not separately captured as its own URL in the registry. ⚠️ VERIFY specific dollar figures for this centre - not confirmed this pass.

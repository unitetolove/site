Three additional Toronto service providers: corrections, HIV/AIDS housing, and a neighbourhood shelter operator

Three real, confirmed Toronto organizations extend this library's operator coverage into corrections- adjacent services and HIV/AIDS-specific supportive housing — with a genuine financial-transparency gap for two of the three, disclosed rather than assumed away.

The finding

The John Howard Society of Toronto is a confirmed real registered charity (CRA Business Number 133412114RR0001). Its Toronto branch files separate financials from the John Howard Society of Ontario parent organization, per the parent's own financial statement disclosure, but branch-specific figures are not surfaced through Charity Intelligence, which has no dedicated Toronto profile CSRL2-678. The Society's own historical research is directly relevant to this library's corrections-homelessness coverage: an August 2010 study, "Homeless and Jailed: Jailed and Homeless," found 49.2% of sentenced prisoners surveyed were already Ontario Works or ODSP recipients before incarceration, rising to 63.9% among the homeless subgroup specifically. The study is real but has real, stated limits — it covers sentenced adult men only, in the Toronto area only, and the data is now over 15 years old. An illustrative annual cost range of $3.5 million to $10.5 million was computed from this data but is deliberately not presented as a single policy-grade figure, consistent with this library's own established discipline of not forcing precision a dataset doesn't support CSRL2-735.

Fife House is confirmed as the largest Canadian HIV/AIDS supportive housing provider (CRA Business Number 130401573RR0001). It already appeared, previously unconnected, in this library's own historical per diem data at $20.25 per day — a real cross-reference identified only now. Fife House's current Executive Director could not be resolved with confidence given conflicting leadership-transition signals across sources checked, and this is explicitly flagged as needing direct confirmation rather than guessed at CSRL2-679.

Christie Ossington Neighbourhood Centre (CONC) is confirmed as a direct shelter operator running two shelters plus 198 hotel rooms — the clearest direct shelter-operator fit among the organizations checked in this research pass. No CRA business number, Charity Intelligence profile, or financial figure was located for CONC despite the attempt — a genuine negative result on the financial-transparency side specifically, not a gap this page glosses over CSRL2-681.

Why it matters

The John Howard Society's own 2010 study is a real, if dated, quantification of how much sentenced incarceration and pre-existing social-assistance receipt overlap — directly relevant context for this library's broader corrections-homelessness pipeline coverage, and worth citing with its real limits stated rather than treated as current. The Fife House cross-reference is a small but genuine example of this library's own research connecting to data already on file under a different heading. The CONC finding matters for a structural reason: a confirmed, direct shelter operator with no locatable financial transparency profile at all is a different, more basic gap than the "profile exists but shows an unusual funding mix" pattern seen elsewhere in this library's operator coverage.

What this page does not cover

This page does not update the illustrative $3.5-10.5 million cost range into a current or precise policy figure, since the underlying 2010 data does not support that precision. It does not resolve Fife House's current Executive Director. It does not locate a financial profile for Christie Ossington Neighbourhood Centre — that remains a genuine, disclosed gap. It does not carry a "case against" any of these three organizations, since the sources reviewed are each organization's own confirmed registration/site data and a dated internal study, not a documented opposing position.

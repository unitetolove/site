Press kit

This page is for journalists, fact-checkers, and anyone else who needs to describe this project accurately on a deadline. Everything below is drawn from what’s already published on this site — nothing here is a new claim.

What this project is

uniteTOlove.ca is one Toronto neighbour’s invitation to a conversation, not a campaign. It asks the city to unite around four things: a floor under everyone, so no one falls further than a life can recover from; a commons around everything we share, owned by the public and walled against capture; a shared civic practice — things done together, weekly, that make a city a city; and receipts on all of it, so every claim is checkable and every promise carries its own test, in public. There is nothing to join and nothing for sale. Nothing on the site asks anyone to vote for anyone. In practice, that takes the shape of a public research library on Toronto civic issues, published in the open with its sources attached.

Who writes it — and why anonymously

Every page is signed The Unknown Soldier. That’s deliberate, and the reasoning is public, not a dodge: the moment a name attaches to an argument, readers start judging the name instead of the work — where they went to school, what box they fit, what to dismiss before it’s even read. Removing the name removes that shortcut and leaves only the work to judge. There’s a second reason, and it’s the real one: an unnamed author can be anyone, including the reader. Checking a claim, joining a project, correcting a mistake — that’s what “being” the Unknown Soldier means, and it was never meant to be one person’s byline. The author has said the name will come off eventually, and that nothing about the work will need to change when it does, because it was written to be judged without it. The full founding letter, in the author’s own words: Who is the Unknown Soldier.

How to verify us

Don’t take our word for it — that’s the point. Every load-bearing claim on this site is meant to trace to a real, checkable source, and pages carry one of a small set of honest status words: sealed (independently checked against original sources, zero unresolved disagreements), in verification (drafted against sources, being checked now, with any specific open item disclosed rather than smoothed over), draft (written, not yet checked), or proposed / coming (scoped, not yet built). The method in full: How we verify. If you find something wrong, the same open channel below fixes it in public — see Corrections & improvements for exactly what happens to a report once it lands.

How to cite or quote us

  • Attribute the site as uniteTOlove.ca (author: The Unknown Soldier).
  • Link the specific page you’re citing, not a screenshot of it — pages get corrected in place, with the old version kept visible and dated, so a live link stays accurate in a way a screenshot can’t.
  • Figures on this site carry an as-of or last-checked date where they appear. Quote the figure together with that date, not on its own — e.g. “as of [date], uniteTOlove.ca reported …, sourced to …”
  • If a figure or claim you want to quote doesn’t show a date on its face, ask us before publishing — use the contact channel below.

The wordmark & assets

The mark is the wordmark itself — “uniteTOlove”, pronounced “unite T–O love” — available as an SVG for light and dark backgrounds:

  • master, light background
  • master, dark background
  • poster cut, light background
  • poster cut, dark background

The O of TO is deliberately swappable — colour it, stamp it, put your own drawing in it. Earlier versions of the mark remain valid wherever they are already printed.

There is no other logo, photo, or headshot to request — there is no face attached to this project by design (see “Who writes it,” above).

Contact

One channel, monitored, read by a person every time: unknownsoldier@unitetolove.ca. It handles press and interview requests the same way it handles corrections and improvements — there is no separate press office. Say it’s a media inquiry in your subject line and you’ll get a faster read.

What we will not do

We don’t endorse or favour any candidate, party, or side. Every resource on this site — backgrounders, the citizen question bank, the candidate guide — is offered to every candidate and every voter on identical terms, regardless of who wins. No donations are asked for anywhere on this site, and none are accepted. Nothing here asks you to vote for anyone.

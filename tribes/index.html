Tribe Map
This is an organization-level directory only — organizations and public communities, never people. Individual member/moderator/participant data is never published here. Sizes under 1,000 are not shown, so a count can never be small enough to identify who's inside a community. Roughly two-thirds of the rows below are listing-only: a community was found to exist, but its current size/activity was not independently confirmed — those carry a ⚠️ unconfirmed mark rather than being silently presented as checked. Free-text notes, moderation/activity signals, and anything else that could read as a “how to work this community” guide were deliberately excluded before this page was built — only name, public URL, self-declared scope/topic, and confirmation status are shown.

The Tribe Map — organizations & public communities

A directory of Toronto/GTA/Ontario public communities on Reddit, Facebook, Instagram, Discord, and YouTube — neighbourhood groups, mutual aid, diaspora communities, hobby/sport clubs, and city/regional hubs. Organizations only: name, public URL, self-declared scope, and whether we could confirm it. No moderator names, no member lists, no per-person data of any kind.

Why map the tribes at all

Nobody organizes Toronto from scratch — Toronto is already organized. Hundreds of communities, congregations, cultural associations, clubs, diasporas, fandoms and crews already meet, already care, already act. Connection means inviting the rooms that already exist to talk to each other, not building new ones — and a map is where that starts. This map will never be finished, and that’s the invitation: find your own tribes on it, and send us the ones we missed. The wider doorway to all of this is Connect — find your people.

The map has more than one room

  • The community census — 430 public communities on Reddit, Facebook, Instagram, Discord, and YouTube: the two directory tables this page introduces.
  • How a million people agree — the platforms & consensus research: the tools and methods cities from Taiwan to Barcelona already use to deliberate at scale, and what Toronto could pick up today.
  • Choosing tools for bigger rooms — 86 deliberation and civic-tech tools checked live: which are actually free and self-serve, and the honest answer for an online assembly.
  • The Assembly Simulator — our full working model of a representative Toronto citizens’ assembly, run end-to-end and documented.
  • The Toronto pilot conversation — a small live trial of the consensus tool we recommend first.
  • Find your people — the Free Toronto guide to joining what already gathers, at no cost.

What "confirmed" means, per source

Reddit: confirmed = we visited the subreddit and recorded a verification date. Listing-only = found via subreddit search only, never independently visited.

Facebook/Instagram/Discord/YouTube: these four platforms are largely login-walled to outside research, so "confirmed" here is weaker by construction — it means a search result surfaced the platform's own stated size at least once ("confirmed listing, no size" means the community's existence was corroborated in a directory listing, e.g. Disboard for Discord, without a size figure). Listing-only means the community's existence rests on a single search-result snippet, never independently corroborated. See each page's own per-row marks.

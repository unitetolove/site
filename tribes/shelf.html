The Common Shelf

Every book here is a door — read it, and you can talk with everyone else who’s read it too; a shared shelf turns a phrase like “leverage point” or “third place” from one person’s jargon into the city’s working language. 102 titles, grouped by section, each one linked to whichever free or library door — Toronto Public Library, Libby/OverDrive, an Internet Archive lending copy, or a free legal edition — is actually available for it today.

Behind this shelf sits a fuller reading guide: a core dozen books meant to be read first, a second ring picked by the door you come in through (skeptic, organizer, builder, newcomer, youth), and a plan for how a city could actually read them together in ninety days — that companion guide is still being finished for publication. See a door that’s gone dead, or a book that should be here? Tell us on the Harvest.

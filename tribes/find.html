Commons Map › Find Your People
This tool only ever shows an organization’s own public door — its name, what it is, and its public website. Never a person’s name, never a contact history, no email, no phone. Results are drawn from the same 3,015 verified-live organizations as the full Organization Directory, sorted here by what you’re hoping to do instead of by category.

Find Your People

Answer up to three questions in plain language and get a short list of real rooms you could actually walk into — 2,529 organizations sorted into ten everyday doorways. An empty answer never means “nothing exists” here: if we haven’t mapped your exact combination yet, we show the nearest rooms we do have and how to tell us about the one we’re missing.

1. What are you hoping to do?
2. Where are you?

3. How hard is the door?

Optional — leave this blank to see every door-difficulty.

Pick what you’re hoping to do, above, to see real rooms.

What’s not here yet

Four more questions we’d like this finder to answer aren’t built yet, and we’d rather say so than guess: when a group next meets, what it costs beyond how hard the door is, what language a room runs in, and whether a room is step-free, has ASL, or sits near transit. Each one needs its own dataset first — a citywide calendar of meeting times, a real cost check, language tags, and accessibility checks — and none of those exist yet. We’re building them, not ignoring them; when they land, they’ll be filters here too.

486 organizations in the full directory don’t appear in this finder at all — mostly companies, university departments, and pieces of venue infrastructure rather than a room a resident can walk into. That’s a decision, not a bug: see them all, by category instead, in the full Organization Directory.

See a room we should have found, or a door we got wrong? Tell us on the Harvest — every correction is read.

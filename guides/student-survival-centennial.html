Student Survival — Centennial College

Part of the Free Toronto guide fleet — a plain-language set of guides pulled from the Free Toronto Resource Atlas, our full 765-entry directory of free and low-cost Toronto resources. Who this page is for: Centennial College students. Every link on this page was checked directly against the provider's own page, most recently on 2026-07-28. Neutral civic reference information — not affiliated with any campaign, candidate, or advocacy organization.

Selection rule for this page: an entry qualifies only if it's run by or exclusively for Centennial College students — a CCSAI (Centennial College Student Association Inc.)-run service, a Centennial-run institutional program (Financial Aid, Career Services, CALCS, Libraries), or a Centennial-specific insurance plan gated by student status. Nothing here requires you to already know which office to ask, but everything here does require you to be a Centennial student (or, for the health-plan and health-insurance entries, to already be paying the charge that funds one).

For everything not specific to Centennial — general-population food banks, tax-filing clinics open to any income-qualifying individual, Good2Talk, public legal clinics, discounts, free learning platforms, transit programs, and the honest gaps in student-parent and general childcare coverage — see the cross-campus base guide, Student Survival. For same-night crisis needs (shelter, food, crisis lines), see In Crisis Now. For food-specific resources beyond a campus food bank, see Food Today & Groceries, including anything near Progress (Markham/401) or Story Arts Centre (Carlaw/Dundas) specifically. This page doesn't repeat any of that content — it points to it once, here, and stays focused on what's actually Centennial-specific below.

Which campus: Centennial's own current campuses page (confirmed 2026-07-28) lists seven: Progress, Ashtonbee, Morningside, Story Arts Centre, Downsview, Performing Arts Centre, and Toronto Airways Inc. Every entry below states which campus(es) it actually operates at — a resource being "Centennial-run" or "CCSAI-run" doesn't mean it's available at every campus.

Centennial splits by full-time/part-time and domestic/international — not by undergrad/grad. Centennial is a college, not a university: its "graduate certificate" programs are post-diploma or post-degree credentials, not a separately governed graduate-student population. We checked directly — CCSAI's Learning Essentials Cost Reimbursement Program states its only eligibility test as "currently enrolled in a Centennial College program and have paid the CCSAI Academic Support Fee," with no credential-level distinction, and Centennial's own graduate-certificate program pages name no separate CCSAI membership tier, health plan, or emergency-fund tier for graduate-certificate students. No CCSAI graduate-students' council, no graduate-specific health plan, and no graduate-specific emergency fund were found anywhere in this pass's research. The real splits that matter at Centennial are full-time vs. part-time (the CCSAI Health & Dental Plan and LERP both gate on full-time, fee-paying status) and domestic vs. international (health coverage runs through two entirely separate, non-interchangeable plans — see "I need health, dental, or mental-health support beyond OHIP" below). Every entry states which of those groups it's for.

Most CCSAI-run entries below are already funded by the mandatory CCSAI Academic Support Fee and related ancillary fees every Centennial student pays — in CCSAI's own framing, these are "included in fees you already pay," not free gifts on top of your tuition.

I need food this week

CCSAI Food Bank Pre-packaged food hampers meant to cover 2–3 days of meals — non-perishable, dietary restrictions accommodated during packing. Campus hours: Ashtonbee Wed 9am–12pm; Downsview Tue 10am–2pm; Morningside Tue 9am–12pm; Progress Tue & Thu 9:30am–12pm; Story Arts Centre Thu 9am–12pm; Performing Arts Centre biweekly Thursdays 9:30am–12pm. Progress campus once every 7 days max; any other campus once every 14 days max. Register with Daily Bread Food Bank first, then complete the appointment request form — confirmation within 48 hours, then a 15-minute slot. Bring your myCard and photo ID (plus proof of ID for any household member you're claiming for). ccsai.ca/food-bank For: any current Centennial College student, any campus. Appointment required (not a pure walk-in) — register with Daily Bread Food Bank, then book.

CCSAI Grab-and-Go Breakfast Light breakfast items (granola bars, fruit, juice boxes, baked goods, cereal) handed out first-come, first-served while supplies last. Weekly: Tue 9am–12pm at Morningside and Story Arts Centre; Wed 9am–12pm at Ashtonbee and Progress; Thu 10am–2pm at Downsview. No distribution at Performing Arts Centre. Monthly specialty bread-and-spread breakfasts run May–August at rotating campuses. ccsai.ca/breakfast For: Centennial students (presumed) — the provider's own page doesn't state who counts as eligible, and never says the word "free." Given it's a CCSAI (student-association) program, treat it as a Centennial-student benefit unless you find otherwise; cost isn't confirmed either — a genuine gap in the provider's own page, not something this guide is willing to guess at.

I need money — CCSAI advocacy and gear-cost reimbursement

CCSAI Student Advocacy Service Free, confidential advocacy from a CCSAI student advocate for grade appeals, issues with faculty, potential government-related concerns (e.g., a labour strike affecting classes), understanding college policies and procedures, domestic and international student concerns, and other issues affecting your student experience. Email the service with your advocacy need; a student advocate responds within up to two business days. ccsai.ca/services/advocacy For: all Centennial College students, any campus. No documents required to start; free and confidential.

Learning Essentials Cost Reimbursement Program (LERP) Reimburses 50% of the cost of mandatory program-specific learning essentials (equipment, tools, or materials required for coursework — not tuition or textbooks), capped at $100 per student per semester, for up to two semesters an academic year (Sept–Apr). Recipients are chosen by random draw, not first-come-first-served — applying doesn't guarantee an award. Needs a proof-of-purchase receipt (no bank statements or photos) plus proof the item was actually required (a course outline or program communication). Summer 2026 window ran May 19–Jul 24, 2026 — almost certainly closed by the time you're reading this; confirm the current term's dates. ccsai.ca/learning-essentials For: Centennial College students who've paid the CCSAI Academic Support Fee, any campus. One application per semester; selection is a random draw among applicants, not guaranteed.

Honest gap: no dedicated emergency/hardship bursary found — re-verified this pass. Unlike Seneca's Institution-Funded Special Bursary or Humber's IGNITE Emergency Financial Relief Bursary, we checked two of Centennial's own Financial Aid pages directly this pass (the Scholarship and Bursary FAQ, and the Financial Supports and Literacy hub) and found no emergency-loan product, short-notice hardship fund, or faculty/school-level emergency fund named on either — only the standard Fall/Winter/Summer scholarship-and-bursary competitions (applied for in advance) and a generic pointer to "budgeting tools, financial coaching, tax clinics, food supports." If you're in a genuine financial emergency, the Student Advocacy service above or LERP above cover narrower slices of the problem; call Financial Aid (416-289-5000) directly and ask what's actually available for your specific situation, since a phone conversation may surface something page-level research can't.

I need health, dental, or mental-health support beyond OHIP

Centennial's coverage runs through two entirely separate plans, not one plan with a grad/ undergrad split — which one applies to you depends on whether you're a domestic or international student, and they are not interchangeable.

CALCS Personal Counselling and Mental Health Support Same-day, single-session counselling focused on practical next steps, plus solution-focused brief therapy (SFBT) for structured, goal-based support — run directly by the college, not CCSAI. Book online, by phone, or by email. Not a crisis line — Monday–Friday, regular business hours only; after-hours crisis situations are referred out. 437-568-4799 · calcs@centennialcollege.ca · book online Personal Counselling and Mental Health Support (Centennial College) For: Centennial students (presumed) — honesty flag, re-attempted this pass and still unresolved: neither this page nor its own FAQ sub-page (which 404'd on this pass's fetch, after a JS-rendering failure on the prior pass) state cost or explicit eligibility in words we could read. Two independent failed attempts across v0.1 and v0.2 — call or email CALCS directly to confirm cost and which campuses run in-person sessions before planning around it.

CCSAI Health & Dental Plan — free 24/7 counselling line included (domestic students) The paid health/dental plan itself (WeSpeakStudent) is not free — $93.84/semester, automatic for full-time domestic students in in-person programs, with prescription/dental/vision/paramedical coverage. What matters most for this section: it includes a 24/7 Student Assistance Program (SAP) — counselling, mental-health support, and wellness coaching via Dialogue — at no extra cost, and this piece survives even if you opt out of the rest of the plan (opting out keeps a $10 portion of the fee specifically for the mental-health-access piece). 1-855-853-0565 · app.dialogue.co ccsai.ca/health-plan For: Centennial domestic, full-time, in-person students (co-op, apprenticeship, and part-time students aren't automatically enrolled). Does not apply to international students — see the international plan below.

International Student Health Insurance (international students) — closes the gap v0.1 left unresearched All registered international students are automatically covered by a mandatory comprehensive health plan through guard.me, included in fees, from the first day of your first enrolled month. Covers emergency medical services valid worldwide except your country of origin. International Education — Health Insurance · guard.me insurance detail For: international Centennial students only, any campus (institution-wide plan, run by International Education, not CCSAI). Honesty flag: the provider's own pages confirm the plan is mandatory and fee-included but do not publish a dollar premium or resolve their own "Can I Opt Out?" FAQ question in readable text — call International Education to confirm cost and opt-out terms before planning around it. Not interchangeable with the domestic CCSAI plan above — different administrator, different rules.

I need free or low-cost legal help

CCSAI Legal Services Legal advice on landlord/tenant rights, document notarization, and affidavit preparation, in 15-minute blocks every Tuesday (9–11:30am, 11:30am–1:30pm, or 1:30–3pm), in-person, by phone, or via Zoom depending on the date. Cannot represent you in ongoing litigation, court proceedings, or immigration matters — this is advice, not representation. Book via the online form; a team member confirms by email. 416-289-5262 · ccsai@centennialcollege.ca · 941 Progress Ave. ccsai.ca/legal-services For: currently enrolled Centennial College students only. Honesty flag: the provider's page never says "free" outright — no cost is mentioned anywhere, read here as free-at-point-of-use consistent with how CCSAI funds its other member services. High demand — appointments fill quickly.

I need a laptop, camera, or other tech gear

Centennial College Library Laptop & Equipment Lending Free short-term equipment loans for currently enrolled students: laptops (4-hour loan — self-serve kiosks at Progress and Morningside, desk-based at Downsview and Ashtonbee), Emerging Technology items (7-day loan, students and employees both eligible), iPads (7-day loan, Downsview only), headsets and computer accessories (same-day, due 30 minutes before closing). Bring your myCard or library card and sign a Library Equipment Loan Agreement form first. Academic use only — you're financially responsible for repair or replacement if equipment is lost, damaged, or stolen. library.centennialcollege.ca — Computers & Equipment For: currently enrolled Centennial College students (and employees, for Emerging Technology items). No deposit, but late fines apply: laptops $5/hour, Emerging Technology $10/day, iPads $25/day, headsets/accessories $1/hour or $1/day — outstanding fines can place a hold on your registration or transcript requests. Note the 4-hour laptop window is much shorter than Seneca's 3-day or Humber's up-to-full-semester loans — don't expect to take it home overnight.

I need free software or cloud storage

Centennial College Free Microsoft Office 365 Office 365 — including OneDrive, SharePoint and Teams — at no cost for Centennial students. Log in through the myCentennial portal and click through to the Office 365 site. libraryguides.centennialcollege.ca — Collaboration Tools For: Centennial students, any campus (online licence portal, not location-specific). Honesty gap: no Centennial-specific free Adobe Creative Cloud program was found this pass, unlike Seneca's — searched, not located, recorded honestly rather than assumed absent forever.

I need career coaching or job-search help

Centennial College Career Services — Advising & Drop-in Coaching Two free options run directly by the college, not CCSAI: a 20-minute virtual Drop-in Session with a Peer Career Coach covering resume formatting, job search, networking, and interview prep (aimed at first-time users); and individual appointments with Employment Advisors for career exploration, career planning/decision-making, career change, job-search goals, and networking tips. Book or drop in through ExperienceCentennial. Career Services — Advising Services For: Centennial students, no program or campus restriction stated (booking centralized through ExperienceCentennial). Honesty flag: cost is never stated in words on the page — read as likely free (standard institutional student-support service) but not confirmed; recorded as unknown rather than assumed.

Honest gap: no career closet found. Neither CCSAI's services list nor Centennial's own career-services pages turned up a free professional-clothing program this pass (re-searched, second attempt) — unlike Seneca's Career Threads (Seneca Works) or comparable services at other Ontario colleges. If a future pass finds one this search missed, it belongs here.

Fitness and recreation — real, but not free

Centennial College Athletic & Wellness Centre (AWC) Full gym membership — ~65,000 sq ft gymnasium, 154-metre indoor track, cardio-fitness area, two aerobic studios, squash courts, rock-climbing wall (drop-in Tue/Thu 1–4pm winter hours), locker rooms and showers — automatically included for full-time students. Not free: $75/semester, bundled into tuition as a mandatory ancillary fee — no opt-out mechanism found this pass. Winter hours Mon–Fri 8am–8pm, closed weekends. Book squash courts online or at Guest Services. awc.ccsai.ca/memberships For: full-time Centennial students, bring your myCard. Honesty flag, re-attempted this pass: the AWC's own pages don't state which physical campus the facility is on, or whether every campus has equal access. Separate "Ashtonbee Fitness Centre" and "Morningside Fitness Centre" listings exist on the Athletics site (gocolts.ca) but returned a 403 error on this pass's fetch attempt (two attempts, one per page) — still not independently verified. Don't assume full multi-campus parity without checking locally. Part-time-student eligibility and any opt-out path weren't found on the pages checked.

This is flagged plainly as not free rather than filed under "recreation you can just use," because "included in mandatory fees" is a materially different promise than "free" — you're already paying for it whether you use it or not, and a reader budgeting month-to-month deserves to know that up front.

Getting between campuses

Honest gap: no CCSAI inter-campus shuttle found. Unlike Seneca's SSF Shuttle Bus or Humber's equivalent, CCSAI's "Transit" page turned out to be purely informational — it describes the GO Transit Student ID, the Metrolinx One Fare discount, and the TTC Student Photo ID ($5.25 card unlocking the $128.15/month student pass vs. $156.00 adult), none of which are Centennial-run services or free. Not cited as a entry here since these are general TTC/GO discount programs, not Centennial-specific benefits.

I'm a First Nations, Métis, or Inuit student

Honest gap: no bookable Centennial-specific Indigenous student service confirmed this pass. Centennial runs an institutional "Indigenous Education Services" office and a linked "Indigenous Reconciliation and Healing (The Eighth Fire)" initiative, but neither page surfaced a specific student-facing service (drop-in counselling, peer support, dedicated bursary-recommendation pathway) in text we could find — The Eighth Fire reads as a strategic/leadership office rather than a services directory comparable to Seneca's First Peoples @ Seneca. Stopped after two fetch/search attempts per cost discipline; flagged for a future pass rather than published on an unconfirmed basis.

Honest gaps — what we looked for and didn't find (or found, but couldn't fully confirm)

  • No campus-hosted-but-public service. Every Centennial/CCSAI resource we verified is restricted to Centennial students. Nothing Centennial-run turned up this pass that a non-Centennial Torontonian could walk into.
  • No dedicated emergency/hardship bursary — re-verified this pass on two Financial Aid pages directly (see the money section above). LERP covers gear costs only, capped at $100/semester via random draw; the Student Advocacy service can help you navigate the system but doesn't disburse money. Neither is a general hardship fund.
  • No dedicated on-campus tax clinic found — Centennial's own tax-information page points students to general CRA resources rather than a CCSAI-run or campus-hosted CVITP clinic (contrast Seneca's SSF Tax Clinic and Humber's IGNITE Free Tax Clinic). Use the general-population Community Volunteer Income Tax Program (CVITP) cited in the G04 base guide instead.
  • No career closet / professional-clothing program found — re-searched this pass, still nothing.
  • No CCSAI inter-campus shuttle found — CCSAI's "Transit" page is a discounts directory, not a shuttle service.
  • No campus-specific childcare or student-parent bursary entry found for Centennial — the same gap the G04 base guide flags for UofT/TMU/York and the per-campus Seneca/Humber guides flag for their own institutions.
  • No bookable Centennial-specific Indigenous student service confirmed — an institutional reconciliation office exists (The Eighth Fire) but no specific student-facing service was surfaced in readable page text this pass; flagged for a future check.
  • CALCS cost and campus coverage genuinely unconfirmed — two independent failed fetch attempts across v0.1 and v0.2; call or email to confirm before relying on it.
  • CCSAI Grab-and-Go Breakfast eligibility and cost genuinely unconfirmed — the provider's own page never states either; recorded as unknown rather than assumed free.
  • AWC multi-campus coverage not independently confirmed — separate Morningside and Ashtonbee fitness-centre listings exist on the Athletics site but returned a 403 error on this pass's fetch attempt; don't assume identical access at every campus.
  • International-student health insurance premium not published — the provider's own pages confirm mandatory, fee-included coverage through guard.me, but not a dollar figure or a resolved opt-out answer; call International Education to confirm.
  • No Centennial-specific free Adobe Creative Cloud found — searched, not located this pass; Office 365 is confirmed free, Adobe is not confirmed either way.
  • CCSAI Career Services advising cost not explicitly stated — read as likely free (standard institutional student support) but not confirmed in words on the page.

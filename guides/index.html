Free Toronto Guides

Twenty short, plain-language guides, each pulled from the Free Toronto Resource Atlas — our full 765-entry directory of free and low-cost Toronto resources — and organized around one need at a time instead of one big page. Pick the one that matches what you're dealing with right now. Every link in every guide was checked directly against the provider's own page. Neutral civic reference information — not affiliated with any campaign, candidate, or advocacy organization.

Want the whole picture instead? These guides are slices of one larger project — the Free Toronto Resource Atlas — organized by topic for quick reading. The Atlas itself is the full, single-page reference with everything in one place, including a "need-first" jump menu covering situations these guides don't each get their own page for yet.

Right now

  • In Crisis Now — same-night crisis lines, shelter beds, suicide and violence support, and emergency safety doors. No appointment needed. Start here if tonight is the emergency.

Money & benefits

  • Claim Everything — free tax filing and the income benefits, credits, and rebates it unlocks.
  • Jobs & Employment Services — free help finding and getting a job: search help, counselling, and placement programs.

Housing & bills

  • Housing: Rent Help, Eviction Defence & Shelter — for the housing crisis that isn't tonight's emergency: rent arrears, eviction defence, subsidized and supportive housing.
  • Utilities & Bills — help with hydro, gas, water, and property-tax bills, before and after a disconnection notice.
  • Phone, Internet & Computer — getting and keeping a phone line, getting online, and getting a working device.

Food

  • Food Today & Groceries — hot meals and food banks for right now, plus ways to stretch a grocery budget week to week.

Health

  • Health Extras: Dental, Drugs, Eyes & More — the health costs OHIP doesn't cover: teeth, prescriptions, glasses, hearing support, and more.
  • Mental Health & Addiction — Ongoing Care — counselling, warm lines, groups, and addiction support for after tonight (for same-night crisis care, see In Crisis Now instead).

Family

  • Single Parents — money, child care, and housing help for parents doing it alone.

Fun & connection

  • Free Fun: Events, Music, Movies/TV/Sports & Attractions — free things to do in Toronto year-round, plus what's actually running this season.

Students

Start with the cross-campus base guide, then move to your own school's guide for what's specific to it — a students' union bursary, a campus food bank, a campus clinic, or a campus health plan won't be repeated on the base guide.

  • Student Survival — cross-campus supports for any Toronto post-secondary student: money, food, health, and where to find your own school's programs. Start here first.
  • Student Survival — University of Toronto (St. George, UTSC, UTM)
  • Student Survival — Toronto Metropolitan University (TMU)
  • Student Survival — York University (Keele + Glendon)
  • Student Survival — OCAD University
  • Student Survival — George Brown College
  • Student Survival — Humber Polytechnic (+ University of Guelph-Humber)
  • Student Survival — Seneca Polytechnic
  • Student Survival — Centennial College

Still in preparation, honestly

A few more guides are planned but not ready to publish yet — we'd rather tell you they're coming than pad this page with something half-checked. Not here yet: a dedicated guide to free training, credentials, and skills-grant programs (academic upgrading, bootcamps, apprenticeship training); a dedicated ESL and workplace-language training guide; a dedicated volunteering guide; and a dedicated Veterans guide. Until those exist, related entries are cited inside the guides above where they fit. If you know of something that should be in one of these, tell us — it'll help when we build them.

Found something wrong, closed, or missing in any guide? Tell us — Corrections. Want the full, single-page reference instead of the topic-by-topic split? Go to the Free Toronto Resource Atlas.

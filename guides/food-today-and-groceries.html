Food Today & Groceries

If you need to eat today, start with the first section. Most of what's listed there — hot meals, drop-ins, community fridges — is walk-in: no appointment, no registration, no waiting list. A few food banks in that same section are the exception and require calling ahead to book; each one opens with a bolded call-first note so you can tell at a glance which doors are open right now and which need a call first. Once today is handled, the sections below cover food for kids at school (school year only — September to June; if you're reading this outside those months, the first section above is where to look instead), stretching a grocery budget week to week through discount practices, campus food banks, and cultural or special-diet food programs.

I need food today

Food banks, hot meals and drop-ins — most are walk-in; a few food banks require calling ahead (marked in bold below).

Community Fridges Toronto (CFTO) 24/7 self-serve public fridges and pantries across the city — take what you need, leave what you can. No registration or check-in of any kind. communityfridgesto.org Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in, any time — no documents, no immigration-status check.

Sikh Spiritual Centre Toronto — Langar Free meals served daily to anyone who walks in, regardless of background, faith, or immigration status. Located at 9 Carrier Drive (Rexdale). Building hours are daily 4:00am–9:00pm; the specific Langar serving windows aren't itemized on the site — call ahead if timing matters. ssctoronto.org Who it's for: anyone, including undocumented people. Walk in. Phone: 416-746-6666.

ISKCON Toronto — Sunday Feast Free vegetarian feast every Sunday, 5:30–8:30pm, with kirtan and a spiritual discourse beforehand — open to congregants and newcomers alike. Sponsoring a feast (birthdays, memorials) is optional, never required to attend. 243 Avenue Road. torontokrishna.com/sunday-feast Who it's for: anyone. Walk in. Sundays only. Phone: (416) 922-5415.

Toronto Lawyers Feed the Hungry Free hot meals four times a week from the Osgoode Hall cafeteria (130 Queen St W) — Tuesday and Wednesday dinner 5:00pm, Thursday breakfast 6:45am, Sunday brunch 10:00am. Dine-in or takeout. Roughly 1,200 meals a week, staffed entirely by volunteer judges, lawyers and law students, part of a province-wide Feed the Hungry network serving 75,000+ meals a year across nine Ontario centres. lso.ca — hunger relief Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in.

Good Shepherd Ministries — Community Meals Free meals 365 days a year for anyone experiencing homelessness, poverty or food insecurity — now the largest free meal program in the City of Toronto, serving 376,473 meals in 2025. A Morning Drop-In runs 9:00–10:00am with light refreshments and a safe space to connect with support services; the main hot meal is served daily 2:00–4:00pm. Open to seniors, people receiving disability payments, people living in poverty, and homeless men and women — broadly open, not restricted to one group. 412 Queen St East. goodshepherd.ca/community-meals Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in.

Haven Toronto Free meals for men 50+ only, plus clothing, hot showers and laundry — specifically for men experiencing homelessness or at risk. The provider's own site is internally inconsistent about meal times: a bullet list says meals run "3 times daily," but the body text says "twice daily at 8am and noon." Call to confirm before planning around a specific time. Open every day 8am–4pm, 365 days a year. Now located at 170 Jarvis Street. haventoronto.ca Who it's for: men 50+, Toronto. Walk in — no appointment necessary. Phone: 416-366-5377 x227.

The Scott Mission — Welcome Centre Meals Free hot breakfast (8:00–8:30am) and lunch (11:30am–12:00pm), dine-in or take-out. 502 Spadina Ave. Available Monday–Saturday only — closed Sunday. Bag lunches are no longer offered. (Separate from Scott Mission's own showers/laundry/ID-clinic drop-in, and from its men's shelter, now located at 346 Spadina Ave.) scottmission.com Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in. Meals line: 416-923-3916.

Yonge Street Mission — Food Food bank: appointment only, once every 2 weeks — call 416-929-9614 x3200. You must live south of Bloor St, east of Yonge St, west of Don Valley Parkway. First visit needs photo ID for all household members, proof of address, and proof of recent income. Food bank hours: Tue–Fri 10am–12pm & 1–3pm, 270 Gerrard St E; closed Mon/Sat/Sun and Dec 24–Jan 2. A separate hot-meal service also runs (dine-in or takeaway, plus weekday meals specifically for youth 16–24), but its walk-in status couldn't be confirmed on YSM's own site this review — call ahead to ask. ysm.ca/get-help Who it's for: food bank — Downtown East catchment above, appointment required. Hot meals — unconfirmed walk-in status, call 416-929-9614 to ask.

Evangel Hall Mission — Drop-In Centre Free meals, washroom and hygiene-product access, hot showers and laundry (registration from 9am), Mon–Fri 9:00am–12:00pm, plus Sunday dinner 5:00–6:00pm with a worship service at 6:00pm. 552 Adelaide St W. Weekly clothing bank (Tuesdays 9–10am) and Tuesday nursing care (10:30am–12pm) also on-site. evangelhall.ca Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in. Phone: 416-504-3563.

Salvation Army Gateway — Drop-In & Take-Away Meals Sit-down meals Tuesdays/Fridays 12:00–1:30pm within a broader drop-in open 11am–3pm; take-away meals on the front ramp Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays at 5:00pm. 107 Jarvis St. thegateway.ca/help Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in. Front desk: (416) 368-0324.

All Saints Church-Community Centre — Meal Program Free breakfast (9:30am, takeout 10am) and lunch (12:30pm, takeout 1pm) Monday, Tuesday and Thursday; lunch only Wednesday. Closed the 1st Wednesday of each month for staff training. Framed around recovery from addiction and homelessness. 315 Dundas St E. allsaintstoronto.com Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in. Phone: 416-368-7768.

PARC — Drop-In & Community Meals Free daily breakfast and lunch, seven days a week, no referral or intake required, plus showers and laundry. Drop-in hours Mon–Thu 9am–1pm, Fri 12:30–4:30pm, Sat/Sun/holidays 11am–3pm. 1499 Queen St W, Parkdale. parc.on.ca/parc-programs/drop-in Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in during drop-in hours — no documents required. Phone: (416) 537-2262.

Muslim Welfare Canada — food & clinic services Food bank is appointment-only at both sites — call ahead. Scarborough: 416-335-9994, Mon–Sat 11am–4pm. Mississauga: 905-281-9730, Sun–Thu 11am–4pm. The bagged-lunch program (Halal Meals on Wheels) is delivered by volunteers directly to shelters in downtown Toronto — it is not a public walk-up site. Also runs a free medical clinic for the uninsured: appointment only, no walk-ins, 647-641-1027, Mon–Fri 11am–5pm booking window. mwcanada.org/freeclinics Who it's for: food bank — Scarborough/Mississauga, appointment only. Medical clinic — uninsured people including new immigrants, refugees, students and visitors, appointment only.

Food banks (bring ID if you have it — most will still try to help without; a few require

calling ahead to book before ID even comes up — look for the bolded call-first note)

Daily Bread Food Bank Central hub routing to food programs at 100+ member agencies across Toronto, via a Find-a-Food-Bank locator and booking portal. No ID required for a single person — ID is needed only for other household members you're registering. Limit: one member food bank visit per week, network-wide. Some member sites have their own catchment restrictions or need an appointment — check your specific location. dailybread.ca — programs by location Who it's for: income-qualifying households, Toronto. Walk in or book online — rules vary by member site. General questions: 416-203-0050 ext 1.

North York Harvest Food Bank Food distribution via 77 community programs delivered through 38 partner agencies in northern Toronto, serving 30,000+ people monthly. To find a location or book an appointment, call the main referral line. northyorkharvest.com — find a food bank Who it's for: income-qualifying households, north Toronto. Call 416-635-7771 ext. 4600 or email referrals@northyorkharvest.com — docs vary by partner site.

The Stop Community Food Centre Food bank (Mondays and Fridays, 10am–2pm) at 1884 Davenport Rd, two drop-in meal locations (~431 meals/day average across 1884 Davenport and Wychwood Open Door at 729 St. Clair West), community kitchens and gardens, a Good Food Market (Tuesdays 12–2pm), plus a tax & ID clinic and perinatal support. The food bank's own catchment area is North of Bloor, South of St. Clair, East of Runnymede, West of Bathurst; households inside it can access once a month, households outside it once every six months. First-time visitors are asked for government ID for all household members plus proof of address — the drop-in meals and kitchens are lower-barrier than the food bank itself. thestop.org Who it's for: anyone, Toronto — food bank catchment-restricted, drop-in meals open to all. Phone: 416-652-7867.

Fort York Food Bank (FYFB) FYFB's own site gives two different sets of hours for Wed/Thu/Sat — call to confirm before you go. Grocery service: Tue & Fri 9am–4pm (numbered queue, first 100 people get a number at 8:45am); Wed/Thu/Sat either 9am–2pm or 9am–12pm depending on which FYFB page you check. Sunday hot meal from 12pm — exact end time and "while supplies last" framing also disagree across the site. 380 College St. fyfb.com Who it's for: income-qualifying households, Toronto. Walk in — no published catchment or document requirement. Phone: 416-203-3011.

Haven on the Queensway Etobicoke residents only. Food bank is by appointment only — call 416-640-2005 (press 3), and you may only call to book on a Tuesday morning, for an appointment the following week. Food Bank's own hours: Wednesday 9:00am–3:00pm, Thursday 1:00pm–6:00pm (different from the centre's general office hours). Also runs First Care (pregnant women/parents of newborns to age 2), Haven's Closet (seasonal clothing), Haven Helping Seniors (food delivery/hygiene kits), and Hope With Wheels (hot meals for people experiencing homelessness in south Etobicoke, downtown Toronto and Mississauga). Some sub-programs are further restricted to specific south-Etobicoke postal codes (M8V/W/X/Y/Z, M9A/B/C/P/R/V/W) — call to confirm yours. havenontheq.org Who it's for: Etobicoke residents only. Food bank — appointment required, Tuesday-morning booking window only.

Syme Woolner Neighbourhood & Family Centre — Food Bank Program The provider's own page opens by stating access is "by appointment" — don't assume 190 Woolner is a pure walk-in without calling first. 190 Woolner Ave: Thu/Fri 9:00am–1:30pm. 2468 Eglinton Ave W: Fridays 9:30am–1:30pm, appointment only, call (416) 766-4634. You may register at only one of the two sites, not both. Also runs a monthly Clothing Bank. symewoolner.org Who it's for: income-qualifying households, York/Weston. Call to confirm before assuming walk-in at either site.

If you need a food bank built around a specific diet or faith

Chasdei Kaduri — Jewish Food Bank Free weekly home delivery of Kosher non-perishable groceries, fresh bakery goods, produce, eggs, dairy and hygiene supplies, chosen by approved clients through an online "shop," delivered Wednesdays 7–11pm. For Jewish families of any denomination living below the Government of Canada poverty line. chasdeikaduri.org/get-help Who it's for: income-qualifying Jewish households. GTA note: this is a delivery-only service based in Vaughan — the organization's own page confirms its office is "NOT open to the general public," and its delivery area runs from Major Mackenzie to Lawrence, between Dufferin and Yonge. That covers North York/North Toronto up to Lawrence Ave but does not reach most of the City of Toronto proper. Requires an online application plus a Notice of Assessment and 3 months of bank statements; review takes about 2 weeks. Phone: (289) 963-5900.

PWA Toronto — Practical Supports (Essentials Market) Registration process isn't published on PWA's own site — call (416) 506-1400 to ask whether Essentials Market is walk-in or requires client registration first. Food bank offering staple and culturally-appropriate foods, fresh produce, pet food, home start-up items, family hampers, special-diet accommodation and baby items, plus free multivitamins every 3 months and holiday food programs. PWA's Financial Assistance program (for HIV-related costs) is currently curtailed to emergency-only cases, pending PWA's own August 2026 Bike Rally fundraiser — it is not a standing fund right now; call (416) 506-1400 to ask before relying on it. Essentials Market days: Wed/Thu 2–7pm, Fri 12–5pm (closed Mon/Tue). 163 Queen St E, 2nd Fl. pwatoronto.org/practical-supports Who it's for: people living with HIV/AIDS, Toronto — the only food bank in Toronto that exclusively serves this community. Phone (416) 506-1400 to reach them. For the fuller picture of what PWA and the rest of this atlas offer someone living with HIV — medical care, testing, legal help, housing and more — see this atlas's "Living with HIV" guide.

If you have a pet to feed too

Toronto Humane Society Pet Food Bank Free pet food and supplies for cats and dogs — 190,000+ lbs distributed in 2026. Walk in at 11 River St. Hours: Mon–Fri 9:00am–7:00pm, Sat–Sun 10:00am–6:00pm. Community Day is the 2nd and 4th Wednesday of each month (not simply "every other Wednesday"), opening later at 1:00pm those days. For households facing financial hardship; no formal income proof required, self-declared need. Active donation shortage as of this update — call ahead to confirm stock is available before you go. torontohumanesociety.com — pet food bank Who it's for: anyone facing hardship, Toronto. Walk in. Phone: (416) 392-2273.

Toronto Cat Rescue — Pet Food Assistance A volunteer-run cat-food donation effort (drop-off points include Weston Area Emergency Services, Fred Victor Shelter and OASIS Food Bank) plus a referral list to the Toronto Humane Society, Daily Bread and PWA Toronto (PWA's food bank is restricted to people living with HIV/AIDS). Honesty flag: donation-dependent, not a guaranteed standing service — the provider's own page confirms this is opportunistic, "whenever possible" drop-offs, not a scheduled program. Treat the Humane Society's pet food bank above as the more reliable first call. torontocatrescue.ca — food assistance Who it's for: cat owners, Toronto. Online or phone (416-538-8592).

Getting food to you if you can't get to it

The Bike Brigade Free last-mile bike delivery of food and essentials, a network of 1,000+ volunteer cyclists working with food banks and other Toronto nonprofits (Community Fridges TO, Fort York Food Bank and others among past/current partners). This is a delivery/logistics layer, not a direct client intake — if you need food, contact one of the partner organizations directly (several are listed above); they may use Bike Brigade to get it to you. bikebrigade.ca Who it's for: anyone, Toronto, through a partner organization.

My kids need food at school (school year only — see "I need food today" above if it's summer)

Student Nutrition Program (SNP) Runs during the school year only — September through June. It is not running as of this writing (2026-07-30, mid-summer) and won't resume until the fall term. When school is in session: free breakfast, morning meal, lunch and/or snack programs at Toronto schools and community sites, universally available to every student regardless of family income — no application, no means test. Over 230,000 Toronto students participate daily. Access is set at the school/site level, so which meals are offered and how often varies by school; once the school year resumes, ask your child's school office what's running there. Need to feed a kid today, right now, in summer? Go back to "I need food today" above — Community Fridges Toronto, Good Shepherd Ministries and the other walk-in doors listed there serve anyone, kids included, no eligibility test. Children enrolled in a CampTO day camp (late June–August) can also get a free daily snack through the City's separate CampTO Nutrition Program; CampTO camp registration itself may carry its own fee unless subsidized. toronto.ca — Student Nutrition Program Who it's for: all students, Toronto, September through June only — no eligibility test, no family-level application.

I'm a student and need food on or near campus

Campus-specific food banks, mostly appointment- or registration-based rather than pure walk-in — plan a day or two ahead where noted.

UTSU Food Bank (University of Toronto) Free, year-round campus food bank for any U of T student facing food insecurity, single students and students with families alike, running since 2001. Appointment required, not a walk-in. Register for a free Daily Bread Client ID, then book via Calendly (opens Wednesdays 9:30am for the following Friday). Summer 2026 schedule: Fridays 11am–3pm, running through Aug 28, 2026 — Fall/Winter 2026-27 dates aren't posted yet, confirm before relying on this outside summer. Room 136, Student Commons, 230 College St. utsu.ca/food-bank Who it's for: U of T students. Appointment required; bring a T-card or current timetable plus reusable bags.

Good Food Centre (Toronto Metropolitan University) Free campus food bank for any TMU student facing food insecurity. Must register — same-day in-person registration available, or book an advance appointment; not unconditional walk-in. Hours Mon/Wed/Thu 12–3pm, Tue 2–4pm (no Friday). SCC B-03A (basement), 55 Gould St. A separate site note references "appointment-only after 3:00 PM" during summer — unclear whether this governs the Good Food Centre specifically; confirm before a late visit. yourtmsu.ca/services/food Who it's for: TMU students. Walk in same-day, or by appointment. Phone: (416) 979-5000 ext. 552362.

YFS Food Support Centre (York University) Contact before visiting — email foodcentre@yfs.ca or call 416-736-2100 ext. 44861 first; not a walk-in. Free non-perishable food and basic-need items for York students who can no longer afford them. 005 Second Student Centre, Keele campus. Hours are published only as an image graphic on the org's page and couldn't be confirmed as text; document requirements aren't stated either — confirm both when you reach out. yfs.ca/fsc Who it's for: York University students. Email or phone first — not a pure walk-in.

Free YFS Grocery Shuttle (York University) Free shuttle bus looping between York's Keele campus and a FreshCo grocery store (Bathurst & Steeles) on select fall/winter semester evenings, 6–9pm. Pickup points: Calumet Loop, Second Student Centre lot, Winters Bus Loop. Honesty flag, reconfirmed again 2026-07-30: the service's own page still reads "Our YFS Grocery Shuttle is back!" but both the Fall and Winter 2026/27 semester dates still say "check back later for the official dates." The service is real and has run in prior semesters, but the current schedule still isn't published. Confirm current dates before planning around it. yfs.ca/groceryshuttle Who it's for: York University students. Seasonal — fall/winter semester evenings only.

I want to stretch my grocery budget

Apps, markdown routines and discount programs. A few of these are crowd-reported shopper practices, not official store policy — the venue is always real and verified, but the specific discount routine may vary by location or change without notice; these are marked clearly below.

Flashfood App showing near-expiry surplus groceries (produce, meat, bakery) at up to 50% off for pickup at partner grocery stores. In Toronto this means Loblaw-banner stores only (No Frills, Real Canadian Superstore, Loblaws, Zehrs, etc.) — Metro is not a Flashfood partner. flashfood.com Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Free app; savings depend on how often you use it and what's posted locally.

Too Good To Go App for reserving "Surprise Bags" of unsold food from Toronto restaurants, cafes, bakeries and some grocers, priced at about half of retail value or less per the app's own claim (exact savings vary by bag and location). toogoodtogo.com/en-ca Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Free app; pay in-app, pick up in the assigned window.

Flipp Digital flyer browser and shopping-list app; shows a clipped flyer deal at checkout for price matching. Price-match acceptance varies by store and has narrowed in recent years — No Frills, Real Canadian Superstore, Food Basics, FreshCo and Giant Tiger are commonly cited as still accepting it in most markets, but confirm at checkout. flipp.com Who it's for: anyone, anywhere. Free app.

savr.app App comparing real-time prices across 15+ nearby grocery chains to route the cheapest weekly shop. Free to sign up per the provider's own marketing; the site itself could not be independently verified for hidden paid tiers due to a JavaScript-only page. savr.app Who it's for: anyone, Toronto.

FoodShare Toronto Community Markets Below-market-price fresh, culturally relevant produce markets in underserved neighbourhoods — a network of 50+ community- or agency-run markets citywide, serving 70,000+ people a year. Most are open walk-in, but a few operate inside seniors' or community-housing buildings and may be less accessible to drop-in visitors — check FoodShare's market map for your nearest site's hours. foodshare.net — community markets Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in.

FoodShare Good Food Box Below-market-price boxes of fresh produce, ordered online at shop.foodshare.net directly for pickup or delivery (Toronto + some Mississauga addresses). FoodShare's main site now routes its "Shop" nav link to a wholesale/agency page — don't follow that link expecting an individual order form. Subsidized, not free; exact current pricing could not be independently confirmed this review. foodshare.net Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Online order; subsidized, not free.

Feed It Forward (pay-what-you-can grocery store) Groceries, bakery items and prepared food priced pay-what-you-can, or free if you can't pay, made from rescued surplus food. 2770 Dundas St W, Unit A. Open Mon–Sat 11am–6pm, Sun 11am–3pm. feeditforward.ca/store-front Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in — pay what you can, or nothing. Phone: (416) 786-4126.

Not Far From The Tree Volunteer fruit-picking service: homeowners with more fruit than they can use register a tree, volunteer pickers show up, and the harvest splits three ways between the tree owner, the pickers, and a community food-security agency. Season runs mid-June to mid-October, mostly downtown core — currently active. Registering invites a "Harvest Share" contribution ($40/season for tree owners, $10 for pickers) — described as optional and waived for anyone who asks, no questions asked, but not simply free by default. notfarfromthetree.org Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Seasonal. Phone: 416-658-0724.

FoodShare Community Kitchens Free guided cooking workshops in small groups, cooking from scratch with fresh seasonal ingredients, at FoodShare's head office (120C Industry St) and satellite locations. Book online via registration form. foodshare.net — community kitchens Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Skill-building, not a direct grocery-cost offset — free. Note: kitchen handles nuts, soy, shellfish, dairy, eggs and other common allergens.

St. Lawrence Market Saturday closing markdowns Crowd-reported shopper practice, not an official market program. Reported steep markdowns (produce, seafood, meat) roughly 30 minutes before Saturday Farmers' Market closing, as vendors clear stock. Farmers' Market hours confirmed 5am–3pm Saturdays, 92–95 Front St E — the venue itself is real and confirmed via the market's own site; the markdown timing is what shoppers report, not what the market publishes. stlawrencemarket.com Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in, Saturdays only, near closing. Phone: 416-392-7219.

Bulk Barn Sunday 15% off + coupon stacking Company-confirmed on Bulk Barn's own site: "Sustainable Sundays" — 15% off each regular-priced bulk item every Sunday when using clean reusable containers or cloth bags (excludes carry-out shopping bags; container must meet Bulk Barn's standards, tared at checkout). Stacking with a printable coupon is shopper-reported only, not stated by Bulk Barn. bulkbarn.ca Who it's for: anyone, Ontario-wide. Walk in, Sundays. Corporate line: (905) 726-5000.

Farm Boy discount produce/meat boxes Crowd-reported shopper practice, not a formal program described on the retailer's own site. Near-expiry produce, meat and berries bundled into steeply discounted boxes on cart displays near the produce section, restocked through the week — reported roughly $3/vegetable box, $2.50–5/ berry box. Managed at store-manager discretion, not guaranteed daily; shoppers report Tuesday/ Thursday mornings as the best times to check, and named Front & Bathurst and St Clair & Yonge as reliable locations. Farm Boy's own official Customer Care FAQ makes no mention of a near-expiry box program — the crowd-practice framing stands. farmboy.ca Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Walk in — availability varies by store and day. Customer Care: 1-833-788-3401 (Mon–Sat 8am–4:30pm EST).

Odd Bunch Subscription boxes of cosmetically "odd" or surplus fruit and vegetables delivered to the door, priced 30–50% below retail (Small box $28→$20/wk, Medium $40→$32/wk, Large $55→$46/wk as of this review). Pause or cancel any time, no contract. oddbunch.ca Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Not free — a paid subscription, discounted vs. grocery-store prices.

CartIQ — Weekly Toronto Grocery Deal Comparison Free site that prices the same 10-item staple basket at No Frills, Food Basics, FreshCo, Walmart and Metro every week, plus a postal-code "check Toronto deals now" tool and a Thursday deals-alert subscription. As of this review, Food Basics narrowly beats No Frills on regular shelf price ($50.60 vs. $52.63 for the basket); Metro runs 18–28% higher. The site's own suggested strategy: Food Basics or No Frills for weekly staples, plus a monthly Walmart pantry run. cartiq.ca Who it's for: anyone, Toronto. Free to browse.

Checkout 51 Free cash-back app: add weekly offers, shop at any store, photograph your receipt to redeem. Offers refresh Thursdays. checkout51.com Who it's for: anyone, anywhere. Free app.

SmartCanucks Long-running Canadian deals/coupons blog and forum with dedicated grocery-flyer pages (No Frills, Food Basics, FreshCo, Metro, Superstore and more, updated weekly) alongside fast-food coupons and contests. smartcanucks.ca Who it's for: anyone, anywhere. Free to read; forum requires a free account to post.

Canadian Savers Free-samples, coupons and contests aggregator running since 2007, updated daily — grocery-brand samples and coupons alongside a broader household/beauty/fast-food roster. canadiansavers.ca Who it's for: anyone, anywhere. Free; a free account unlocks some entries/points features.

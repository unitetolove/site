Student Survival — OCAD University

Part of the Free Toronto guide fleet — a plain-language set of guides pulled from the Free Toronto Resource Atlas, our full 765-entry directory of free and low-cost Toronto resources. Who this page is for: OCAD University (OCAD U) students. Every link on this page was checked directly against the provider's own page, most recently on 2026-07-28. Neutral civic reference information — not affiliated with any campaign, candidate, or advocacy organization.

Selection rule for this page: an entry qualifies only if it's run by or exclusively for OCAD University students — an OCAD Student Union member service, the university's own registrar or School of Graduate Studies fund, or an OCAD-specific campus program. Nothing here is a general Toronto or general-Ontario-student resource restated for convenience — for those, go to Student Survival (the cross-campus base guide) first, and to whichever topical guide matches your need (Claim Everything for general tax/benefits, Housing for general housing, In Crisis Now for same-night crisis needs, Food Today & Groceries for food beyond a campus program). That's the only pointer you'll see to "resources anyone can use" on this page — everything below this line is OCAD-specific.

OCAD U runs on a single downtown campus (100 McCaul St. and nearby annex buildings) — there's no multi-campus split to navigate here the way there is at U of T, York, Humber or Seneca. What this campus does have that a flat "student resources" list misses: two separate service providers, and a real but thin graduate-student funding layer. The university itself runs the Emergency Bursary Program, the School of Graduate Studies' own grad-only research funding, the Student Wellness Centre, Tool Loans, the Laptop Program (fee-bundled tech), and the Indigenous Student Centre. The OCAD Student Union (OCAD SU) — a separate, member-run organization — runs the food programs, Micro-Grants, and the free lawyer/advocacy service, and administers the health & dental plan alongside the university.

Unlike U of T (five separate unions) or TMU (three separate unions), OCAD does not appear to split undergrad and grad students across separate student organizations. This pass searched directly for a distinct OCAD graduate students' association and found none — OCAD SU's own materials describe one ~5,000-member organization with no undergrad/grad carve-out, and every OCAD SU service below is written into policy as open to "OCAD students" without a grad exclusion. The one place the undergrad/grad line shows up concretely in this pass's research is university-run funding: the School of Graduate Studies runs its own grants that undergrads can't apply to, alongside the Emergency Bursary Program that both groups can use. Every entry below carries a CAMPUS-GATED (OCAD U students/OCAD SU members only) or CAMPUS-HOSTED-BUT-PUBLIC (open to anyone, not OCAD-restricted) label, plus a Group tag (ALL / UNDERGRAD / GRAD) wherever the source data supports one.

I need food this week

OCAD SU Food & Materials Programs Three food-security programs in one place. Hot Lunch — a twice-weekly (Tue/Thu, 2:30pm) mostly vegan and gluten-free meal in Lambert Lounge (100 McCaul St, Room 187), pay-what-you-can with a suggested cash donation or non-perishable item; no student is turned away for lack of funds. Student Pantry — free fresh and non-perishable food, prepared meals, grab-and-go snacks, personal care items, menstrual hygiene products and sexual health supplies, no-assessment basis, plus limited President's Choice gift cards (Mon–Thu 12–4pm); 100 McCaul St Room 271 (2nd floor) and 230 Richmond St W Room 620 (6th floor). Safer Sex Supplies — free, no-assessment basis, at the OCAD SU office (or the Student Wellness Centre when the SU office is closed). ocadsu.org/services/food-materials Group: ALL. CAMPUS-GATED — OCAD SU members (current OCAD students); Student Pantry also explicitly covers anyone who attended OCAD since September 1, 2022. No documents required — stated as a no-assessment basis for the Pantry and Safer Sex Supplies.

I need money — emergency bursaries, grants, and research funding

OCAD students facing financial need have three separate money doors, worth knowing all three exist because they don't overlap: a university-wide emergency bursary open to everyone, a grad-only research/travel funding stream, and a small SU-run event grant that isn't emergency-focused at all.

OCAD University Emergency Bursary Program One of several non-repayable bursary categories the university funds. The university's own bursaries page states bursaries generally range $500–$5,000, with roughly 1,000 students receiving bursary support annually across all categories, and lists "Emergency bursaries" as its own category. Honesty flag: the page doesn't publish an emergency-specific amount, deadline, or document list — apply through the full bursary listing on AwardSpring (ocadu.awardspring.ca), or contact Financial Aid directly. Email financiala@ocadu.ca, or call 416-977-6000. ocadu.ca — Bursaries Group: ALL (undergrad and grad — the page doesn't exclude either). CAMPUS-GATED — OCAD U students. Documents required: unknown — apply via AwardSpring for current requirements.

OCAD University Graduate Studies — Project & Travel Grants The university's own grad-only funding stream, separate from the Emergency Bursary above and not run by OCAD SU: Project and Travel Grants (up to $2,000/year, to "support their practice and research activities") and MRP/Thesis Grants for second-year students ("to support final project research and creation"). Open to registered grad students throughout the year; priority deadline for funding consideration is mid-January. Grad students can also hold Teaching or Research Assistantships (40–150 hours/term, roughly $2,000–$6,000 per appointment) as paid work, separate from these grants. gradadmissions.ocadu.ca — Funding Group: GRAD only. This is discretionary research/practice funding, not an emergency fund — a grad student facing a financial emergency should still use the Emergency Bursary Program above, which doesn't exclude grad students.

OCAD SU Micro-Grants A third, different-purpose fund: up to $250 to "support non-academic events, creative projects, and community initiatives" for OCAD SU student members and student groups — not personal emergency money. Projects must align with the SU's equity and social justice principles; selected applicants complete a 90-minute policy training. Apply online. 2026 cycle: applications due August 21, 2026; projects due August 31, 2026. (The SU's separate Project Grants stream is currently inactive.) ocadsu.org — Funding Group: ALL. CAMPUS-GATED — OCAD SU members and student groups. Dated cycle — confirm current dates on the page if you're reading this after August 2026.

I need cheap or free health, dental, or wellness care

OCAD University Student Wellness Centre Free short-term counselling (single-session appointments, virtual or in-person) to help you navigate a wellness challenge, plus medical appointments with physicians who accept UHIP, OHIP and out-of-province health cards. Also runs a communal "Student Living Room" space. 230 Richmond St. W., 6th and 8th floor. Book counselling online; call 416-977-6000 ext. 260 or email swc@ocadu.ca. ocadu.ca — Student Wellness Centre Group: ALL. CAMPUS-GATED — treated as gated: the page doesn't explicitly restrict to OCAD students, but it's a campus clinic with no stated public-access path. Hours weren't stated on the page as read today — call ahead.

OCAD SU / OCAD U Student Health & Dental Plan (Studentcare/Alumo) Extended health, dental and travel insurance automatically bundled into your student fees if you're registered in at least 1.5 credits per semester — you don't pay for this separately at the point of use, because it's already paid through your fees. Covers prescription-style extended benefits, dental, and travel; international students also hold mandatory UHIP and can add Alumo for supplemental dental/prescription coverage. You can opt in, opt out, or add dependents during the first month of each study period. ocadsu.org — Health and Dental Plan Group: ALL — unlike U of T or TMU, OCAD doesn't run a separate grad-only plan; one plan covers undergrad and grad alike. CAMPUS-GATED — OCAD U students with at least 1.5 credits/semester. Not a discretionary free benefit — bundled into fees you're already paying; opt-out available for domestic students with proof of alternate coverage. Exact premium amount not published on the page read today.

I need free or low-cost legal help

OCAD SU Legal Aid & Advocacy Two free services. Advocacy Services (Manager: Dr. Jacqui Spencer) — confidential advice, advocacy and support for academic misconduct, appeals, policy questions, hearing preparation, mediation, representation, and concerns like bullying, discrimination and harassment (not therapy, not legal advice); book via a Google Form, response within 48 hours excluding weekends/closures. SU Lawyer (Mbuso Nkosi) — free legal consultation on family, civil and immigration matters, including housing, contracts, immigration, criminal matters, employment, family law, and document preparation/review/notarization (not court representation, not student-vs-student disputes); virtual appointments Fridays 9am–12pm (weekly fall/winter, biweekly summer) — email lawyer@ocadsu.org. ocadsu.org — Legal Aid & Advocacy Group: ALL. CAMPUS-GATED — OCAD SU members (current OCAD students) only.

I need a computer, tool, piece of gear, or software

OCAD University Tool Loans (Studios) Loan access to an extensive range of studio tools — hand tools (hammers, prybars, clamps, scrapers, pliers, utility knives, wire cutters, saws, screwdrivers, socket sets, wrenches, Allen keys), power tools (saws, drills, angle grinders, sanders, routers, joiners, heat guns, rotary tools), woodworking tools (hand planes, spoke shaves, files, chisels, gouges, razor saws, mitre boxes), plus drawing/measuring tools, electrical equipment, lighting stands, textiles equipment (sewing machines, sergers), and transport equipment (panel trucks, dolly carts). PPE (masks, respirators, gloves, eye/ear protection) is sold, not loaned. ocadu.ca — Tool Loans Group: ALL (studio-based programs). CAMPUS-GATED (inferred, not stated specifically for this service). Honesty flag: cost and the exact loan-access procedure weren't published on either page checked — general studio-access language elsewhere on the site says access requires "training and demonstrated competence." Call 416-977-6000 to confirm before relying on this.

OCAD University Laptop Program (fee-included tech & software) Not a free loan — a mandatory annual technology fee ($221–$390 for 2025–26, depending on program/year of entry, charged upon fall and winter registration) that bundles: software licensing (50–68% of the fee) covering Adobe Creative Cloud, Microsoft Office, Windows licensing and Lynda.com; support services (30%) covering IT Help Desk staff, loaner laptops, dongles, power adapters and hardware; and the remainder as print credits. This is OCAD's version of a laptop-loan program — access runs through the fee everyone already pays, not a separate discretionary free service, and the page doesn't detail how you actually check out a loaner laptop. ocadu.ca — Laptop Program Fees Group: ALL. CAMPUS-GATED — mandatory fee, amount varies by program/year of entry. Included in fees you already pay — never call this "free." Correction to v0.1's framing: v0.1 said flatly "OCAD U doesn't run a dedicated laptop-loan program" — true for a discretionary free* service, but this fee-funded program does include loaner laptops as part of its Support Services allocation.*

I want career support

OCAD University RBC Centre for Emerging Artists and Designers (CEAD) — Career Advising & Development One-on-one Career Advising sessions (book via the Talent Network platform — complete your profile, pick a slot under "Events & Advising," meet by Microsoft Teams; come with a resume/CV and portfolio if ready) to discuss career pathways, experiential learning placements and how to source opportunities. Also runs Student + Alumni Job Boards, the Design Career Fair, the Art & Design Grad School Fair, Career Launchers (opportunities for senior students/new grads), Design Charrettes with real clients, the Good W/ Food dinner series, Creative Professionals-in-Residence for portfolio reviews and mentorship, and peer support via Student Career Ambassadors. Contact careerdevelopment@ocadu.ca. ocadu.ca — Career Advising Group: ALL, plus recent graduates (up to 5 years post-completion). CAMPUS-GATED — OCAD students and recent alumni. No clothing/attire-lending service found on either CEAD page checked — see Honest gaps below.

I'm an Indigenous student

OCAD University Indigenous Student Centre Academic, cultural, social and emotional wellbeing support for First Nations, Métis and Inuit students at OCAD U: computer workstations, a resource library, archive, student office, and food pantry on-site; weekly events, guest lectures, workshops, visiting artists and field trips centred on Indigenous visual arts and culture. 113 McCaul Street, Room 410. Hours Mon–Fri 9am–5pm (closed weekends/holidays). Contact isc@ocadu.ca or 416-977-6000. ocadu.ca — Indigenous Student Centre Community-specific — this door is for First Nations, Métis and Inuit students at OCAD U. The provider's own page doesn't state whether any programming extends to non-Indigenous OCAD community members, so this is treated as community-specific throughout rather than assumed open.

Honest gaps — things OCAD students will look for that we don't have yet

A dedicated on-campus gym or free fitness/recreation facility. Reconfirmed this pass: no dedicated OCAD U fitness centre page was found (a direct URL guess 404'd, and a follow-up search surfaced no replacement page). OCAD U's own "Fitness and Wellbeing" content, as flagged in v0.1, describes only "intramural" indoor soccer, volleyball and basketball "games scheduled weekly" — real, but with no stated cost, facility location, registration process, or contact info beyond a general campus-life referral. Unlike TMU, Humber, Seneca or U of T's Hart House, OCAD U does not appear to operate its own fitness centre — plausibly a real structural gap for an art-and-design school without a large athletics budget, not a research miss. Not cited as a entry given how little is confirmed; call 416-977-6000 if you're chasing this specifically.

A career closet / free professional-attire lending program. Searched again this pass, including both CEAD career pages (career-advising and career-development) — found none through OCAD U or OCAD SU's own pages. If one exists under a name this search didn't catch, it isn't confirmed and isn't claimed here.

A U-Pass or OCAD-specific TTC transit discount. No evidence found of a Toronto-wide U-Pass program (Toronto never adopted the model most other Canadian cities use) or an OCAD-specific transit subsidy beyond the standard TTC post-secondary concession fare already noted in the base guide's Fair Pass section. A genuine citywide absence, not a research gap specific to OCAD.

Campus-specific childcare or student-parent bursary entries. Searched directly (a parallel check on the George Brown guide found a real, if honesty-flagged, childcare-partnership finding for that campus) — no equivalent OCAD-specific childcare partnership or student-parent bursary surfaced. Still a real gap, not resolved this pass.

A distinct OCAD graduate students' association. Searched directly (OCAD SU's homepage and "about" page) and found no evidence of one — OCAD SU appears to represent undergrad and grad students together as a single ~5,000-member organization. Stated as a structural observation in the intro above, not claimed as a confirmed absence (a body could exist under a name this search didn't catch), but nothing found this pass contradicts it.

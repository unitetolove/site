Student Survival — Humber Polytechnic

Selection rule for this page: an entry qualifies only if it is run by or exclusively for Humber Polytechnic students — including University of Guelph-Humber (UofGH) where IGNITE or a named Humber-institution page explicitly covers both — via a student union/association service, a school-level emergency fund, a registrar/financial-aid office program, or a campus-specific program. Nothing here is a general Toronto or general-Ontario-student resource restated for convenience. For those, go to Student Survival (the cross-campus base guide) first, and to whichever topical guide matches your need (Claim Everything for general tax/benefits, Housing for general housing, In Crisis Now for same-night crisis needs, Food Today & Groceries for food beyond a campus program). That's the only pointer you'll see to "resources anyone can use" on this page — everything below this line is Humber/UofGH-specific.

A one-time honesty note about IGNITE. IGNITE is the single student union/student government that serves both Humber Polytechnic students and University of Guelph-Humber students — formed from the merger of the former Humber Students' Federation and the UofGH Student Association, and its own pages state plainly it represents "over 30,000 full-time students in one common voice." This pass searched directly for a distinct UofGH student government and found none — UofGH's own "Student Government" page confirms IGNITE is the sole representative body. There is no separate graduate students' union at Humber or UofGH. Where a row below doesn't explicitly restate UofGH inclusion, that's flagged honestly rather than assumed either way.

A one-time honesty note about undergrad vs. grad (the biggest v0.1 gap, researched fresh this pass). Three structural facts matter here: 1. Humber Polytechnic now grants real Master's degrees — as of its 2022 polytechnic designation, two programs are live: Master of Engineering in Advanced Manufacturing Engineering and Master of Computer Science – Cybersecurity Operations and Management (confirmed via Humber's own international-programs page). No dedicated grad-student union, grad-specific wellness stream, or grad-specific IGNITE service tier was found for these students — Master's students use the same IGNITE and SWAC doors as everyone else. The one place the undergrad/grad line shows up concretely is bursary tier amount: Humber's own Tuition Bursary pays $500 for a diploma, $750 for an Ontario Graduate Certificate, and $1,000 for a Bachelor's or Master's degree (the latter two grouped together, not split). 2. University of Guelph-Humber (UofGH) is an undergraduate-only institution. UofGH's own in-course bursary page states its aid is "designed to help current undergraduate students complete their degrees" — confirming UofGH itself grants no graduate credentials. A UofGH student is, structurally, always an undergrad. 3. Humber has no separate part-time students' association either. IGNITE's own Emergency Financial Relief Bursary and Health & Dental Plan are explicitly restricted to full-time students — a real access gap for part-time students that this pass did not find a Humber-side fix for. Where an entry below is full-time-only, that's stated plainly.

Every entry below carries a Group tag (ALL / UNDERGRAD / GRAD / student-parent / full-time-only) and states which campus(es) it actually runs at, per the fleet's multi-campus honesty standard — "your student card gets you in" without naming which campus is accurate but incomplete, and this page doesn't do that.

I need food this week

IGNITE Soupbar Free grab-and-go frozen 1L containers of homemade soup, at the IGNITE Student Centre (North Campus) and L Commons (Lakeshore Campus). Honesty flag: closed for the summer as of this check — IGNITE's own page states it relaunches in Fall 2026. ignitestudentlife.com/soupbar Group: ALL. Campuses: North + Lakeshore. Humber students (IGNITE); explicit UofGH inclusion not restated on this specific page. Walk in — but not running again until fall.

FLAS Grab 'n Go Food Bank Free non-perishable food items, take-what-you-need, run by the Faculty of Liberal Arts & Sciences on the 5th floor of the LRC, outside the FLAS office, on scheduled dates through fall/winter terms. Honesty flag: on its summer break as of this check — the provider's own page confirms it returns in the fall but doesn't give an exact date. humber.ca — FLAS Grab 'n Go update Group: ALL. Campus: North Campus only — no Lakeshore or UofGH-side equivalent found. "Members of the Humber community" per the provider's own page; UofGH inclusion not stated. Walk in during posted dates — but not running again until fall.

I need money — union, registrar, and career-fund doors (three separate systems, worth knowing all three)

Humber-area students actually have three separate money systems, run by three separate offices, and they don't automatically know about each other:

IGNITE Emergency Financial Relief Bursary — the union door One $500 bursary per academic year for a full-time Humber or UofGH student who can show financial need through IGNITE's budget calculator (no fixed income line published). Applications stay open until funds run out — no fixed deadline. Humber students apply through MyHumber; UofGH students use a separate Qualtrics form. ignitestudentlife.com — Bursary Group: ALL, full-time-only. Institution-wide, not campus-specific. Apply online.

Humber Financial Need & Tuition Bursaries — the Humber Polytechnic registrar door A separate, ongoing bursary system run by Humber's own Financial Aid office, not IGNITE: Tuition Bursaries scaled by credential level (Diploma $500, Ontario Graduate Certificate $750, Bachelor's or Master's $1,000), with 2026–27 cycle deadlines (Fall 2026: Aug 14; Winter 2027: Dec 11); individually-assessed Financial Need Bursaries via a Financial Aid Advisor; automatic Student Access Bursaries for OSAP recipients with unmet need over $4,500; and a $2,000 Students In/From Care (SIFC) Bursary. Apply through MyHumber > Student Awards and Financial Aid. humber.ca — Scholarships & Bursaries Group: ALL (credential-tiered — this is where the undergrad/grad amount split shows up). Institution-wide. UofGH inclusion not stated — UofGH runs its own separate system below.

University of Guelph-Humber In-Course Bursaries (Financial Need Assessment) — the UofGH registrar door UofGH's own ongoing need-based bursary process for currently-enrolled undergraduates — not entrance-only, and separate from both doors above. Submit a Financial Need Assessment Form to Student Financial Services (deadline October 7 for the fall/winter cycle; a separate process applies for summer-only students and for international undergraduates). Bundled into the same annual cycle as a general need-based "University of Guelph-Humber Bursary," a First Generation Bursary, and Indigenous- and Black-specific Diversity Bursaries. guelphhumber.ca — In-Course Bursaries Group: UNDERGRAD only — UofGH grants no graduate credentials. UofGH-specific; not available to Humber Polytechnic students who aren't also UofGH-enrolled.

Humber Employability Experience Fund — a fourth, narrower door, career-tied not crisis-tied Reimburses up to $2,000 (scaled to hours participated) for taking part in unpaid Hackathons, Skills Competitions, Industry Projects, or Challenges. One application per academic year; apply after completing the activity and submit documentation. Deadlines: Summer Sept 15, Fall Jan 15, Winter May 15. Not emergency money — a reimbursement for career-building activity, run by Advising & Career Services. careers.humber.ca — Employability Experience Fund Group: ALL. Humber students, domestic or international; UofGH inclusion not restated on this specific fund page (unlike the general Career Services entry below, which does confirm it).

IGNITE Free Tax Clinic Simple tax returns prepared free by a trained student volunteer. Honesty flag: the 2026 clinic season is already over as of this check, and IGNITE's own page says the 2027 launch date is "to be determined," with more information "early 2027." ignitestudentlife.com/tax-clinic Group: ALL. Institution-wide. Humber and UofGH students, simple returns only. Season currently closed — watch the page for the 2027 date.

If you need a tax clinic before IGNITE's reopens: the general-population Community Volunteer Income Tax Program (CVITP) clinics listed in Student Survival don't require you to be a student at all.

I need free or low-cost health, dental, or counselling care

Humber & Guelph-Humber SWAC Health Services Free, confidential primary care: a doctor, a Registered Dietitian, a psychiatrist, birth-control/STI counselling, and pre-placement immunizations. Doctors are available five days a week in fall/winter terms per UofGH's own page; virtual appointments run first-come, first-served. humber.ca/student-life/swac/health-services Group: ALL. Institution-wide (North + Lakeshore, not broken out by campus on the page read). Registered Humber and UofGH students — shared access independently confirmed on UofGH's own page. Book online.

Humber & Guelph-Humber SWAC Counselling Services Free, confidential one-on-one counselling for stress, relationship issues, or personal difficulties — email counselling@humber.ca to start. Two more free supports on the same page: Good2Talk (24/7, 1-866-925-5454) and TAO (Therapy Assist Online, self-guided digital tools). Honesty flag: Humber's own dedicated mental-health-services page returned an access error to this check three times across two build passes now — the "free and confidential" fact is independently confirmed via UofGH's own wellness page instead; counselling-specific wait times aren't stated anywhere reachable today. guelphhumber.ca — Wellness Group: ALL. Institution-wide. Registered Humber and UofGH students. Email to book; centre operates by appointment, 8:30am–4:30pm, per the general SWAC hub page.

IGNITE Health & Dental Insurance Plan — not free, but you're already paying for it Dental, prescription drugs, vision, extended health and travel coverage, billed automatically into fees ($116.65/semester for full-time Humber students; full-time UofGH students billed automatically, part-time UofGH students opt in). Use your Student Benefit Card; claims run through ClaimSecure's eProfile. You can only opt out in your first semester, and only if you already have another qualifying plan — miss that window and you're locked in for the year. ignitestudentlife.com — Health & Dental Plan Group: ALL, full-time-billed (UofGH part-time students opt in). Institution-wide. Not free — mandatory fee, refundable only via a first-semester opt-out.

IGNITE Sleep Lounge A rest space, walk in with student ID, no appointment, Monday–Friday 9am–4pm per IGNITE's own page. Honesty flag: closed for the summer as of this check, relaunching Fall 2026; exact campus location and whether it's free aren't stated on the reachable page. ignitestudentlife.com/sleep-lounge Group: ALL. Campus: not confirmed which campus(es). Humber students (IGNITE); UofGH inclusion not confirmed. Walk in — but not running again until fall.

For dental clinics, Good2Talk, and other supervised-student clinics open to the public (not Humber-specific but usable by any Humber student), see the "I need cheap or free health, dental, or wellness care" section of Student Survival.

I want career support

Humber Advising & Career Services Career Support Peer Services (resume, cover letter and LinkedIn profile reviews, mock interviews), one-on-one Career & Student Success Advising, a Career Coaching Service, and the Career Connect Portal (jobs, work-integrated learning, on-campus positions). careers.humber.ca Group: ALL. Institution-wide, not broken out by campus. The provider's own page explicitly names Humber, University of Guelph-Humber, and international students as covered — one of the few Humber-institution pages this pass found that confirms UofGH inclusion in its own text.

Humber Employability Experience Fund — see the "I need money" section above; it's a career fund, but it's cash, so it's cited there rather than duplicated here.

No career closet or professional-clothing-lending program found. Searched again this pass via both Career Services pages — see Honest Gaps below.

I need a laptop, gear, software, or a place to make things

Humber ITS Laptop Loans Free short-term loans (up to 4 hours, no charger) or full-semester loans for enrolled Humber students. Reserve online (Web Checkout → Tech Zone → Student-Laptops); short-term pickup at North Campus (3rd floor LRC) or Lakeshore Campus (L Building, 1st-floor printer area). Bring a current student card or valid photo ID. Late fees run $10/hour, capped at $50/day, and you carry full financial liability for loss, theft, or damage. humber.ca/its — Laptop Loans Group: ALL. Campuses: North + Lakeshore. Humber students verified enrolled this semester; UofGH inclusion not stated on this page. Reserve online, pick up in person.

Humber Free & Discounted Student Software (incl. Microsoft Office) Humber ITS's student-software catalogue lists Microsoft Office — "Free - Academic Program" for Humber students, alongside a broader catalogue of free and discounted titles (AutoCAD suite, Power BI, DaVinci Resolve and more), via the myapps.humber.ca and humber.onthehub.com portals. humber.ca/its — student software Group: ALL. Institution-wide. Humber students confirmed; UofGH inclusion not stated on this page. (2026-08-04 link-rot correction: its.humber.ca/computers is gone — this successor catalogue page, fetched today, confirms free Microsoft Office but does not state the old page's "1TB OneDrive" figure, so that figure is no longer claimed here.)

Humber & Guelph-Humber Library Idea Lab Free-to-use makerspace: 3D printers, laser/vinyl cutters, sewing/embroidery machines, heat presses, and audio/video/photography studios, plus camera and audio-recorder kit checkout (2-day loan, $10/day overdue fine — not a usage fee). North Campus, Lakeshore Campus, and Humber Downtown locations; book space via LibCal, priority to students working on assignments. library.humber.ca/idealab Group: ALL. Campuses: North + Lakeshore + Downtown. Current Humber and UofGH students (also faculty/staff). Book online, use in person. Contact idealab@humber.ca or 647-577-6432.

I'm a student-parent

University of Guelph-Humber Parent Bursary A $1,500 need-based bursary for UofGH students who are parents, part of the same Fall-semester In-Course Bursary cycle described above (Financial Need Assessment Form, October 7 deadline). Amount and existence confirmed on UofGH's own page; detailed eligibility criteria (income threshold, number/age of children) aren't itemized there. guelphhumber.ca — Awards & Bursaries Group: student-parent, UNDERGRAD (UofGH only). Not available to Humber Polytechnic students who aren't also UofGH-enrolled — Humber Polytechnic itself has no parallel found (see Honest Gaps).

A note on the Humber Child Development Centre, so you don't waste a trip: it's on Humber's North Campus and it does take children, but it is not a Humber-student benefit — it's a general community childcare centre at the standard Canada-Wide Early Learning & Child Care ($22/day) rate, open to "students and faculty of Humber... [and] our community neighbours" with no stated Humber-student priority or discount, and a waitlist that can run six months or more for subsidized spots. If you need childcare as a Humber student-parent, this is a real option but not a special one — treat it the same as any other CWELCC-rate centre in the city.

I want free or included fitness and recreation

Humber Fitness Centre (SWAC) Free gym and fitness-class access via the Avro Mobile app (set-up needs a photo, a signed waiver, and an emergency contact) — the provider's own page states plainly: "Free access available" for Humber Students, Staff & Faculty, vs. "Paid membership required" for Community & Alumni. North Campus and Lakeshore Campus locations; Lakeshore memberships are currently not accepting new registrations per the same page. Personal training and nutrition consultations cost extra. A standalone Community/Alumni annual membership at the same facility runs $339/year — that's the free value a student is getting. humber.ca/student-life/swac/fitness Group: ALL. Campuses: North + Lakeshore (Lakeshore currently closed to new registrations). Humber students, staff, and faculty confirmed free; a linked access-requirements page also references "Humber or Guelph-Humber official" ID checks, suggesting UofGH students are covered too, though the page's own "free access" line names Humber only. ONECard may be checked at any time.

Honest gaps — things Humber and UofGH students will look for that this atlas can't confirm today

A career closet / free professional clothing program. Searched again this pass across both Career Services pages (student-services and employability-experience-fund) — found none. Humber's Fashion Arts & Business program ran a one-time discounted "Eco Closet" pop-up (clothing $5–$12, not free) in November 2022, but nothing found today confirms an ongoing, currently-running version. If you need professional clothing for an interview, contact Career Services directly to check for a current-year equivalent before counting on it.

A discounted or free transit pass baked into fees, or a free inter-campus shuttle. Confirmed again this pass: Humber does not run a mandatory U-Pass (a 2018 student-newspaper report says this was considered and rejected; nothing found since shows it adopted), and UofGH's own commuting page lists only paid options — a discounted TTC pass purchased at the Humber Bookstore, and a GO Transit Student ID that still requires purchasing fare. No free shuttle between North and Lakeshore campuses was confirmed (the shuttle-bus page itself returned an access error to this check). Your best options remain the general-population ones in Student Survival: the income-tested Fair Pass discount, or the standard TTC post-secondary concession fare.

A Humber Polytechnic-specific (not UofGH) student-parent bursary or childcare subsidy. This pass found a real UofGH-specific one (FT-15517, above) — a genuine improvement on v0.1, which found nothing. Humber Polytechnic itself still has no equivalent found, and the on-campus Child Development Centre is general community childcare, not a student benefit (see the note under "I'm a student-parent," above).

A distinct graduate students' association or grad-specific wellness/union tier. Searched directly this pass — none found. Humber's two Master's programs and UofGH's (nonexistent) graduate tier both route through the same IGNITE/SWAC doors as undergraduates; see the intro's undergrad/grad honesty note for the one place a real split does show up (bursary tier amount).

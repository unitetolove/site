Student Survival — Seneca Polytechnic

Selection rule for this page: an entry qualifies only if it's run by or exclusively for Seneca Polytechnic students — a Seneca Student Federation (SSF)-run service, a Seneca-run institutional program (Financial Aid, IT Services, First Peoples @ Seneca), or a Seneca-specific insurance plan gated by student status. Nothing here requires you to already know which office to ask, but everything here does require you to be a Seneca student (or, for the health/dental and health-insurance rows, to already be paying the charge that funds one).

For everything not specific to Seneca — general-population food banks, tax-filing clinics open to any income-qualifying individual, Good2Talk, public legal clinics, discounts, free learning platforms, transit programs, and the honest gaps in student-parent and general childcare coverage — see the cross-campus base guide, Student Survival. For same-night crisis needs (shelter, food, crisis lines), see In Crisis Now. For food-specific resources beyond a campus food bank, see Food Today & Groceries, including anything near Newnham (Finch/404) or Seneca@York (Steeles/Keele) specifically. This page doesn't repeat any of that content — it points to it once, here, and stays focused on what's actually Seneca-specific below.

Which campus: Seneca Polytechnic's own current campus list (confirmed 2026-07-28) is Newnham, King, Seneca@York, Markham (Seneca International Academy), Peterborough (aviation, years 2–4 only), and Online. Every entry below states which campus(es) it actually operates at — a resource being "Seneca-run" doesn't mean it's available at every campus, and several below (the shuttle, ITS loaners, Career Threads) explicitly are not.

Seneca splits by full-time/part-time, and by domestic/international — not by undergrad/grad. Unlike York, UofT or TMU, Seneca does not (yet) have a separate graduate-students' union, a graduate-specific health plan, or a graduate-specific emergency fund: Seneca's first-ever master's degree (an AI program) only launched in Fall 2025, and the SSF's own Student Development Grant policy explicitly folds "graduate certificate" students into the same full-time-day-student eligibility bucket as diploma and degree students — no separate lower tier was found anywhere in this review's research. The real split that matters at Seneca is full-time vs. part-time (several entries below are full-time-only) and domestic vs. international (health coverage runs through two entirely different, non-interchangeable plans — see "I need health, dental, or mental-health support beyond OHIP" below). Every entry states which of those groups it's for.

Most SSF-run entries below are already funded by the small mandatory-unless-opted-out SSF membership fee every Seneca student pays each term (roughly $36.50–$40, depending on campus) — in SSF's own framing, these are "included in fees you already pay," not free gifts on top of your tuition.

I need food this week

Seneca Student Federation (SSF) Food Support Program Monthly food assistance for full-time Seneca students facing food insecurity: either an Odd Bunch box of fresh fruit and vegetables (valued roughly $40–$50) delivered to your home address, or a grocery gift card. One registration per month, up to 400 student spots per month, issued within 2–3 weeks of registering. Online registration only — walk-ins are not accommodated. Office: Newnham campus, Room E1402. Phone 437-312-0401. ssfinc.ca/food-bank For: full-time Seneca students, any campus (delivery-based/online registration; administered from Newnham). No walk-in option. Students in Mississauga/Peel can also use Sai Dham Food Bank per the provider's own page.

Seneca Soup Program One free bowl of soup per visit, any time during cafeteria hours, at King, Newnham, and Seneca@York dining halls — halal, vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options offered daily. Add "Seneca Soup Program" under Meal Plan on the OneCard site first (no charge applied), then just present your Seneca OneCard at the register — no need to say anything. Seneca Soup Program (Dining Services) For: full- or part-time Seneca students, King/Newnham/Seneca@York cafeterias only — no Markham/Peterborough/Online equivalent found. No documents required beyond your OneCard.

I need money — emergency help, taxes, tuition, or professional-development funding

Seneca Institution-Funded Special Bursary Non-repayable bursary up to $2,500 for full-time or part-time Seneca students with low family income. Can't be combined with an active OSAP loan in the same period. Apply by downloading the PDF application, completing every field, attaching all required supporting documents, and emailing it to theservicehub@senecapolytechnic.ca from your Seneca email before the published deadline. Seneca Institution-Funded Special Bursary For: full-time or part-time Seneca students, any campus. Honesty flag: the provider's own page doesn't state which specific hardship situations qualify (only "low family income") or publish a deadline — call The Service Hub to confirm the current application window before assuming it's open. $2,500 is a stated maximum, not a guaranteed amount.

SSF Tax Clinic Free income tax return preparation for Seneca students with simple tax situations, through the CRA's Community Volunteer Income Tax Program. March–April tax season, Monday–Friday 9am–3pm, by appointment only, currently Zoom-only — no in-person appointments. Book via the Square booking link or email the SSF. ssfinc.ca/tax-clinic For: Seneca students, any campus (remote/Zoom only), simple tax situations only. Seasonal — closed outside tax season.

SSF Student Development Grant Funding toward learning-development opportunities outside the classroom — career, industry or association conferences, seminars, workshops and presentations. No maximum amount is published; grants over $50 require a testimonial afterward, and priority goes to first-time applicants. Apply through the online Student Application Form at least 4 weeks before the event; cheques are issued from the Newnham SSF office. ssfinc.ca/student-development-grant For: full-time day students in any approved credential level — including graduate-certificate students — any campus. Honesty flag: the linked policy document is dated 2019; call SSF to confirm current terms rather than assuming an old rule still applies verbatim. No dollar cap published.

I need health, dental, or mental-health support beyond OHIP

Seneca's health coverage runs through two entirely separate plans, not one plan with a grad/ undergrad split — which one applies to you depends on whether you're a domestic or international student, and they are not interchangeable.

Seneca Polytechnic Counselling Services Free, confidential personal counselling for any currently-enrolled Seneca student, run directly by the institution. Participation is voluntary and consent-based. Counselling Services (Seneca Students portal) For: Seneca students, any campus, currently enrolled. Honesty flag, reconfirmed 2026-07-28 on a second independent fetch attempt: the provider's own page confirms counselling is "free of charge for enrolled students" but still does not state a booking method, hours, or direct contact info — call The Service Hub if you need to book and can't find the booking page yourself.

SSF Health and Dental Plan (domestic students) Extended health, dental, vision, prescription-drug, and accidental-injury coverage, automatic for full-time domestic Seneca students who've paid the premium. Includes a 24/7 mental-health support line and short-term therapy/lifestyle counselling through WeConnect: 1-855-853-0565. Not free — 2025–26 premiums (taxes included) run $135.84 (Summer 2025) to $277.37 (Fall 2025, covering through Aug 2026); Winter 2026 is $253.11, Summer 2026 is $138.70. You can opt out (with proof of other private insurance) at wespeakstudent.com before the per-term deadline — opting out is irreversible for the policy year. ssfinc.ca/healthplan For: full-time domestic Seneca students only, any campus (institution-wide plan). Not free — mandatory premium unless opted out with proof of other coverage.

International Student Health Insurance (international students) Mandatory hospital, physician, diagnostic lab/x-ray, eye exam, emergency, and medically-necessary- in-hospital dental coverage for international students in a full-time professional program, administered by Morcare and charged automatically with tuition. No opt-out while studying at Seneca, except students who get OHIP or IFHP coverage within 30 days of enrollment (credited down to the domestic rate, $138.70). 2025–26 premiums (taxes included): $823.60 Fall term, $621.63 Winter term, $411.96 Summer term. Refund questions: theservicehub@senecapolytechnic.ca. Health Insurance — International Students For: international Seneca students only, any campus (institution-wide plan). Not free, and not interchangeable with the domestic SSF plan above — this is the international-specific door, run by a different administrator with different opt-out rules.

This is Seneca's own campus counselling and insurance coverage — separate from Good2Talk (1-866-925-5454 / text GOOD2TALKON to 686868), the free 24/7 phone/text helpline open to any Ontario post-secondary student regardless of school, already covered in the base guide.

I need free or low-cost legal help

SSF Legal Aid Free 15-minute legal consultations for full-time Seneca students — notarization of documents, affidavits, landlord/tenant rights, and more. Lawyers will not represent you in ongoing litigation or court proceedings — this is advice, not representation. Provided by lawyers from Himelfarb Proszanski Barristers and Solicitors. Appointments every Wednesday, 9am–4pm, in person at Newnham campus (Building E, Room E1419) or via Zoom. Book through the link on the SSF site. ssfinc.ca/legal-aid For: full-time Seneca students only. Newnham campus in person, or Zoom (any campus remotely). Part-time-student eligibility isn't stated on the provider's page — don't assume you're covered if you're part-time; call to confirm.

I need a laptop, camera, or other tech gear

Seneca ITS Loaner Services Free short-term loan of a laptop, camera, tripod, or HDMI cable from ITS Service Counters — King (55 laptops), Newnham (163 laptops), and Seneca@York (148 laptops) campuses. First-come, first-served, in person only — no phone/email booking, no advance reservation. One of each item type per borrower; 3-day maximum loan, returned by 4pm on weekdays, with a one-day rest period before re-borrowing the same item type. No deposit, but late fees apply ($20/day laptop, $5/day camera or HDMI cable, capped at $1,000 total), and you're financially liable for loss or damage up to $1,000. Equipment can't leave Ontario. Loaner Services (Seneca IT Services) For: actively-enrolled Seneca students, King/Newnham/Seneca@York only — no Markham/Peterborough counter counts were found this review. Bring your Seneca OneCard with photo, or one piece of government-issued ID.

I need free software or cloud storage

Seneca Free Microsoft Office 365 Office 365 Pro Plus (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneNote and more) at no cost, installable on up to 5 personal or work devices (Windows and Mac). Sign in at portal.office.com/account with your Seneca email credentials and select Install under Office apps & devices. Office 365 (Seneca Students portal) For: all active Seneca students, any campus (online licence portal, not location-specific).

Seneca Free Adobe Creative Cloud & Adobe Express Adobe Express Premium free for all full-time Seneca students. Adobe Creative Cloud (Acrobat, Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and more, plus cloud storage) free only for students in specific programs that license it. Sign in at creativecloud.adobe.com/apps with your Seneca email. Adobe (Seneca Students portal) For: all full-time Seneca students get Adobe Express Premium; full Creative Cloud is program-specific — the provider's own page doesn't name which programs, so don't assume you have Photoshop/Premiere access without checking your own program's software list.

I need business or interview clothing

Career Threads (Seneca Works) Free professional clothing to keep — suits, blouses, dress shirts, trousers, skirts, and accessories — for Seneca students and alumni preparing for interviews, networking events, or other career activities. Walk in and browse; no appointment required per the provider's page. Currently at "The Boutique," Room B2024, Newnham campus, Monday–Friday 10am–4pm (a temporary space since May 25, 2026 due to construction, expected through Aug 24, 2026 — confirm the room hasn't moved again if you're reading this after that date). Career Threads (Seneca Works) For: Seneca students and alumni. Newnham campus only — no King or Seneca@York location found this review.

I'm a First Nations, Métis, or Inuit student

First Peoples @ Seneca (Indigenous Student Services) Academic, personal and career counselling with cultural and traditional sensitivity, drop-in counselling and peer tutoring through the Indigenous Student Services Peer Support Program, help completing band-funding/sponsorship applications, Elder support and traditional teachings, a dedicated computer lab, and study/social space. The office also recommends candidates for a slate of Indigenous-specific bursaries and awards — First Nations Métis Inuit Seneca Student Bursary, Seneca Indigenous Student Bursary, Manulife Bursary for Indigenous Students, Peggy Pitawanakwat Indigenous Endowed Bursary, Bruce Berman Alumni Bursary, and more (dollar amounts not published). Primary office: Room E1401, Newnham campus, Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm (extended hours fall/winter). Indigenous Counsellor: Karen White — 416-764-0337 — karen.white@senecapolytechnic.ca. First Peoples @ Seneca — Services and Support For: First Nations, Métis or Inuit Seneca students (self-identified). Primary office at Newnham, but staff maintain visiting schedules across all six Seneca campuses per the provider's own page — community-specific, not campus-gated to Newnham alone.

Getting between campuses

SSF Shuttle Bus Service Free shuttle bus connecting Newnham, King, and Seneca@York campuses on multiple lettered routes (A–G), roughly 7am–8pm on weekdays during term. Summer 2026 service: May 4–Aug 14, 2026, weekdays only — closed Victoria Day, Study Week (Jun 22–26), Canada Day, and the Civic Holiday. Stops: Newnham (TTC loop outside Building C), York (between the Stephen E. Quinlan and Victor Phillip Dahdaleh buildings), King (outside Magna Hall main entrance). Present your Seneca OneCard to ride. ssfinc.ca/shuttlebus For: Seneca students, faculty, and staff only; no guests. Newnham/King/Seneca@York only — no Markham/Peterborough stop found. Fall/winter schedule will differ from the Summer 2026 dates above — reconfirm each term.

Honest gaps — what this lane looked for and didn't find (or found, but couldn't publish)

  • Free fitness/recreation — researched twice, still not confirmed to a current-year fee. Seneca Athletics & Recreation exists at King, Markham, Newnham, and Seneca@York, funded through a mandatory per-term ancillary fee. v0.1 found a page carrying a stale "2019/2020" label; this review found a different fee-description page, dated December 2021 and filed under the 2021/2022 academic year, showing $0 across all campuses — an even less reliable source than v0.1's. Per LINK LAW/verified-only-publish, still no fee figure or row published. It is real, it is not free, and it's bundled into your ancillary fees if you're a Seneca student — call the Office of the Registrar or Athletics & Recreation directly for the current-year number.
  • No campus-specific childcare or student-parent bursary row found for Seneca, the same gap the base guide flags for UofT/TMU/York. See the base guide's "I'm a student parent" section and the Single Parents guide for general-population supports.
  • No separate emergency-loan program or faculty/school-level emergency fund found beyond the Special Bursary. Two institutional pages (Financial Supports & Resources; the Awards, Scholarships & Bursaries hub) were checked; neither named an emergency-loan product or a faculty-specific emergency fund the way York's LA&PS/FGS bursaries work. The Special Bursary (FT-15603) currently appears to be Seneca's one institution-run emergency-hardship door.
  • No separate graduate-student union, graduate health plan, or graduate emergency fund exists at Seneca — confirmed this review, not assumed. Seneca's first-ever master's degree only launched Fall 2025; graduate-certificate students share the same SSF membership and financial-aid infrastructure as everyone else. The real distinctions that matter here are full-time vs. part-time, and domestic vs. international.
  • No Seneca-hosted-but-public service found. Every Seneca/SSF resource this lane verified is restricted to Seneca students (or students + alumni, for Career Threads). Nothing Seneca-run turned up this review that a non-Seneca Torontonian could walk into.

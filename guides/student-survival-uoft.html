Student Survival — University of Toronto

This page is only for University of Toronto students, and only for what's genuinely University-of-Toronto-specific: your students' union's own programs, your registrar's own emergency funds, your campus's own clinic, food bank, gym, or store. Nothing here is a general Toronto or general-Ontario-student resource restated for convenience — for those, go to Student Survival (the cross-campus base guide) first, and to whichever topical guide matches your need (e.g. Claim Everything for general tax/benefits, Housing for general housing). That's the only pointer you'll see to "resources anyone can use" on this page — everything below this line is UofT-specific.

University of Toronto has three physical campuses (St. George, UTSC/Scarborough, UTM/Mississauga) and five separate students'-group organizations that each run their own emergency fund, food bank, and/or health plan: UTSU (full-time undergrads, St. George), UTMSU (full-time undergrads, UTM), SCSU (undergrads, UTSC), APUS (part-time undergrads, tri-campus), and UTGSU (graduate students, tri-campus). They are not interchangeable — a UTSU-run bursary will not pay out to a UTSC student, and an undergraduate health plan will not cover a graduate student. Every entry below is labeled with which student group it serves and which campus(es) it actually operates at.

Labels used throughout: - Group: ALL (any UofT student) · UNDERGRAD · GRAD · PART-TIME (APUS) - CAMPUS-GATED — you must be enrolled at the named campus/union; staff will check. - CAMPUS-HOSTED-BUT-PUBLIC — U of T-run or -branded, open tri-campus or not gated to one union.

I need money right now — bursaries, grants, and loans

Two different systems exist side by side: your students' union's own emergency fund, and the university's own (registrar / School of Graduate Studies) emergency grants and loans. Where both exist for your group, apply to whichever is faster or check both — they are not mutually exclusive on this page, though some programs require you to have applied to one before the other (noted below).

UTGSU Emergency Grant Up to $1,000 total across your UTGSU membership for graduate students facing serious financial need from a specific emergency (medical crisis, family illness/death, natural disaster, sudden job loss causing food insecurity within two weeks, gender-based/IPV situations). You must first have exhausted or been found ineligible for SGS's own emergency funding (below) before UTGSU will pay out. Apply online via HelloRubric within six weeks of the emergency; decision within three business days. utgsu.ca — Emergency Grant Group: GRAD. CAMPUS-GATED but tri-campus — UTGSU represents grad students at St. George, UTM, UTSC and UTIAS (Aerospace); School of Theology students excluded. Rolling applications.

SGS Emergency Grant The university's own (not union-run) one-time non-repayable emergency grant for Doctoral, Master's, or Professional Master's students hit by an unexpected financial crisis. Requires full-time registration in an OSAP-eligible program and, for domestic students, having applied for OSAP or equivalent first. Start by contacting your Faculty Registrar's Office or Graduate Unit, then the SGS Financial Advising Team for a Financial Need Assessment. Not available August 1 – October 30 each year. Decision within 2–3 weeks by U of T email. 416-978-2839, sgs.financial.assistance@utoronto.ca. sgs.utoronto.ca — Emergency Grant Group: GRAD, tri-campus (OISE students apply via OISE Student Services instead). Grant amount not published on the provider's page. This is the fund UTGSU's Emergency Grant (above) expects you to try first.

SGS Emergency Loan A different tool from the grant above: an interest-free bridge loan (typically $1,000–$1,500, sometimes more) for grad students who have funds already coming to them — OSAP, an award installment, TA/RA pay — within 30–120 days, but need cash now. Apply by 2pm for a decision within two business days; funds via ACORN within 48 hours. Must be repaid within about four months, no later than August 31 — an unpaid balance blocks fall re-registration. 416-978-2839, sgs.financial.assistance@utoronto.ca. sgs.utoronto.ca — Emergency Loan Group: GRAD, tri-campus, domestic and international. Repayable, not a grant — confirm you can meet the repayment date before taking it.

UTSU Student Aid Program (SAP) Bursaries up to $500 per application for UTSU-fee-paying undergraduates, across nine categories (books, exam-deferral fees, health/wellness, accessibility, transit, emergencies, university fees, and more). Apply via a Google Form with category-specific documents. Honesty flag: no application window is currently open as of this fetch — the provider's own page reads "Check back later for application dates for the Fall 2026 term." utsu.ca/student-aid Group: UNDERGRAD. CAMPUS-GATED: UTSU-fee-paying undergraduates, St. George only. Window-dependent — currently closed.

U of T Grant (Registrar's Office) The university-wide (not union-run) need-based emergency grant for undergraduates at any of the three campuses, once other funding is exhausted. Apply via the Undergraduate Grant Application Portal; faculty/college decision in 4–6 weeks; funds land on ACORN. Summer 2026 window: May 4 – August 7, 2026 — open now as of this fetch. Fall–Winter 2026-27 window opens September 7 or October 5 (varies by faculty), closes March 26, 2027. registrar.utoronto.ca — U of T Grants Group: UNDERGRAD, tri-campus, domestic and international. Grant amount not published on the provider's page. Grad students use SGS's Emergency Grant/Loan above instead.

UTM Grant (UTM's own process) UTM administers its own version of the U of T Grant with a UTM-specific two-step process: a UTAPS application via Need Navigator, then the online undergraduate grant application selecting UTM as your division. Fall window Sept 8 – Oct 30, 2026; Winter window Jan 6 – Feb 26, 2027; no summer window. International students cannot use this specific grant — they're routed instead to a one-time emergency-support consultation with a Financial Aid Advisor. utm.utoronto.ca/registrar/finances/grants Group: UNDERGRAD. CAMPUS-GATED: UTM, full-time domestic students for the grant itself; international students get a different, advisor-based path. Same underlying U of T Grant program as above, run through UTM's own portal.

UTSC Emergency Aid A different, narrower tool than the grant programs above: short-notice cash specifically for food and shelter costs, for any UTSC student in unexpected financial difficulty. Apply by speaking with a Financial Aid Advisor in the UTSC Office of the Registrar; approved funds arrive in 3–4 business days. utsc.utoronto.ca/registrar/emergency-aid Group: ALL (undergrad and grad UTSC students). CAMPUS-GATED: UTSC only. Amounts, detailed eligibility, and deadlines aren't published on the provider's page — confirm with an advisor.

Honest gap — APUS (part-time undergraduates): APUS's own Bursaries program page (apus.ca/services/bursaries/) is real but was found stale on today's fetch — its most recent content is the Winter 2024 cycle (closed, dated March 2024), with no update since. Rather than cite a 2+-year-old cycle as if it were current, this guide does not list it as an active bursary. Part-time undergrads facing a financial emergency should use the U of T Grant above (open to all three campuses regardless of union) or contact APUS directly to ask whether the bursary program has since resumed.

I need food this week

The UTSU Food Bank (St. George, appointment-based, Fridays) is already in the base guide's food section. Group: ALL — the provider's own page states the Food Bank is "a free, year-round Food Bank open to all University of Toronto students in need of food," not restricted to undergraduates; grad-student eligibility is confirmed, not merely "not stated." CAMPUS-GATED: St. George only.

UTSC and UTM each run their own separate campus food bank:

SCSU Food Centre (UTSC) Free food access — grocery pick-ups, hygiene program, clothing events, cooking workshops — for UTSC students, staff and faculty. Pick-up sign-up Fridays and Mondays in Fall/Winter (Mondays only in Summer), one pick-up per cycle; emergency appointments available outside the cycle. Room SL 210-B, Student Centre. scsu.ca/equity-centres Group: ALL UTSC students (undergrad and grad; also staff/faculty). CAMPUS-GATED: UTSC only.

UTMSU Food Centre (UTM) Free food for any UTM student facing food insecurity — unlimited non-perishable staples, monthly perishable groceries, plus toys, clothes, home necessities and toiletries. Registration under 5 minutes, no income/financial checks. Drop-in "Confidential Hours" or discreet online ordering. Room 2102, Davis Building. utmsu.ca/service/food-centre Group: ALL UTM students. CAMPUS-GATED: UTM only.

Honest gap: no separately-confirmed graduate-specific food bank at St. George was found this review — UTGSU does not appear (per public information found) to run one independently of UTSU's.

I need free or low-cost health, counselling, or dental care

The base guide already covers Good2Talk, the U of T Faculty of Dentistry's reduced-fee clinic, and the OISE Psychology Clinic — open to the public, not UofT-restricted, fully written up there.

Campus medical & counselling clinics — three separate offices

University of Toronto Health & Wellness Centre (St. George) Free medical and mental-health services for current UofT undergrad and grad students residing in Ontario: same-day Medical Clinic, periodic health visits, sexual/reproductive health, some free immunizations, nutrition consults, same-day counselling, peer support, and 24-hour phone/chat via Telus Health Student Support. 700 Bay St, 416-978-8030. studentlife.utoronto.ca/department/health-wellness Group: ALL. CAMPUS-HOSTED-BUT-PUBLIC within the UofT community — clinic is physically St. George.

UTSC Health & Wellness Centre Free medical (doctors/nurses, sexual health, STI testing, concussion support) and mental-health services (counselling, workshops, group therapy) for UTSC students. Sam Ibrahim Building, 5th Floor, 416-287-7065. Bring TCard + health-coverage card. utsc.utoronto.ca/hwc Group: ALL UTSC students. CAMPUS-GATED: UTSC only.

UTM Health & Counselling Centre Free 24/7 counselling for UTM students via the Telus Health Student Support app/phone line, plus a physical office, Room 1152, Davis Building. 1-844-451-9700. utm.utoronto.ca/health Group: ALL UTM students. CAMPUS-GATED in practice — in-person booking/hours not itemized on the page fetched.

Student-group health & dental insurance plans — five different plans, not interchangeable

This is the biggest gap in the prior draft of this page: which health/dental plan covers you depends entirely on your student group, not just your campus, and the coverage numbers genuinely differ. All five are compulsory fees with a limited annual opt-out window if you have comparable coverage elsewhere.

UTSU Health & Dental Plan (undergrad, St. George) Mental-health practitioners to $100/visit (15/yr); Rx to 80% ($5,000/yr); massage/acupuncture to $30/visit (20 combined/yr); vaccinations 100% to $200/yr; travel to 120 days/trip, $5M lifetime; dental preventative to $800/yr, exams/X-rays 70%, fillings/root canals 60%; plus Empower Me 24/7 counselling and the Dialogue virtual-care platform. Opt-out (fall-term students): Sept 4–30, with proof of comparable coverage. utsu.ca/health-and-dental Group: UNDERGRAD. CAMPUS-GATED: St. George (+ eligible Toronto School of Theology students).

UTMSU Health & Dental Plan (undergrad, UTM) Rx 90%; $125/visit to 25 mental-health appointments/yr; dental $800/yr; vision $200/yr; travel to $5M on a 120-day trip. 2025-26 cost: $488.60/yr ($271.76 health + $216.84 dental) on ACORN. Opt-out only Sept 1–30 (fall/winter) or Jan 1–30 (winter-only) — no exceptions outside the window. utmsu.ca/service/health-dental-plan Group: UNDERGRAD. CAMPUS-GATED: UTM only.

SCSU Health & Dental Plan (undergrad, UTSC) Rx, dental, and eye exams beyond OHIP, plus dependent coverage. Fall coverage Sept 1–Dec 31; Winter coverage Jan 1–Aug 31 (no automatic Summer coverage unless enrolled the prior Winter) — a different coverage-window structure than UTSU's or UTMSU's. Requires 3+ full courses/semester. Opt-out Sept 1–30 or Jan 1–31. scsu.ca/health-dental Group: UNDERGRAD. CAMPUS-GATED: UTSC only, 3+ full courses/semester. Premium amount not published on the page fetched.

APUS Health & Dental Plan (part-time undergrad, tri-campus) Overall health max $10,000/person/yr (excl. drugs, gender affirmation); Rx 40% co-pay to $2,000/yr; physio/counselling/acupuncture $30/visit to 20/yr; mental-health counselling to 15 visits/yr at $125/visit; gender-affirmation coverage to $10,000 lifetime; dental $500/yr max at 30% co-pay; plus a Student Assistance Program (5 hrs counselling) and MindBeacon/Tranquility digital CBT. greenshield.ca — APUS plan Group: PART-TIME undergrad, tri-campus. Premium amount not published on the page fetched.

UTGSU Health & Dental Plan (grad, tri-campus) Prescription drugs, health practitioners, medical equipment, and travel health coverage beyond OHIP/UHIP. Opt-out is annual and does not carry over — you must opt out every year. Extended coverage for graduating students: Summer grads Sept 1–Oct 3; Fall grads Jan 1–31. Studentcare 647-249-0419; UTGSU health@utgsu.ca / 416-978-8465. utgsu.ca/health-and-dental Group: GRAD, tri-campus. Premium not published on either provider page checked this review — this is the one plan on this page where the guide could not confirm a cost figure at all; ask UTGSU directly rather than assuming an undergrad-plan number applies.

I need help filing my taxes

Two separate free student tax clinics, run by two different student groups:

UTSU Tax Clinic Free 1-on-1 CVITP filing for UofT students with simple tax situations, in-person at Student Commons (230 College St), booked via Calendly. Honesty flag: the 2026 clinic season has ended — "Check back on this page next year for the 2027 Tax Clinic." utsu.ca/tax Group: ALL (undergrad and grad, per the page's own wording — not restricted to UTSU members). CAMPUS-GATED to in-person at St. George. Seasonal — closed outside tax season.

Woodsworth Cooperative Tax Clinic A second CVITP clinic — Woodsworth College Students' Association and Rotman Commerce — open to any UofT student, any college, for simple tax situations and modest income (generally under $35,000). Honesty flag: this year's client appointment dates weren't confirmed on the page fetched. woodsworthtax.ca Group: ALL. CAMPUS-HOSTED-BUT-PUBLIC: any UofT student, any college. Seasonal.

I need a laptop, tech gear, or free software

University of Toronto Libraries — Technology Loans Short-term, in-person borrowing of laptops, chargers, HDMI adapters, and AV equipment from multiple libraries (Robarts, Gerstein, Music, Chemistry, OISE, Engineering & CS at St. George, plus UTM and UTSC branches). Honesty flag: none of the pages checked publish specific eligibility, cost, or loan-duration terms — confirm at the desk. library.utoronto.ca — technology loans Group: ALL. CAMPUS-HOSTED-BUT-PUBLIC — open tri-campus.

UTSC Library — Experiential Learning Laptop Borrowing Narrower but far better documented: 5 laptops, first-come first-served, for students in a designated Experiential Learning course, up to two weeks (renewable). Preloaded with Microsoft 365, Teams, Zoom. Financially liable for loss/damage. utsc.library.utoronto.ca — EL laptop borrowing Group: UNDERGRAD (EL-course enrollees). CAMPUS-GATED: UTSC, designated EL course only.

Honest gap: this review checked two U of T IT pages (easi.its.utoronto.ca/shared-services/office365 and library.utoronto.ca/use/tool/microsoft-365-software) looking for a confirmed free Microsoft 365 offer for students specifically. Both pages describe Microsoft 365 access existing at U of T but neither states student eligibility, what's included for students, or how to download it, clearly enough to cite as a fact here. Not stated as existing here — genuinely unconfirmed, not overlooked. (Students borrowing the UTSC EL laptop above get Microsoft 365 preloaded on that specific device, which is confirmed — see above.)

I need free fitness or recreation

Hart House Membership (St. George) Full access to Hart House's pool, indoor track, weight room, and drop-in fitness classes, included at no extra charge for any UofT student currently enrolled in and paying incidental fees for the current term. It is currently the summer term: UofT students who are not enrolled in (or not paying incidental fees for) a summer term must buy a paid Student Summer Membership at an Information Services desk to keep using the Fitness Centre — access does not carry over automatically from the winter/fall term. Confirm at registrar or ACORN whether your incidental fee covers the current term before assuming free access. harthouse.ca/join/membership Group: ALL (incidental-fee-paying students, current term). CAMPUS-HOSTED-BUT-PUBLIC — facility is St. George only.

UTM Recreation, Athletics & Wellness Centre (RAWC) Free access for all registered UofT students with tuition and incidental fees paid in full — no extra fee. Fitness centre, group fitness, drop-in sports, squash/tennis courts, beach volleyball, aquatics, towel service, 3-hour locker rentals ($14/month). TCard required at all times (2 day passes/year without it). utm.utoronto.ca/athletics — membership Group: ALL. CAMPUS-HOSTED-BUT-PUBLIC — facility is UTM only. Closes a gap the prior draft of this page flagged as unconfirmed.

UTSC Athletics & Toronto Pan Am Sports Centre (TPASC) Free access to TPASC and the UTSC Tennis Centre, plus other campuses' athletics facilities and free drop-in programming, for current part-time or full-time UTSC students with incidental fees paid. Registered students can also pay into intramurals/climbing/learn-to-play programs. tpasc.ca/portal/u-t-scarborough/membership Group: ALL UTSC students. CAMPUS-GATED: UTSC (facility co-owned with the City of Toronto, operated by TPASC Inc.). Closes a gap the prior draft of this page flagged as unconfirmed.

I need professional or interview clothing

UTSC Arts & Science Co-op — Free Interview Attire A free interview-suiting session with a professional stylist plus a complete outfit to keep (accessories, shoes; new or gently used), via Dress for Success Toronto or New Circles GLOW, no registration fee. A second employment-suiting session is available after you're hired. utsc.utoronto.ca/artscicoop — free interview attire Group: UNDERGRAD (UTSC Arts & Science Co-op students only). CAMPUS-GATED and program-gated — narrower than a general career closet.

Honest gap: no UofT-wide (any campus, any program) career-closet/professional-attire lending was found this review, confirming the same gap flagged in the prior draft — the UTSC Co-op program above is the one exception found, and it's scoped to co-op students specifically.

I need free stuff, gear, or discounts

The U of T Free Store (Borden Building South, St. George, TCard required) is already in the base guide. Group: ALL, tri-campus in spirit but the physical store is St. George.

UTSC Free Store Free gently-used household items (dishware, cutlery, cookware, small kitchen appliances, cleaning supplies — no furniture), behind the UTSC Student Residence Centre, Elm townhouse complex. Honesty flag: published hours are dated Fall 2024 on the page fetched today — treat as indicative and confirm before visiting. Run by Regenesis UTSC. utsc.utoronto.ca/sustainability/free-store Group: ALL. CAMPUS-GATED: UTSC only, a separate location from the St. George Free Store above — residence students get move-in priority. Closes a gap the prior draft of this page flagged as unconfirmed.

I'm a student parent

Family Care Office (University of Toronto) Free support for UofT students (and staff/faculty/librarians) with dependents: help searching for GTA child care and before/after-school care, backup child-care information, counselling/wellness referrals, and pointers to the office's own financial-assistance and housing pages for student families. 416-978-0951, family.care@utoronto.ca, 215 Huron St., Suite 603. familycare.utoronto.ca Group: ALL, tri-campus. CAMPUS-HOSTED-BUT-PUBLIC — not gated to one college or union.

Honest gap, still open: the office's own page doesn't itself state a student-parent bursary dollar amount or eligibility test — this guide doesn't invent one; follow the site's own Financial Assistance section directly.

I'm a UTSC student — academic support bundle

SCSU Academic Support Centre A UTSC-specific bundle alongside the library laptop program above: a laptop rental program run jointly with the UTSC Library, free printing, an academic bursary, career-development workshops, study sessions, and appeals/academic-integrity support. Honesty flag: the provider's page confirms these exist but doesn't itemize eligibility, cost/limits, or dedicated hours — contact asc@scsu.ca to confirm before relying on any one service. scsu.ca/equity-centres Group: UNDERGRAD (and, per the food-centre row, this office also serves UTSC students broadly). CAMPUS-GATED: UTSC only.
